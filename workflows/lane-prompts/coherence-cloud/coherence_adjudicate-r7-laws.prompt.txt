WRITE THE LAWS: "Part L — the family's laws", a new section placed before Part F, normative. Keep it short: at most about two pages of rules. Every sentence must be one a builder can implement once, as a general resolver.

LAW 1 — WHAT A VALID MEETING IS: the invariants. One list that holds after EVERY commit, at every rung and on every path. Derive it from item 110 (b) and (f), J144 and the rulings that stand. It covers at least:
  - no junction between a raised way and a ground way;
  - an aqueduct crosses a wall's line only under a span, never at a support;
  - a road deck never crosses a standing wall's line; it comes down before it, to a gate on ground part 0;
  - no wall band, strip or line over a ramp, stair or foot;
  - no gate's, postern's or tower's footprint in an under-deck strip;
  - no fixed place, with its footprint, in a fixed-place zone. Define fixed place once, as a class (a place the wall's own law fixes: a river's water gate, an anchor and its tower, a castle's weld vertices, its town-side gate and vertex towers, a harbour-mouth chain), not as a list to extend;
  - a run beneath a span capped at its soffit;
  - a vault span read open, with no chamber, while anything lies across its strip (define "anything" once);
  - a held building's chamber and footprint untouched;
  - every structure's placement as held by its lock.
Define "strip", "zone", "footprint", "beyond the foot" and "the band" ONCE, as functions of the structures that stand. Several raised ways are their union. Village kinds take their section.

LAW 2 — ONE WAY TO RESOLVE ANY MEETING, whatever op made it:
  (a) The op that creates or changes the meeting reshapes its OWN structure first, locally (G1).
  (b) Failing that, it reshapes the standing structure's local shape only: gates, posterns and towers moved along that structure's line; a vault span read open. Never its placement (J144), never a fixed place, never a held building.
  (c) Failing that, the op gives way (G3):
      - by hand: refused, with the nearest lawful alternative (item 101 R2), and never an Unlock offered for a blocker that is not locked;
      - at generation or a regeneration: the newer structure is not built, or the stage takes its ladder, with what an unwalled stage then reads stated once;
      - at an epoch: it waits, except an op that cannot wait (a river already moved), which takes one named fallback;
      - in a replay: the same as an epoch.
  State which structure is "newer" for every path once: generation stage order, an epoch's op order, a creation prefix's log order, a regeneration's held-before-every-stage.
  Things that do not stand yet when a step reads them — footprints, widths, stations before S4 commits — are read from the rows they will be built by.

LAW 3 — ONE TIE-BREAK among lawful shapes: the least displacement, measured along the line it moves on, then a fixed order (spine order, then address). Deterministic.

LAW 4 — EVERY OP RE-CHECKS. Every op on every path that changes the geometry, kind, width, class, existence or held state of a wall-like structure or a raised way re-checks law 1 for the meetings it touches, in its own commit, and resolves them by laws 2–3. That includes every generation stage, every epoch op, every village step, every hand tool, warp, revert, split or merge, a replay, a skeleton import and undo. Undo is the event's inverse.
  - G2 stands: every reshape of a held thing is shown, as a fate by hand and a report entry otherwise.
  - A bend of the user's own stroke is a bend, not a fate.
  - The types must exist: EditFate's `reshaped` with what it met and the change, and the report entry.
