WHAT THIS SEAT IS
Round 7 works the raised-way × wall family alone (the owner's J144). Its laws (docs/validation/coherence/adjudication-r7.md's Part L, under J146 with J147–J151) were refined through eight probe passes, written into the design by fourteen repair seats and assembled (docs/validation/coherence/assemble-r7.md). Six family-focused checks then read the live design, one lens each:
  - docs/validation/coherence/famverify-table-r7.md (VF7-TBL-*: 0 major, 7 minor — the laws' own lens found no major);
  - famverify-generation-r7.md (VF7-GEN-*), famverify-epochs-r7.md (VF7-EPO-*), famverify-hand-r7.md (VF7-HND-*), famverify-readers-r7.md (VF7-RDR-*), famverify-tests-r7.md (VF7-TST-*).
Their results are in docs/validation/coherence/results/coherence_famverify-*-r7.json. The majors are texts, lists, fixtures and tests that do not yet conform to the laws, not defects of the laws. You decide every finding so that repair seats can write the fixes, and the six checks can run again. The loop ends when they find no major (J144, J151).

READ FIRST, WHOLE:
  1. The six famverify files.
  2. adjudication-r7.md: Part L (the laws: the design's home is now §2.7's support step (6)), Part H's head (the citation rule: every family text cites "§2.7's laws" with the law's number, the example after it), the restated veto list, and Part P pass 8's verdict.
  3. docs/PROGRESS.md: the J144 – J151 rows (J151's grading: a case where EVERY outcome keeps every law-1 invariant and owner rule, passes the runtime check and fails no named test is a minor VALID CHOICE; no lawful outcome, an outcome breaking an owner rule or a law-1 invariant, an unchecked invariant, or a check or named test failing a correct build is MAJOR).
  4. The live text each finding cites, by grep and in ranges (docs/ARCHITECTURE.md is about 9.9 MB: never read it whole).

DECIDE EVERY FINDING, VF7-* — one row each:
  - FIX: the finding stands; give the exact words the owning unit writes (the finding's required change, or your own where its words would open a new case — say why).
  - FIX, VALID CHOICE PICKED: a valid choice (J151) whose pick costs nothing and keeps the laws whole; give the words.
  - VALID CHOICE LISTED: a valid choice left to the builder; give one line naming the outcomes for fix-list.md's builders' choices.
  - MERGED: the same defect as another id; name it.
  - INVALID: the laws or a ruling already close it, or its change would break an owner rule; say why in one line.
Re-grade any finding graded against J151, naming the reason. Where two lenses report one defect, decide it once. A finding whose fix needs a change to the laws themselves is the exception: rule the narrowest change as an END line for the model unit (§2.7's support step (6)), and keep the laws from growing (J150). Never change an owner rule. Never record the program's reading of the owner's words as the owner's (VF7-TBL-01 is such a case: fix it).
