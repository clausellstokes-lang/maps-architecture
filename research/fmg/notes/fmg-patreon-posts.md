# Azgaar's Patreon (patreon.com/azgaar), 2018-11 → 2026-10: what the public posts and comment threads add to the FMG survey

Research date: 2026-10-16. Researcher scope: the Patreon corpus only. Source: the complete public post list archived locally from the Patreon posts API as `research/external/azgaar-patreon/page-1.json … page-4.json` (60 post records; page 4 empty) and `research/external/azgaar-patreon/posts/*.md` (60 files: title, body with image URLs and links kept, and the comment thread). Post URLs, publication timestamps, `comment_count` and `current_user_can_view` come from the JSON records; everything else from the post files. The repo clone `reference/fmg-src` was consulted only for `docs/updates/*` (to measure overlap, see §1) and `docs/wiki/Changelog.md` (one version date). No web research was done.

Tag legend (binding): **[STATED]** the author says it in a post body or in a comment he wrote (cited by post URL + date, or by commenter + date); **[SHOWN]** visible only in an image, known here from its URL/caption since images were not opened; **[INFERRED]** my reconstruction, with reasoning; **[NAMED-ONLY]** a feature or technique named without method; **[OBSERVED]** a fact about the archive itself (counts, flags, overlap measurements). Clean-room: no code is copied; methods are described in my own words; name lists, charge lists, palettes and data tables are given as counts and shapes only; short verbatim phrases of the author's prose are quoted only where they state a rationale, with citation.

## 1. Summary and read count against the total

### Takeaway

**60 of 60 files read in full; 59 of 59 public post bodies read; 104 of 104 archived comments read (28 of them the author's own replies). One post, "[Update] Omnibar (v1.153.0)" of 2026-09-16, is patron-only (`current_user_can_view: false`), its archived file holds no body and no teaser text, and its 32 comments are not in the archive; it is recorded as unread (§7).** Corpus size 18,540 words across the 60 files [OBSERVED, `wc -w`]; 22 files carry image URLs (none opened) [OBSERVED, grep].

The single most important finding is structural: **37 of the 59 public posts (38 counting the locked Omnibar post) are near-verbatim twins of the 38 release-note files under `reference/fmg-src/docs/updates/`**, which the releases note (`fmg-releases-and-changelog.md` §8) already read in full. A sentence-level comparison (script at `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-maps-architecture/066c3e02-47d5-566f-a043-4b593d3898a3/scratchpad/overlap.py`, normalised sentences of ≥6 words, bodies only; the four posts that share a version directory, v1.62.0 ×2 and v1.64.0 ×2, were re-checked pairwise against their own file) shows that every difference is markdown formatting, bullets, link text or a heading, with the repo copy being the *richer* one in four cases: v1.123.0 adds the texture-resolution range, v1.149.0 adds a per-version line-count table of the JS→TS migration, v1.150.0 adds a four-row transport-domain table, and v1.153.0 (Omnibar) exists in the repo in full while the Patreon post is locked [OBSERVED, overlap run 2026-10-16]. **Consequently the Patreon corpus does not change any method claim the survey already made from the update notes; what it adds is (a) 22 posts that have no repo counterpart, (b) 104 comments including 28 author replies, and (c) the publication chronology itself.** The 22 non-twin posts are: the 2018 Headnote; the 2019 contest; 14 Armoria posts (12 from 2020-12 to 2021-12, plus 2023-08-04 and 2023-09-19); the v1.5 alpha-test call; two "resources" work-in-progress posts (2021-05); the DALL-E 2 experiment (2022-07); and two Deorum posts (2022-10, 2023-08) [OBSERVED, mapping in §2 "Twin" column].

What those additions contribute, in order of value to SettlementForge:

1. **The author's granularity rationale for resources/economy** (2021-05-05 comment): a reader asked for geological layers; the reply was that it "may be a bit too complex. We have limited number of cells representing huge areas, so I'm more leaning towards Civ / Colonization type of resource generation" [STATED, Azgaar 2021-05-05, https://www.patreon.com/azgaar/posts/resources-editor-50907494]. This is the only place in any FMG source where the one-good-per-cell design is justified, and it dates the economy's design to May 2021, five years and one month before it shipped (2026-06-17) [INFERRED from dates].
2. **The author's explicit refusal to build a city generator** and reliance on Watabou (2019-06-08 reply): the Watabou handoff is a deliberate boundary, not a gap [STATED, Azgaar 2019-06-08, https://www.patreon.com/azgaar/posts/headnote-22710088].
3. **Reader critique of the economy model that the author did not answer**: consumption tied to urban population only while production is sized to the whole population; stone and salt "hardly being produced at all"; units unintuitive; production appearing in ocean cells one cell off the landmass on an upgraded map; a request that goods precede cultures in the pipeline; and a historical objection to the poll-tax default [STATED, Magnus Meyer 2026-06-18/19, Dom Oshanek 2026-06-20, Epik Berm 2026-07-24, https://www.patreon.com/azgaar/posts/update-economy-0-161328322]. None of these has an author reply in the thread [OBSERVED absence].
4. **The author's funding philosophy and its one exception**: "I'm not a huge fan of the subscription model and not going to make any special content for supporters, sorry. All content will be public" [STATED, 2018-11-14, https://www.patreon.com/azgaar/posts/headnote-22710088]; the 2026-09-16 Omnibar post is nevertheless patron-only [OBSERVED, JSON flag]. Whether this is a changed policy or a setting slip cannot be determined from the archive [INFERRED].
5. **The Armoria lineage of FMG's emblems**: 12 posts give the heraldry engine's design (tincture classes and a stated decision to model only the 7 common tinctures plus 3 stains; custom raster charges single-colour, SVG charges template-based and multi-colour; 38 shield shapes in 8 categories with a licensing caveat on 7 film-derived shapes; a Rule-of-Tincture violation fix; a deterministic SVG API that renders a given coat of arms by URL; PWA packaging at ~1 MB) [STATED, §5].
6. **Dating evidence**: the Patreon post for Journeys is dated 2026-09-01 and titled "v1.50.0" (a typo for 1.150.0), while the wiki changelog dates 1.150.0 to 2026-09-05 [OBSERVED, Changelog.md:82]; the Rivers and Lakes post credits the lake integration to a contributor's pull request a month before 1.6 shipped [STATED, 2021-03-02].

### What the corpus does not add

No new terrain, climate, hydrology or routing *method* beyond the update notes already surveyed; no parameter that is not also in `docs/updates`; no post about the Voronoi graph, the heightmap algorithm or the biome matrix (those are in the 2017–2018 blog, see `fmg-blog-history.md`). The 2020-2022 Patreon era is thin on method and thick on announcements; the author's technical prose starts at v1.99 (2024-08) and the 2026 posts are the technique-bearing ones, exactly as the brief anticipated, but all of those are twins of repo files.

### Gaps

Images not opened (22 posts carry them); anything only visible in an image is tagged [SHOWN] from its caption or the surrounding sentence. The Omnibar post body and its 32 comments are unread. The external links in posts (Reddit threads, the wiki, YouTube, the Runevision article, the two Shadertoy shaders, the Armoria API documentation, the PLOS ONE paper) were not followed.

## 3. What the Patreon posts add, by technique or design theme

Each subsection first states what the existing notes already hold (from the repo twins, the changelog or source), then what is new here. Where the only addition is a comment, that is said plainly.

### 3.1 Hydrology: lakes, evaporation, depressions, authored rivers

Already in the survey: the lake-typing rules, the Penman-style evaporation, the depression handling and the 1.65 data-model split are cited from the identical repo notes (`fmg-releases-and-changelog.md` rows 1.6/1.62/1.65; `fmg-physical-pipeline.md` §3) [OBSERVED, twin check].

Added by Patreon:
- **Origin of the lake integration.** The author credits a contributor's pull request a month earlier for making rivers see lakes at all, and describes his own work as turning that fix "into a solid system" and making lake type a *consequence* of the river system rather than a label [STATED, 2021-03-02, https://www.patreon.com/azgaar/posts/fantasy-map-v1-6-48228540]. The lake type therefore post-dates and depends on flux accounting; it was never an independent classifier [INFERRED].
- **The author's own mental model of depression filling**: a process "simulating" what "millions of years of erosion" do, by raising depressed cells incrementally; the 2021-06 refinement leaves "too depressed" cells as lakes so "the map is not getting smoothed so much" [STATED, 2021-06-08, https://www.patreon.com/azgaar/posts/map-generator-52273742]. This is the creator's stated purpose for the mechanism the survey proposes to replace with a priority flood: the *goal* (drainage to the ocean without flattening the map, deep basins become lakes) is exactly what a priority-flood with a lake rule delivers, so the replacement honours the intent [INFERRED].
- **Authored rivers are the user's liability.** For the select-cells river mode: "No matter what the heights are, custom rivers are responsibility of users, so you need to find plausible courses manually" [STATED, 2021-07-28, https://www.patreon.com/azgaar/posts/update-fantasy-54228749]. That is an explicit authored-override contract: the generator does not validate authored hydrology against the height field.
- **Frozen-lake threshold restated**: mean surface temperature < 3 °C, "now based on average surface temperature" [STATED, 2021-03-02], and sea ice at mean temperature < −2 °C "with some variations" with the explicit knock-on that "Routes tend to avoid areas with ice", changing burg and route generation [STATED, 2020-06-21, https://www.patreon.com/azgaar/posts/into-battle-v-1-38469074].
- **Navigable rivers as a reader pain point**: the one thing a commenter singled out in the economy release was that river ports and sea routes had "been a headache" [STATED, Sovrynth Systems 2026-06-17].

### 3.2 Climate: seasonal interpolation, poles, deserts

Already in the survey: 1.90's pole temperatures, tropical belt and desert rule; the physical note's description of temperature as a world-wide annual mean.

Added by Patreon:
- **A per-burg seasonal and diurnal temperature curve exists** (v1.721, 2022-02-05): a contributor fitted constants over ~1000 real cities "using ML" so that a burg's FMG mean temperature and latitude interpolate to a temperature "at any day of the year, day or night" [STATED, https://www.patreon.com/azgaar/posts/update-fantasy-62171949]. The method is [NAMED-ONLY] (no model form given); the data flow is [STATED]: inputs are the cell's annual mean and latitude only, so the curve is a *derived* layer over the same thin climate model, not a change to it [INFERRED]. The survey's "temperature is a world-wide annual mean" should be qualified to "…at cell level; a fitted seasonal/diurnal curve is derived per burg" (§6).
- **Stated climate plans never shipped** (as of 1.154 by changelog absence): the same fit for precipitation ("Is it a rainy month?") and "a real-world equivalent for every burg" exposing real climate data [STATED, 2022-02-05] [INFERRED absence: no changelog entry mentions either].
- **Design intent for defaults**: "the default settings are aimed to create climate possible on an Earth-like planet", with the acknowledged side effect that the southern hemisphere is warmer on most maps [STATED, 2023-08-06, https://www.patreon.com/azgaar/posts/update-climate-87288699]. The comparison chart is an animated image [SHOWN, caption only].
- **Orographic rationale for a template**: the Taklamakan template's central valley "is lacking precipitation" because of the ring of mountains "and usually forms big desert" [STATED, 2021-07-31, https://www.patreon.com/azgaar/posts/update-new-54338119] — a template designed to exercise the wind/precipitation model, which confirms the author treats rain shadow as a working feature rather than a side effect [INFERRED].

### 3.5 Routes: the Urquhart decision

Already in the survey: the Urquhart-over-Delaunay rationale and stored cell chains (repo twin v1.99.0).

### 3.6 Journeys: itinerary semantics

Already in the survey: journeys as a feature; the repo twin adds a transport-domain table the Patreon post lacks.

Added by Patreon: none beyond the twin; the contract itself deserves restating because the human-layers note treats journeys as "political/simulation content": (1) a leg's domain decides both the pathfinder and the admissible cells; (2) the land pathfinder prefers the existing road network and only falls back to A* over cells weighted by biome movement cost and elevation with road discount; (3) land legs across landmasses are refused rather than silently routed by sea; (4) two clocks (hours spent vs calendar days from the per-transport "travel day"); (5) explicit duration beats computed duration; (6) water legs may touch shore at endpoints only [STATED, 2026-09-01, https://www.patreon.com/azgaar/posts/update-journeys-168302939]. ## 4. The author's stated philosophy, priorities and refusals

All [STATED] unless marked; citations by date (URLs in §2).

**Funding and openness.** Supporting the project financially is welcome but not expected; no subscriber-only content, "All content will be public"; the project is "non-profit" and the money mainly reassures his family that the time is not wasted (2018-11-14). Development is part-time: "pretty slow as I have full-time job to do now" (2021-05-08). Contributions are credited by name in nearly every 2021–2026 post (lake fix 2021-03-02; burg temperature 2022-02-05; submap and Dropbox 2022-05-13/2021-10-06; poles and 3D 2023-08-06; charges and multicolour 2023-08-04; travel builder, styles, desktop, URL params, save picker, diplomacy brush 2026-08-27 → 2026-10-05). The one patron-only post (2026-09-16) contradicts the 2018 statement [OBSERVED].

**What counts as realistic enough.** Plausibility to the eye, not calibration: the new routes "much more realistic" by visual comparison (2024-08-15); the economy "not a computationally expensive realistic ticking simulation" but "a plausible web" judged by whether it "feel[s] believable" (2026-06-17); climate defaults "aimed to create climate possible on an Earth-like planet" with "a degree of randomization" (2023-08-06); resources at "Civ / Colonization" granularity because cells "represent huge areas" (2021-05-05); lake types that respond to precipitation and temperature are demonstrated by video rather than by numbers (2021-03-02).

**What is "just for fun" or view-only.** Eroded terrain and satellite texture: "experimental and mostly just for fun, having no impact on your map data" (2026-06-12); trade animation "purely a visualization and it never changes your map data" (2026-06-17); the vignette is "visual-only" (2023-11-27); the Watercolor preset's labels are "somehow jokey" (2021-07-10); the desktop app has "no exclusive features or significant performance benefits" (2026-08-27).

**What he declines or defers.** A city generator inside FMG (2019-06-08); geological layers (2021-05-05); all historical tinctures ("Only 7 basic ones are common", 2020-12-29); schedules — "there is no schedule for implementing these features" (2024-08-15), "None of this has a release date" (2026-08-27), WebGL "not yet promised" (2026-08-27); paid AI imagery in a free tool (2022-07-29/08-01); validating authored rivers ("responsibility of users", 2021-07-28); editing emblems inside FMG (Armoria is the editor, 2021-02-23; "Custom library pictures remain a Generator feature and are edited through its picker", 2026-10-05).

**Engineering priorities.** Backward compatibility is unconditional: every 2021–2026 release post says old `.map` files load and upgrade automatically, even when the upgrade is heuristic (routes from SVG paths "not perfectly precise", 2024-08-15; moved coastline vertices "does its best to read… from SVG", 2026-08-27). Refactoring is reported as a deliverable in its own right (2021-03-06, 2021-07-28, 2022-02-05, 2026-08-27, 2026-09-01) and a reader noticed (EternalDeiwos 2022-02-02). The migration rule is "the tool must keep working at every step" (2026-08-27). Data is to leave the drawing (rulers 2021, rivers 2021, routes 2024, layers 2026, styles 2026, icons 2026). Features are shipped with an editor, an overview and a regenerate entry as a matter of course (markers 2021-10-06; routes 2024-08-15; goods/markets/trade 2026-06-17; journeys 2026-09-01).

**Admitted weaknesses.** Touch devices "barely usable" (2022-05-31); names generator cannot do right-to-left or logographic scripts (2020-06-21); hierarchy display "can quickly get messy" (2022-06-06); emblem generation "may create inappropriate ones" (2021-02-23); TinyMCE "ridiculously big" (2022-02-14); real-world heightmaps break off a 2:1 aspect (2022-02-19); Armoria's API "can be pretty hard to use" (2021-03-10); unsigned desktop builds (2026-08-27).

## 5. Armoria and Deorum, condensed to what matters for SettlementForge

### 5.1 Armoria (heraldry generator, editor and API) — the emblem engine behind FMG

Timeline [STATED, URLs in §2 rows 7–13, 18–20, 31–32, 44, 47]: announced 2020-12-10 as a standalone alpha; custom charge upload 2020-12-24; custom tinctures 2020-12-29; 38 shield shapes 2021-01-05; UX and performance 2021-01-12/18; PWA 2021-01-22; integrated into FMG as v1.5 on 2021-02-23; API for deterministic coats 2021-03-10; charge batches 2021-03-17/18, 2021-11-25, 2023-08-04 (72, multicolour), 2023-09-19 (190 → 339 defaults, inscriptions, charges outside the shield); translations 2021-12-21; linked-window editing from FMG 2026-10-05.

Design facts relevant to an emblem subsystem:
- **Tincture model**: three classes — metals, colours and stains — plus a "patterns" class that merges furs, two-colour partitions and semy-of-charge; only the 7 common tinctures and 3 stains by default, with user-defined tinctures and per-tincture generation frequency; rare historical tinctures deliberately excluded [STATED, 2020-12-29]. A "Rules of Tincture common violation" fix for generated arms shows the generator enforces the metal-on-colour rule [STATED, 2021-01-12] (the source-level confirmation is in `fmg-human-layers.md` §3.11).
- **Charges**: raster uploads are single-colour silhouettes (transparency required); SVG uploads follow a template and receive the chosen tincture; later up to three tinctures per charge [STATED, 2020-12-24; 2023-08-04]. Charge art is sourced from a heraldry wiki under CC BY-NC-SA [STATED, 2021-03-17; 2021-11-25] — a licence constraint SettlementForge must respect if it reuses the charge set (the host audit notes the fork already prunes to CC0 charges).
- **Shield shapes**: 38 in 8 categories including 5 generic fantasy and 7 film-derived shapes the author advises against commercial use [STATED, 2021-01-05]; shape selected by culture in FMG [STATED, 2021-02-23].
- **Composition**: ordinaries may be layered over charges; strokes customisable; inescutcheon follows shield shape; charge size adapts to shape; up to 4 charges on quartered-type divisions; inscriptions and extra-shield elements (crowns, supporters) since 1.1 [STATED, 2021-01-12/18; 2023-09-19].
- **Identity and API**: a coat of arms is encoded in a shareable link (2021-01-18) and the HTTP API renders either a random coat or a specific GUI-made coat as SVG at a requested size by URL path [STATED, 2021-03-10, https://www.patreon.com/azgaar/posts/armoria-api-coat-48595182]; the API is documented in its own repository [NAMED-ONLY]. For SettlementForge the transferable pattern is "emblem = small declarative record; rendering = stateless function of record + size", not the heraldry itself [INFERRED].
- **FMG coupling**: FMG generates and regenerates but does not edit; editing goes to Armoria and back (SVG download/upload until 2026, linked window with undo/redo and tincture edits since 1.154); any image may replace an emblem; new charges are mirrored into FMG's generator [STATED, 2021-02-23; 2026-10-05]. In 1.154 library icons can be charges or whole emblems [STATED, 2026-10-05].
- **Packaging**: PWA, ~1 MB code plus charges, offline after first load [STATED, 2021-01-22]; translation via Crowdin, >600 strings [STATED, 2021-12-21].

### 5.2 Deorum (character/portrait generator) — out of scope

Deorum is a gallery of AI-multiplied hand-drawn portraits (120 originals → >10,000 variations, >1600 characters, 32 races) with AI-refined biographies, metered GPT-4 generation and a CC BY-NC 4.0 licence [STATED, 2022-10-23; 2023-08-13]. Character generation is outside SettlementForge's world-regime scope; the only transferable fact is the author's cost stance on hosted AI (§3.14).

## 6. Implications for a native SettlementForge world regime

Verdicts are the researcher's [INFERRED], grounded in the cited statements. They do not overturn any survey verdict; they sharpen six of them and add three.

| Theme | Verdict | Reasoning from the Patreon evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Lake typing from flux vs evaporation; deep depressions become lakes | KEEP (contract) | The author's stated purpose of depression handling is drainage without flattening and deep basins as lakes (2021-06-08); a priority-flood with a lake rule meets the purpose. The frozen (< 3 °C) and sea-ice (< −2 °C) thresholds are explicit boundary values to carry into the climate contract. |
| Authored rivers unvalidated against heights ("responsibility of users") | REPLACE | FMG's authored-override contract is explicit and deliberately unchecked (2021-07-28). PLAT's authored state should be validated against the canonical height field at authoring time with a receipt, not silently accepted. |
| Per-burg seasonal/diurnal temperature curve from mean + latitude (fitted constants) | GENERALIZE | A derived per-cell seasonal curve is cheap and the author considered it useful for storytelling (2022-02-05); make it part of the climate output contract (mean, amplitude, phase) rather than a burg-only UI graph, and document the fit instead of embedding opaque constants. |
| Earth-like defaults with explicit pole temperatures | KEEP (as boundary inputs) | The author moved from an implicit gradient to two explicit pole inputs because users asked (2023-08-06); boundary conditions as inputs is the charter the survey already recommends. |
| Real-world heightmap import tied to a 2:1 aspect | REPLACE | Admitted limitation (2022-02-19); PLAT imports should carry explicit georeferencing and resample into the integer-centimetre frame. |
| Coastline fractalisation | KEEP as presentation, REJECT as canonical geometry | View-only displacement of a canonical outline; the author validates it on a synthetic diamond, i.e. it is an operator over any polygon (2026-04-26). Parameters (depth, √edge amplitude, decay, zones) are a good default vocabulary for a renderer detail layer. |
| Urquhart route network | KEEP | The "chain vs network" diagnosis is a requirement, and the fix is chosen for simplicity and cost (2024-08-15); nothing in the comments contradicts it. |
| Journeys: domain-gated pathfinding, two clocks, explicit-duration precedence, cross-landmass refusal | GENERALIZE | The semantics are sound and host-neutral; the host should own itineraries but reuse these rules as the engine's path-query contract (domain → admissible cells → cost surface). |
| Economy granularity: one bonus good per cell, "Civ / Colonization" style | KEEP (placement only) | The author's own rationale (2021-05-05) is cell size; SettlementForge's engine should provide bonus-resource placement over biome/elevation/temperature/shore/river predicates and nothing more. |
| Economy market/production/trade/taxes | REJECT (host owns) | Single cycle by design (2026-06-17); readers report urban-only consumption, under-produced staples and unintuitive units, none answered. The host's own economy should not inherit this. |
| Military, battles | REJECT (host owns) | Cell-based generation aggregated to regiments "for usability" (2020-04-01) is a UI compromise; the author documented it only because it confused users. |
| Burg groups by rules (population percentile, location, features) with per-group preview generator | GENERALIZE | A classification-rules layer the host can define; the per-group generator selection is the Watabou handoff made explicit (2026-01-08). |
| Watabou as the city layer | KEEP (boundary) | A deliberate seven-year decision (2019-06-08 → 2026-01-08); SettlementForge's town map is already its own and should keep the same boundary with the realm engine. |
| Emblems | GENERALIZE (optional) | Keep "declarative record + stateless render at size" (the Armoria API pattern, 2021-03-10) and the kinship idea; respect CC BY-NC-SA charge art; heraldry itself is optional content. |
| Icon library reference-not-copy | KEEP | Already in the survey; the reader evidence that people hand-edited `.map` files to shrink them (2026-10-06) strengthens the case for an id-keyed format. |
| 3D erosion / satellite texture | REJECT (world regime); allowed as viewer | "Just for fun", view-only, GPU-dependent with fallback (2026-06-12). |
| Data out of the drawing; refactor as deliverable; "keep working at every step" | KEEP (discipline) | The retreat began with rulers and rivers in 2021 (3.13), not 2024; the author's own migration rule is the incremental, shim-based path the survey recommends. |
| Hosted-AI cost stance; keys stay with the user | KEEP | The 2022 → 2026 path (refuse paid imagery, ship doc-only bot, then user-key map access) is a policy SettlementForge can adopt directly. |
| Large third-party editor (TinyMCE) | REJECT in the host unless re-justified | The author doubted the trade-off at adoption (2022-02-14); the host inherited it with the fork. |
