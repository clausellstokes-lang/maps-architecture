## How each clause is marked

* **needs-M-1**: a number, threshold or file the clause's spec or verdict must state is an M-1 deliverable (named).
* **M0-dependent**: the clause extends, re-runs or reads the outcome of an M0 item that had no landed record when read, or an M0 bar's verdict (named).
* **specifiable-now**: neither; the design alone fixes it, and any M0 item it reads already had a landed record.
* Precedence: needs-M-1, then M0-dependent, then specifiable-now. Secondary dependencies stay listed.
* The pipeline (M0-x-02, M0-x-04) is carried by clause 4, and the tier runner and timing script (M0-f-02a/b, M0-t-00) by clause 20. They are not repeated on every stage or bar clause.
* "Landed" means a `plat/docs/build/landed/<id>.json` record existed in the working copy on 2026-10-05, while run wf_8f7c7489-304 was in flight. It is not a checker verdict, so re-read it before relying on it.

*Revised 2026-10-05 on an independent check: clauses 1–123 keep their numbers; clauses 124 onward were added by the revision and sit in their sections out of numeric order.*

**Totals:** M0-dependent 52, specifiable-now 65, needs-M-1 14; 131 clauses.
