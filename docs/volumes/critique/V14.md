### 1.1 Blockers

**C14-01 — blocker — buildability / owner fidelity (items 132 (f), 133 (b), 134 (a), 135 (a)). An above-ground site
inside a settlement's frame but outside the enclosure has no mechanism, and a `free` site inside a frame makes two
documents own one ground.** The admissibility table (l. 681–690) gives `hinterlandNear` the attachment `attached` (on
a hinterland face) or `free`, and `hinterlandFar` `free` or `attached`, for kinds whose policy lays ground, an
enclosure and buildings — `camp`, `watchpost`, `graveyard`, `mine`, `shrine`, `ruin`, `fort`, `tower`, `manor`,
`temple`. But "An attached site document has no `ground` part: its levels are `site` parts" (l. 986–987) and G5 (e)
lays the enclosure, plots, buildings and plans "on the site document's `ground` part" (l. 1394), so an attached
watchpost, camp or manor in the hinterland lays nothing above ground; a `tower` or `manor` has no levels but
`[builtLevel]` as V03 basements of a building that is never placed. The `surface` attachment is admitted on
`urbanSurface` only. And a `free` site inside a frame gets its own frame of ≥ 120 m with S1's terrain and S12a's fields
(G3 (c), l. 1155–1163) over ground the settlement's document already owns — two documents, two field patterns, two
terrains at one world address, which item 135 (a)'s "one geometry refined three times" forbids; nothing adopts it (G8
(e) runs only when a settlement is placed over a site, and the hand `adoptSite` is for a site "placed after the
settlement"). The B-SITE fixture ("a tower and a manor in the hinterland") and T-V14-13 ("the manor's on its hinterland
face") therefore describe sites the volume cannot make.
*Required change:* (1) In V14 §2.5's table, inside a settlement's frame (`hinterlandNear`, `hinterlandFar`) admit
`attached` for kinds whose policy has `ground: 'none'` and no `buildings` (cave, hideout, crypt, cellarComplex,
hermitage's cell excepted) and a new attachment **`inFrame`** for every kind with a ground policy; never `free`
inside a frame. (2) Define `inFrame` in V14 §3.9 as **adoption at the drop**: the card lives in the host document; its
envelope becomes a `Reservation` of kind `worldSite` on the clicked hinterland faces claimed by §7.3's reservation
operator (the row V14 §4.5 already uses); G5 (e) runs on the host's ground part 0 inside the reservation — S7's trace,
S10/S11 on the reservation's faces with the card as the town root, V03's plans; its levels are `site` parts of the
host document by `nextSitePart`; its hinterland unit is not run (the host's S12a fields stand, re-derived locally
round the reservation by §7.3's S12 row). (3) The context record `attachment` gains `inFrame`; §1b.1, §2.2
(`SiteAttachment`), §2.9, §4.7 (the locality row: the reservation's faces and the blocks they touch) and §4.8 gain the
rows; T-V14-9 (bar 6) and T-V14-13 name an `inFrame` watchpost and manor; B-SITE's "a tower and a manor in the
hinterland" become `inFrame`. (4) `hinterlandFar`'s `free` is admitted only where the click lies outside every
settlement frame (then it is `country`), so strike it. Record J-V14.41 (adoption at the drop over a second document in
the frame, because one ground has one owner).
