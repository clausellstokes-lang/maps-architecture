### 1.1 Blockers

**C4-01 — blocker — parity / coherence. Registering the forecast into S7b breaks T14-1 and the parity presets, while the
volume claims both green.** BCR-19 wires U-FORECAST into S7b (6) and (7) (l. 176, 1389–1396). Before M6 those steps are
owner-staged and allocate nobody (§4.5 S7b (6)–(7) "owner-staged"). After M6, every default CITY whose trunk serves
about 14 ha or more (the historical main's law reaches `widthCm` ≥ 110 and `headroomCm` ≥ 160 at about 13.8 ha, II.7.2) gets
an underground allocation of `undergroundShare` (0.04) of its poorest band. Where a vault, catacomb, quarry or buried
source is forecast, it also gets an undercity allocation. Those households leave the ground's districts, so the ground's
plots and uses change. T14-1's clause "with the layer hidden a CITY's render matches, pixel for pixel, the render the same
seed gave before the staged stages registered" therefore fails. The volume nevertheless lists T14-1 among U1's exits
(l. 1162) and calls invisibility its parity metric (l. 1101–1104, J-V4.47). The BCR preamble "None changes a ground
output" (l. 1237–1238) is false for BCR-19. The base never sets `undergroundShare` or the `underground: 'preferred'` shares
to 0 in the parity presets: only the system chances and the undercity's `maxNeighbourhoods` are zeroed. So under `MFCG`
and `FTG` sewer-front dwellings still take households off the ground, and T-V4-35 (l. 1143) does not check for them.
*Required change:* (1) add BCR-22 to `### 13.2 …`, T14-1: "…pixel for pixel… under the parity presets `MFCG` and `FTG`;
under the default presets the ground differs only by the households and `underground: 'preferred'` uses S7b (6) and (7)
send below, a one-time shift of the M6 output version recorded in `plat/goldens/` (§13.3)". (2) Add BCR-23 to II.7.4's
`UndergroundRule` comment and §7.6's parity preset rows: `undergroundShare` 0 at every rung, the `underground: 'preferred'`
table share 0 and `crowdingShare` 0 under `MFCG` and `FTG`. (3) Extend T-V4-35 with "no underground plot, no undercity
allocation, and the ground's model digest equal to the pre-M6 digest on 200 seeds per rung". (4) Rewrite the section 10
preamble to name BCR-19 as the one ground-output shift. (5) Record the shift in V4 §8's U1 exit as a declared shift
record, not as "T14-1 green".

**C4-02 — blocker — buildability / coherence. The tail writes the ground, contradicting J-V4.2, T-V4-3 and T12-1.** The
volume states that "No tail stage writes the ground arena" and that the ground's digests are final before the tail
(l. 462–463, J-V4.2). Its own ladders then write ground records from the tail:
* U-FRONT F0: "S10b's reconcile re-places the allocation on the ground" (l. 630–631).
* U-CITY C1: "the allocation re-placed on the ground (F-VOID)" (l. 749).
* U-LINK L1: a dropped neighbourhood's "allocation re-placed" (l. 785).
* The failure table's U-CITY row: "households re-placed" (l. 889).
* Item 63's `underground` → `front` → `hidden` chain for a clandestine institution the tail cannot seat. It needs a
  ground front plot (§4.7 *Concealed institutions* (2)).
* L5: U-DIG's basement piece forces §2.6's basement decision on a ground building (l. 796–799; II.7.6 step 1).

A re-placement is a use and building write on ground plots after the first paint. It changes the ground digest that
J-V4.2 declares final and the "0 writes on the ground `PlatKey`" of T12-1 and T-V4-3. An implementer cannot satisfy
both. *Required change:* state one mechanism and amend the invariants to it. The chosen mechanism:
(1) S7b (6) and (7) cap each allocation at `forecastSafety` × the forecast capacity (new `UnderLayoutRule` field, default
0.85, own, calibratable), so over-forecasts are rare.
(2) A tail surplus is absorbed below first. Surplus households raise the occupancy of the placed underground cells up to
`crowdCap` households per cell (default 2, own). An undercity neighbourhood that drops sends its households to the
sewer-front pseudo-district's free capacity, then to the other neighbourhoods.
(3) Only the remainder, and every institution the tail cannot seat, goes to one declared tail stage, **T8 reconcile**. T8
is the only tail stage that writes the ground. It runs S10b's reconcile on vacant ground plots and item 63's chain for
concealed institutions, uses only, no geometry. The ground's model digest is finalised after T8.
(4) L5's basement moves to the main path. When S10b seats a subterranean dossier institution on a surface plot, S11
forces its building's basement by §2.6's requested-mode ladder, a BCR to §2.6 and §4.7 S11. S10b's placement takes a
hard row "the plot's terrain gate admits a basement" for that institution.
(5) Amend J-V4.2, §3.1's sentence, BCR-5's tail bullet ("they write no ground arena but T8's reconcile uses"), T-V4-3
and T12-1's clause to count T8's writes. Add a bar: T8 writes on ≤ 2 % of CITY seeds at the default presets. Report
every T8 write in `requestedVsBuilt`.

a test asserts it.
(3) Bind the record. The first neighbourhood takes the assignment: its `District.identity.dossier` is the record's
binding key and its name is the record's name (a dossier fact, protected, never the naming slice). A `Black market
bazaar` record binds to the core's black-market use.
(4) Under `forbid`, S7b (7) makes no allocation and U-CITY does not run.
(5) Add bar T-V4-37: B-DOSSIER fixtures with `require` at TOWN on a site with no void yield exactly ≥ 1 neighbourhood on

### 8.2 The two uncarried hand-offs (C4-64, C4-65)

**C4-64 — major — buildability / coherence (revise-V3's hand-off, `docs/lane-events.log` 22:48). The crypt join reads a room
that no longer decides the crypt, and it cites a fold that no longer exists.** V4 §3.5.7 (c) puts the join's head "in the
crypt room (V03's `AccessHostRow` catacomb → crypt)". It then rules: "A church without a crypt room (V03's floor-vault fold
on wet ground) draws nothing" (l. 1336–1337). As revised, V03 makes the crypt a model fact, `Basement.crypt`, derived with
the basement (BCR-V3-20), and removes the floor-vault fold (J-V3.44). V04 has 0 hits for `Basement.crypt`. So an
implementer holding V04 would test for a crypt the wrong way. Four more gaps come from the same hand-off:
- V04 §3.5.1 (11) and §2.3a bind a dossier crypt record to a plan room. V03 §3.7.4 binds it to the church building, which
  makes that building's crypt `always`.
- A church-class building must never host a catacomb access of source `system` (V03 §3.7.2 (6), T-V3-23 (d)). V04 does
  not say so.
- V04 never writes `Entrance.faces` `tunnel` (BCR-V3-21); it has 0 hits for `Entrance`.
- V03 §3.12 (6) reads "volume 4's chamber column points" as fixed rings, but V04 draws its columns "not modelled" (l. 1124).

*Required change:*
1. **The join.** §3.5.7 (c)'s test reads `Basement.crypt` on the building (BCR-V3-20). The sentence at l. 1336–1337
   becomes "A building without `Basement.crypt` draws nothing." The head stays in the crypt, by V03's `AccessHostRow`:
   catacomb with cause `crypt` → crypt.
2. **The dossier row.** §3.5.1 (11) and §2.3a's crypt row bind a dossier crypt record to its church building (V03 §3.7.4),
   never to a plan room.
3. **Where an entrance may stand.** The burial-face entrance (§3.5.1) puts its head in a cellar, in the chapel's nave or in
   a store. A building whose class crypt column is `always` or `basement` never hosts a catacomb access of source
   `system`. T-V4-50 gains that clause.
4. **Entrances.** U-FRONT F4 and F6 and U-CITY C4 write `Entrance.faces: ['tunnel']` on every underground building they
   seat (BCR-V3-21). V03 §3.12 (4) and the VTT portals read it.
5. **Columns.** §3.5.0a (5) models its columns. `Chamber.columns: PointCm[]` is derived, never persisted, for `hall` and
   `temple` chambers. The points lie on the chamber rectangle at `columnPitchM`, less the columns the decay removed, by
   the same uniform that keeps the drawn columns. `@plat/under` exports it as `ruinColumns(chamberId)`. V03 §3.12 (6)
   reads it as fixed rings, and the ruin program's column dots read it too. T-V4-44, the ruin structure bar, gains a clause:
   the modelled points equal the drawn dots. Its lifecycle: derived with the chamber, with no persistence and no migration.

**C4-65 — major — buildability / coherence (revise-V7's hand-off, `docs/lane-events.log` 22:42; V07 l. 179–182). A
village's inherited underground has no rule, and one union value is missing.**

  (l. 458) lacks that value. The build would fail on the type. V07's fallback is a report line.
- V07 §2.4's `parish_church` is not among V04's counted church classes (l. 1164). A town grown from a village (V06)
  therefore under-counts its churches.
- V04 has no maintenance mode for a system that an earlier, larger rung left under a village rung.
- At a village rung, the ruin centre falls to "the town root's anchor" (l. 1098). V07 reads the one village district's
  centroid when no marker or node locates the centre.

*Required change:*
1. **The union.** §2.2 l. 458 gains `'rung'`. §3.5.1 (6): below TOWN an existing complex never grows. `blockedBy` gains
   `'rung'`, the state is `stagnant`, and `fill` rises by (7)'s rule. T-V4-51 gains the decline case.
2. **The count.** §3.5.1 (3): `churches` counts the classes `cathedral`, `great_temple`, `church` and `parish_church`, plus
   each monastery's church.
3. **The maintenance mode.** §3.1 and §3.10: at a village rung, a catacomb, quarry, vaults or buried system that an
   earlier rung left is re-derived from its kept galleries by `corridorUnion`. It never grows, and U-FADE ages it. No new
   system of those kinds is laid, because §3.5.0's gate stands.
4. **The ruin centre.** §3.5.0a (1), l. 1098: at a village rung with no stage-1 district, no marker and no magical node,
   the centre is the centroid of the village district (V07's one district). Failing that, it is the town root's anchor.
5. **Wells.** l. 1368 already makes a well pin an access, at every rung, where its shaft passes through a ruin or cavern
   chamber. The volume only states that V07's village well pins are the same pin record (V07 §3.16). No new rule is

6. **Dig rows.** §2.3's `DigRule` defaults line adds "V07 fills `classRow` for its village classes (V07 §3.16 (3))".
