### 1.1 Blockers

**C13-01 — blocker — owner fidelity (item 140 M6; the brief's bar 3), determinism. A placement changes the frame's
model digest.** T-V13-2 (l. 1975) and J-V13.5 claim that moving a placement "changes no arena digest of the frame" and
§2.7 (l. 792–796) that "a frame document's model digest reads neither `worldRef` nor `Recipe.charter`". But P4 writes
`Terrain.datumCm` in the frame — §3.0 (l. 873–876) "in the frame document, exactly: `Document.worldRef`,
`Recipe.charter`, `Terrain.datumCm` (through its accessor), the orientation-dependent terms"; §2.6's datum row (l. 768)
"P4 (placed: the world DEM at the origin) … `Terrain.datumCm` (unchanged storage)"; and BCR-V13-2's new text (l. 2178)
sets `datumCm` "by the placement (P4 …) on a placed one" and makes `Terrain.wind` "on a placed frame, the charter's wind
in frame terms" (l. 2179). `Terrain` is a model record hashed in the model digest (§6.2; the base's `Terrain` is the
ground arena's own entity, §2.3). So every `setPlacement`, `mirrorFrame` and `unplaceFrame` changes the frame's model
digest, bar 3 fails, and a replay of the frame from its own log cannot reproduce its digest because the world's
placement is not in that log (C13-04 and C13-40 compound it). The daylight term of §4.5 reads the sun (§8.4's
`sunPath`), so BCR-V13-13's "the model-side sampler reads the same accessor" moves field pass 1's model output with the
placement too. *Required change:* (1) In §2.2's frame side and BCR-V13-2: `Terrain.datumCm` and `Terrain.wind` stay
exactly what S1 wrote; neither is written by a placement. (2) `datumOf(doc)` returns `Terrain.datumCm` on a standalone
frame and, on a placed frame, the world's DEM at the placement's origin **read through the world document at call time**
(a derived reading, never stored in the frame); likewise `windOf(doc)` and the sun through `sunAt`. (3) §3.0's list of
what P1–P5 write in the frame shrinks to `Document.worldRef` (C13-40 makes it an event) — nothing else. (4) The model-side
sun stays the recipe's latitude (`Intent.latitudeDeg`, DR29) until a `worldWins` re-derives the frame; a difference between
the recipe's latitude and the placement's is a `PlacementAnomaly` kind `sunDiffers` on the card. (5) T-V13-2 asserts the
frame's **model digest** byte-equal before and after every placement edit, and that only the records digest (the charter
as recorded input) differs.

cells) × 15 ns, re-cost the three rungs from the shipped templates' op lists, and add SP-W6 (W1 over the six templates at
9.2 M cells) to §8.4 with the pre-decided fallback (the continent template's noise capped at 4 octaves, the finer octaves
added only inside a placed frame's margin by S1).
