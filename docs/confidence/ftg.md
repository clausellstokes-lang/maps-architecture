## §1 Evidence base for FTG — what kind of evidence exists, and how far each kind reaches

FTG is the odd one out of the four references: it is **server-generated, proprietary, has no open-source ancestor, emits no console stage trace, and has no call-count census** (there is nothing to profile on the client but scene building). What it has instead — and MFCG does not — is a **complete client-visible parameter schema**, a **creator-written user guide that describes the algorithms in prose**, four years of **monthly devlogs that narrate old algorithm → failure → new algorithm**, and **two decoded settlement payloads** that give every road edge, building ring, entrance, district ring, water outline and tower position with centimetre precision.

### §1.1 Binding corrections applied in this assessment (from the critic files and post-critic addenda; they supersede older text in the findings)

* **Coordinates are zig-zag `sint32` centred on (0,0)**, ±2500 m for a CITY, 1 unit = 1 cm, `+x` east, `+y` north (`_critic-ftg.md` F1; ftg-payload-visual Critic-2 addendum A). Every length in ftg-payload-visual §5 is halved and every area quartered: buildings ≈ 10 × 10 m (median 103 m²), roads 192 km, block lots median 441 m², water step 50 m, tower spacing median 39 m, forests 1.82 / 1.59 km², fields ≈ 2.5 ha (F2, §4). The "self-intersecting ring", "duplicate vertices" and "building outside the extent" hazards are retracted (F4, F5); 0.01 m edges, 2.5 m² footprints, 109 entrance-less buildings and the duplicate `key: 9` stand.
* **`roadLength` law (F3, SETTLED by the roadlength100 capture):** the form's "(m)" is real metres; main roads persist at L + 0.5 m; district-stage normal streets at (L + 0.5)/2; suburb streets at exactly L; suburb lot outlines at 14.70 m; the halves are independently jittered placed segments, not midpoint splits. Edge type 0 = district and suburb streets, 1 = main roads, 4 = suburb lot outlines, 3 = map border, 12 = piers, 14 = water outline, 6 = rural lanes.
* **Server generation takes seconds (≈ 3 s PUT, full layout ≤ 7 s), not ≈ 300 s** — the 300 s in ftg-payload-visual §1/§9, `_critic-ftg.md` F20/F27, ftg-docs-params §5.7 and the posts files was the recorder's wait cap (roadlength100 README, side finding; ftg-payload-visual Critic-2 part 2).
* **Pipeline order:** phase 1 = buildings and people (forced buildings → dependency loop → factions/friends/rivals), phase 2 = map (water → staged road network and districts → suburb stage → outer settlement with farms/forests/features). ftg-posts-c §5's "layout first, people eighth" and ftg-payload-visual §6's "landscape incl. forests first, populate last" are wrong (`_critic.md` C12, `_critic-ftg.md` F14).
* **Walls follow lot/district outlines, not a convex hull** (F15): all 17 Galeiaward towers are vertices of the waterfront district ring; `wallPlacement` = Around all lots / Around stage lots; the 2023 "percentage of buildings within wall" rows are the ancestors of per-stage `proportionOfBuildings` + `placeWallAfter`.
* **Parameter semantics** (F6–F8): `minDistrictArea` is a **merge floor** in m² (10 ha / 7 ha), not a split trigger; `verticalBranchSegments`/`horizontalBranchSegments` = segments laid **before** proposing a left/right branch; `spokeStaySpokeProbability` = P(a branch off a main road is itself a main road).
* **Blocks (F13):** 901 serialised block lots hold 1.52 buildings each, 22.9 % empty, 38 % coverage; **district-stage lots are not serialised** (only 53 % of buildings lie inside any lot polygon) — the core's block structure must be recovered as road-graph faces.
* **Subdivision is three levels and two dates** (F17): (i) district lot → block lots by inserted roads until below the target area; (ii) block lot → building plots by recursive random perpendicular cuts (2025 centroid rule, 2026 shrink-then-split); (iii) the 2023 image-based strip/burgage readings describe level (ii) before either rewrite.
* Saturated coloured squares are **both** market stalls and shanty-town tents (F16); `Delegating` = babies with a caregiver (F18); bridge distance is metres, default now 40 (F19); farmland has four modes (F21); person types are five (F23); GEMS moved from raw to produced (F24); calendar field 22.2 is the current hour, not a 9-day week (F12); GeoJSON still omits districts (F26).
* **Profiler label caveat** (`captures/README-labelfix-2026-09-25.md`) does not touch FTG's numbers: FTG's captures carry no class-level generation call tables (server-side); the client profile rows are chunk-attributed symbols only.

## §2 Capability matrix

Scale for the four confidences: probability in [0, 1] that an expert team, working clean-room from this evidence base only, achieves the stated goal for that capability. `functional` = same feature, same option surface, statistically similar structure; `visual` = a viewer places the output in the same quality/style class; `algorithm identity` = our derivation matches THEIR algorithm, not merely its output ("deriving their code"); `seed-exact` = same seed → same map, which needs their exact RNG, draw order and constants. Confidences are deliberately non-uniform; each carries a one-line justification. Tags: [STATED] creator text, guide, request/preset JSON or the module's own code; [SHOWN] recorded, measured or visible; [INFERRED] reconstruction; [NAMED-ONLY] feature named, no method.

**Seed-exact convention for FTG.** The reference has **no seed model** (`seed: null` in both captured requests; the creator: settlements are stored objects, not functions of a seed, ftg-posts-a §2.22/§5; forests and decoration areas are filled at render time, ftg-docs-params §2.9), no open-source ancestor, and generation is server-side. Same seed → same map is therefore **0.00 for every generative row** — there is not even a reference target to match. Rows that are pure data, formats or deterministic transforms carry "—" (not meaningful); in the structured summary those rows are given `conf_seed_exact` equal to their `conf_functional`, as the sibling MFCG lane did, and the **overall** seed-exact figure is computed over generative rows only.

**A2 — Two-phase pipeline, staging, failure policy.**
Evidence: [STATED, verified ×3] "two phases: the buildings and people, and then drawing the map"; phase 1 = forced buildings → dependency loop → factions/friends/rivalries; phase 2 = water → road network and districts "in configured stages" → suburb stage → outer settlement (`createSettlement__algorithm.md` via ftg-docs-params §0.2; `_critic.md` C12; `_critic-ftg.md` F14); [STATED] the creator's invariant "generates the details before the layout … I don't see this ever changing" (127128581; ftg-posts-b §T-19/§4.1); [STATED] between stages "unconnected normal roads are pruned, and main roads are trimmed" (ftg-docs-params §2.3); [STATED] V1's failure policy — bounded retry then "the algorithm gives up", and its merge logic (ftg-posts-b §T-19) — replaced by V2's landmark-first order so the hard placement is trivially satisfiable (§T-22); [STATED] one acknowledged unfixed failure (rivers bending back into the ocean, ftg-docs-params §4.4) and "more segments will create larger lots — but this isn't guaranteed"; [SHOWN] no stage trace on the console (ftg-payload-visual §1). **One paragraph.** FTG can be reproduced *functionally* with high confidence because its input schema is completely known (two captured requests, the preset catalogue, the form's own option lists) and its author documents mechanisms in prose — a proposal-queue road grower over a planar graph with incremental face detection, staged growth by building quotas, district lots subdivided by inserted roads to an area target, block lots cut into plots by a stated reflex-pre-cut / centroid-edge / shrink-then-split construction, a suburb stage with invisible lot edges and a stated priority formula, walls traced on lot outlines, coasts in two arms with overall-then-detail shaping, and a content phase driven by a stated virtual-population dependency loop — while two decoded payloads pin the output statistics (15° turn cap, 90° junctions, half-step normal streets, 10 × 10 m buildings, entrances on the frontage, no-Poisson trees, 39 m tower spacing, 50 m water sampling) tightly enough to calibrate a re-implementation. *Deriving their code* is a different matter: at the mechanism level our derivation would match theirs about half the time overall and better than that for the stages they narrated, but every numeric threshold (road separation, snap radius, split-line placement, plot depth, shrink distance, area budgets), the district-type scoring, the curve constructions for coasts and rivers, the farmland partition and the castle/harbour fitting are unstated and would be our own inventions that merely reproduce the measured distributions. Seed-exact reproduction is not a meaningful target: FTG itself cannot regenerate a settlement from a seed, generation is server-side and proprietary, and there is no ancestor source — so 0.00, honestly. For the owner's fusion base, the FTG side contributes exactly the parts that are best evidenced (the staged, quota-driven, constraint-checked road/lot pipeline in real metres; the content-first manifest that gives every lot a context; the explicit lot → plot → building hierarchy) and the parts that must be invented are the ones the tiebreaker ruling says to design from historical constraint anyway. Effort: ≈ 45 expert person-weeks for the map-generation subset (roads/lots/plots/buildings 12, water/coast/rivers 6, suburbs/walls/castle/harbour 8, outer landscape/forests/decorations 6, manifest driver and context vector 5, renderer/labels/export 8), ≈ 110 for the whole product including the people/economy/time simulation (20), the editor and regeneration tools (15), integrations (5), original content authoring for 200+ building types, 80 nodes, professions, items, names and templates (15), and calibration/testing (10) — the creator's own overhaul was 800 files / 70 000 lines and he reports three discarded road algorithms, which the stated evidence lets a re-implementer skip.

## §4 The ten riskiest unknowns, ranked (impact on the base first)

1. **District-type selection and placement scoring (C6).** "Based on criteria like the remaining unplaced buildings, and what the lot is next to" is all that exists; the residential order rule and the shanty-town staging are the only stated parts. This decides the social geography of every FTG-style city; a wrong guess produces plausible maps with a different class/industry layout. Also unknown: how sub-areas (industrial + park inside waterfront) are carved, how singletons (castle, university, cathedral, market) are guaranteed, and the `Number of districts` mapping.
2. **Road legality thresholds, jitter law and stage termination (C5).** Minimum separation, parallelism rejection, snap radius, what "optimising … making it longer" reaches for, the distribution of the ≤ 15° bend, vertical-vs-horizontal branch semantics, and how a stage's target lot area is derived from its building quota. The measured cap and lengths constrain but do not identify these.
3. **District-lot division to area (C7).** Where the dividing road goes and how it connects; the plot band depth and frontage; the Gaps inset. District-stage lots are not serialised, so the block structure inside rings must be recovered as road-graph faces before any of this can be measured.
4. **Plot-subdivision constants (C8).** "Too large for a building" threshold, the 2026 shrink distance *d*, minimum width and sharp-angle filters, retry count, the fate of the inner core, reflex-corner ordering. The construction is known; the constants are not — and they set the terrace grain.
5. **Wall tracing (C11).** Which lot edges the `Around all lots` wall follows (an offset? the exact outline?), the tower law (median 39 m; all towers on ring vertices), gate placement (none drawn, but names cite gates), the river split, the unexplained July-2025 tweak.
6. **Coast and river curve construction (C1, C2).** Control-point counts, the overall-shape spline, the erosion operator behind Bays/Rough/Jagged, the ease-in envelope, the bay/headland classifier, the river path generator and branch start rule, bridge placement under the 40 m minimum, the "significant portion" ocean threshold.
7. **Farmland partition and outside placement (C14).** How outside lots are split to a count (roads as seams is inferred), the count estimate, the "similar features next to each other" clustering, crop assignment, hedgerow placement, farmstead lot sizing and driveway rule.
8. **Suburb geometry (C10).** Derivation of the perimeter polyline, the spoke-distance multiplier's base, the 14.7 m lot-depth rule, offshoot heading and termination, main-road lot construction, the perimeter expand/round distances (C17).
9. **Castle, harbour and decoration fitting (C12, C13, C16).** Castle wall randomiser and fit; how a waterfront district picks its coast stretch and orients the template; mole paths and pier counts; pond/copse/stone-circle/ruin generators and trail routing; the singleton plot-decoration rule behind exactly 14/14/14.
10. **Determinism and the content generators (A4, C18, B3).** Whether a non-null `seed` reproduces anything; render-time fills; the district-name contraction rule and landmark detection; building name/description generation; backfill selection and household composition. These matter less for geometry parity but are where the base must *exceed* FTG (per-entity sub-seeds, stable ids, evolution).

## §5 Cheapest experiments that would raise confidence most

The roadlength100 capture proved the method: **one temporary settlement per day, one form leaf changed, decoded with the existing stdlib scripts**, closes a question in an afternoon. In order of value per capture:

1. **Recover the road-graph faces from both payloads** (no new capture; a face-walk over the decoded graph). Gives the block-size distribution inside district rings, the position of dividing roads relative to lot OBBs and longest edges, and the plot band depth — the direct discriminator for unknowns 3 and 4. Also yields empty-lot statistics for the `Number of districts` question.
2. **`initialRoadsType` sweep** (Dead-end, Single through-road, Three-way, Six-way) and **`branchType: CURVE_AROUND_ORIGIN`** on the Galeiaward base: pins the seed pattern and the ring-radius rule (does a branch keep |p − origin| exactly?) — unknown 2.
3. **`verticalBranchSegments` 2 vs 5 with `horizontalBranchSegments` fixed** (and vice versa): identifies what "vertical" and "horizontal" mean from the resulting block aspect ratios — unknown 2.
4. **`spokeStaySpokeProbability` 0 vs 1 and `maxSpokesInStage` 1 vs 3**: counts type-1 runs and their branching to confirm the documented semantics.
5. **`wallPlacement: AROUND_STAGE_LOTS` on stage 1, and `placeWallAfter` on stage 2**: two tower chains → the tracing rule and tower spacing law from ≥ 46 towers instead of 17 — unknown 5. Zoom screenshots at the 100 m bar give band width and gate treatment.
6. **A VILLAGE with no coast and no river, farmland Around perimeter**: a clean, water-free graph for unknown 3, a second sample of the 1.52-buildings-per-lot figure, and a hamlet-scale suburb for unknown 8.
7. **Coast presets ×3 (Long beach, Rocky, Cliffs) with `Ease into detail` off**: decoded coast polylines give amplitude/wavelength spectra per option — a cheap way to choose between midpoint displacement and noise for unknown 6; `maxDivergences` 2 with `Sharp curves` gives branch-start positions and the island rule in action.
8. **Two identical requests with `seed: 12345`** (if the field is honoured): any difference in the decoded graphs settles A4 in one comparison; identical outputs would raise seed-exact for the *product*, though not for our derivation.
9. **Upper-class-only district and shanty-town settlement (average lifestyle Squalid)**: confirms the 11.3/11.6 decoration fields, the tent placement mode, and the "after the first half" rule.
10. **Frame-by-frame reading of the guide's proposal-queue GIFs** (no capture): the order in which green proposals turn grey exposes the district-stage priority ordering that only the suburb section states.
11. **Screenshot metrology at the 100 m bar** on existing captures: plot depth (shrink *d*), Gaps inset width, wall band width, pier width — constants for C7/C8/C11/C13 at zero capture cost.
