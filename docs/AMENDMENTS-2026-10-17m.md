## The owner's rules on territory (2026-10-17; quoted verbatim, typos kept; the readings are the session's, vetoable)

**659. OWNER RULE (T1) — Territory borders: occupation by a settlement power or military, vassals, and the persistent
encounter map (owner, 2026-10-17; `docs/PROGRESS.md` J377).** "also your thoughts on territory borders  which FMG does have
some inclusion on inclusive and expanded with our new emergent persistent encounter map work where it is occupied by a
settlement power or military, and vassals and the likes?"

  **(a) BORDERS ARE A PICTURE OF POLITICAL FACTS, NEVER A FACT.** The host owns who holds what — tier, population,
  prosperity, relationships with their subordinating kinds, treaties and sovereignty transfers, occupations, razings,
  foundings; the map projects them over the terrain and returns derived territory, one way (V13 §3.9's W8b, item 714;
  the port's `polity` input, item 721). **(b) THE HOST'S "TERRITORY" IS NOT THE POLITICAL MAP.** The host's
  territory is the total nearest-settlement partition its distances ride on; the political picture is a second
  partition, the **sway**, and the host's crossings never come from it (W8a and W8b; J-RR.48). **(c) OCCUPIED BY A
  SETTLEMENT POWER OR MILITARY.** Occupation is an overlay, never ownership; a garrisoned fort or watchtower is a held
  point of its holder; an army in the field is presence only and moves no border until a dated outcome, an occupation or
  a treaty transfers it (J-RR.52, J-RR.56). **(d) VASSALS AND THE LIKES.** An allegiance graph from the host's own links —
  vassals (including the occupation's vassalized rung) and puppet seats as members; satellites as claim points of their
  parent's allegiance; tributaries and compelled allies as bands and relations — with exclaves where the chain breaks
  (J-RR.51). **(e) THE
  REFERENCE GENERATOR'S INCLUSION, EXPANDED.** Its states, provinces and diplomacy become allegiances and links on a
  standalone import (item 709); unlike it, land beyond everyone's reach stays wild. **(f) THE PERSISTENT ENCOUNTER
  MAP.** The encounter crop reads the polity at its point and week — whose land, held or debatable, occupied, patrolled,
  fought over — and a road crossing a border proposes a toll house, bridge fort, watchpost or pass fort the DM may accept
  (V14 E1 and E4, items 764, 766; V13 §3.9 (16)).

**660. OWNER RULE (T2) — An upper limit per settlement, bounded but exponential (owner, 2026-10-17; J377).** "and
territory has an upper limit per individual settlement that expands bounded but exponential to the size, tier,
prosperity, relative distance and dynamics of neighbors and contnious chain of vassals."

  **(a) BOUNDED BUT EXPONENTIAL.** A settlement's reach grows at least geometrically with its tier and is capped per tier: reach =
  min(Λ·τ, capTier), τ growing by at least half again per rung (J-RR.49; V13 §3.9 (7)). **(b) SIZE, TIER, PROSPERITY.** τ = τTier × kSize ×
  kProsperity × (1 + the chain bonus). **(c) RELATIVE DISTANCE.** Strength fades with travel time on the host's own cost
  law at walking pace, so mountains resist, a navigable river adds its own crossing time (V13 §2.3's `river.crossingDays`)
  and a valley town's reach hugs its valleys — the loss-of-strength
  gradient. **(d) DYNAMICS OF NEIGHBORS.** Kin and allies split at the travel-time midline without pushing; neutrals
  yield to the stronger; adversaries push by relative strength and leave a **debatable land** held by neither where they
  are within a margin (J-RR.50). **(e) THE CONTINUOUS CHAIN OF VASSALS.** "Continuous" read as an unbroken chain of links
  whose sways touch or are joined by a road within the host's reinforcement band; the chain grows the overlord's own
  reach by a bounded bonus with diminishing returns by depth, reinforces adversarial comparisons only, and stops at the
  realm's cap; a broken chain is an exclave (J-RR.51). **(f) EVERY COEFFICIENT A TUNING CELL,** UNMEASURED until
  measured, with its historical anchor (a market day's walk, a city-state's two to three days, a realm limited by weeks
  of courier travel; V13 §2.3, item 699). **(g) BORDERS MOVE ONLY ON DATED FACTS.** One receipted revision
  per week over a dirty box, with dwells so a settlement at a band edge does not flicker; the host's frozen territory and
  its crossings untouched (J-RR.53).

**661. OWNER RULE (T3) — Military enforcement, fights over the march, the ceiling kept, patrols (owner, 2026-10-17;
J378).** "and of course military strenght to enforce. where two hostile neighbors fight regarding the march. still having
the cieling that you described but where contested, this also becomes part of the tie breakeralso there should be
patrols"

  **(a) MILITARY STRENGTH TO ENFORCE, AS THE TIE-BREAKER.** A second strength — the host's force band (its capacity facets without the
  economy facet, J384 (4)) and garrison presence per seat, continuous vassals' forces, garrisoned sites — at army pace, entering only land the civil projection left
  debatable (J-RR.52). **(b) THE CEILING KEPT.** Military strength never extends a reach, never moves a decided line, never
  takes a town's own ground. **(c) HOSTILE NEIGHBOURS FIGHTING OVER THE MARCH.** The host's dated verdicts on fights there
  move a hold band per segment and side that decays by a season; the fight itself is a fighting overlay that never moves
  a line (J-RR.56). **(d) PATROLS.** Each power patrols its roads and its side of a debatable stretch at a cadence derived
  from its garrisons, its neighbours' hostility and the road's grade, the host's own road closures and wardens first;
  present on a fixed weekly phase keyed by its segment and power, never a roll; drawn as a layer, read by encounters,
  offered to the host as an extent and its basis (never as a time) and never imposed (J-RR.54; V13 §3.9 (14)).
