### 1.5 The fix shape for M0-a-09's round S15

* **Function and rule.** Fix `kerb.ts` `rowPath` as `cycleOf` (`:899`) and `laidExactly` (`:871`) use it. The held
  path of a run's segment k (placed vertex k to k+1) must run along **that segment's own held edges**. It may not
  pass through another vertex of the run, and it is never the way back round the ring. Mirror 2's exclusion set is
  the least rule that does this. A stronger form would also bound the path to the segment's corridor: every vertex
  within the insertion's move of the input segment, the hot-pixel test `laidExactly` already applies. That form also
  covers a run whose own segment is held through a vertex shared with another part. `sharedPath` (`:956`) looks up an
  earlier part's held segment through the same `rowPath`, and should pass that run's placed vertices as well.
* **The clauses it must satisfy.** §3.7: kerb(F) is the one function, and corridorUnion commits exactly it. §4.6:
  "S9's `corridorUnion` then commits exactly those kerbs", the contract S8 ≡ S9, on **every** face of the district,
  including faces S8 never cut. Face 1015 holds no seed, yet it refuses the whole district's batch. Each of the
  seven block misses listed after the refusal reads `(S9: the L1 face, no L2 record)`, and with the refusal gone the
  seed's 62 beyond rows fall to 0 (§1.4). §12.4: S9's share is 25 ms per district. The
  fix adds no read and no pass; the search is the same breadth-first search over the row's edges. N-45 (a): `keptDeep`
  stays as the post-condition at its bound, `DRY_MOVE_CM`. The read succeeds because the cycle is right, not because
  the bound is wider (J264, ZERO REFUSALS). N-60 (a)/(b): the smoke's expectation is 0 / 0 beyond. This is a new
  finding of class (ii) BAND: the engine lost a band. On this face the loss was refused rather than silent. Owner
  **M0-a-09**.

## Appendix — the probes (session scratchpad; none in the tree)

* `probe1.mts kind seed smoke|probe`. `smoke` runs `measure.ts` `s8s9(derive(kind, seed, {}))` exactly as the
  harness's worker does. `probe` runs the S9 loop by hand: `k.tx(L2, corridorUnion(o, {owner, level: 1}, widths))`
  per district in the harness's order. At a refusal it prints `kerbCensus(p)` and the refused face, re-read alone at
  L1 and L2. `PREFACE=<face>` reads that face before any S9 commit. `ROOT` selects the tree or a mirror.
* `probe2.mts kind seed sx sy px py r`. It finds the block seed's L1 face and prints `readKerb` (F's rings, the kerb,
  the splits, the spines with hw and ends, the census) near the point: before S9, and again at S9 after the earlier
  districts' commits, at L1 and at L2. It then runs the district's corridorUnion and prints its closings and the L2
  face at the seed. On mirror 1 it also dumps the dry-run's parts and labelled faces near the point (`facediag-*.json`).
* `probe3.mts kind seed blocks`. For named blocks: the stop kerb, the harness's stop region (`stopRegion`), the
  working ring per seed with the read's spines near the far vertices, then S9 as the harness runs it, and the
  far-vertex lists in each direction.
* `fix25.mts`. The harness's `cutRegion` / `seedComponents` on the fusion 25 fixture, with the loops' areas, seeds and
  pinch widths.
* `queue.sh`. `probe1.mts … smoke` on mirror 2 over the REPORTED seeds, three at a time.
* Mirror 1's hooks: at `KEPT_DEEP` (`kerb.ts` `dryRegion`) and on a named face, `globalThis.__diag` and
  `__faceDiag` save the refusing part's clipped ring, held cycle, placed vertices with their levels and rows, each
  segment's `rowPath`, every edge carrying the part's row, and the kept face with its label. `measure.ts` has the
  eight-miss cap lifted and a `KERBREAD` line per refused read. None of this changes a result: fusion 4's and
