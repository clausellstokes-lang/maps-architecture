  and after and equal to a twin arena's rejected batch; the same slots taken afterwards).
- **The widened types compile in all fourteen projects** (F13).
- **No registered input has dependent gate crossings** (F3: 78 plans, 496 crossings, the closest pair 16.9 m apart, a
  per-split plan equal to the sequence on every one). The dry run is still the only design exact by construction on
  every lawful input (two crossings of one way within 52 cm differ by the snap rule), so the design stands; its gain
  today is the receipts', and that is said.
- **The field equals the regular expression on every commit-time refusal met** (ten of ten in the edge suite's planted
  S1 cells); the site families met no water-batch refusal at all in 38 derivations.
