## 0. In plain words

The kernel is the part of the design that does the shape mathematics. All 24 kernel items handed to round 2 are now
genuinely fixed: new lines that snap onto old ones can no longer leave a too-short piece (re-measured: none in 600 000
trials), corners that records point at are named in the drawing so a warp or an upgrade cannot make them point at the
wrong corner, street widths and wall heights stay put when a junction is inserted, the wall band can no longer be erased
by the street-cutting steps, and the parallel workers each start from a frozen copy of the town. Looking for new
problems found sixteen, three of them serious. The frozen copy the workers receive leaves out the index of the thin
strips built just before it, so the workers cannot see them. Regenerating a district on the town's edge loses the 2 m
strip along that edge. And the rule that lets a new canal meet existing streets only at prepared points is missing from
the kernel's core insertion rules, which also cannot tell which points were prepared. Each finding carries a concrete
required change; nothing is deferred.
