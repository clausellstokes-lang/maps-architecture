## 0. In plain words

This seat checked the parts of the design that say what is built, in what order, how it is tested, how it is kept
legally clean and which decisions were taken, after the first fix-all round was merged into `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`. Of
the 39 problems assigned to this area, **37 are now answered by a concrete mechanism in the text** (one of them, the
calibration against historical maps, is the owner's later stage and its interface is in place). **Two are not fully
fixed.** First, the design still uses two names taken from FTG's add-on for the virtual tabletop (`stateId` and
`SIMULATE_TIME`), while its own clean-room rule says those names are never reused and are on the list the build scanner
rejects — so the design would fail its own check. Second, the test of "does re-rolling an area give the same kind of
result as a fresh town" still fails a perfect re-roller: executed here, a perfect one passes the four-number check only
70–79 % of the time, and the rule demands 95 %. A corrected test (compare where each re-rolled number ranks among 50
fresh towns) was executed and behaves correctly: a perfect re-roller fails about 1 % of test campaigns, as intended, and
FTG's known "too similar" fault fails every time. The check also found **twelve new problems** the revision introduced,
one of them serious: the side-by-side protocol lets a milestone go ahead when the owner has not looked, while the build
plan says it may not — so "mastered" could be declared without the owner's eye, which the owner's own definition
requires. The other eleven are small contradictions (records kept in a folder the product's tests may not read, a test
scheduled before the feature it measures, a stale sentence about FTG sizes, a miscounted table). No deferral wording
remains in this area's sections.
