## 0. In plain words

All 27 things round 2 set out to fix in the drawing, labelling, export and speed sections are fixed in the text: each
now has a concrete rule, the numbers behind the rules were re-computed and agree, and nothing in these sections is put
off. None of the earlier fixes in these sections broke. Checking the new text turned up ten new problems, one major and nine minor. The major
one is that the model-speed targets from the improvement plan (a town's model in 0.1 s, a city's in 0.3 s, a
metropolis's in 1 s) are only met at each size's typical population, and the larger towns, cities and metropolises within
each size are slower — this should be declared for the owner's veto the way the first-map miss already is (NR-1). The others are
a sentence that promises cities in under half a second when the largest take 0.6 s, a missing field in the style-sheet
format, three places where the new "background drawing thread holds the only copy of the drawing list" rule left other
parts of the program without a stated way to reach it, an unstated random seed for the tree-pattern tiles, a 3-D export
extension that may need declaring, edits waiting while a saved town is being checked, a stale name in the build plan,
and four algorithm sources missing from the clean-room list.
