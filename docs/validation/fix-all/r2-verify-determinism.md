## 0. In plain words

This seat re-checked the part of the design that makes the same seed give the same town, keeps edits safe and handles
failures, after the second fix-all round was merged. **All sixteen items handed to this area are now answered by a
concrete mechanism in the text**: undo and redo are recorded as events, so they work when several people share a town
through a host; a shared link is refused, with the file offered instead, whenever the town needs a setting pack or host
content the other machine may lack; replaying a saved town names streets exactly as the session did; "New random"
checks that something really changed; work limits grow with the town's population; and river and terrain edits now
move bridges, re-route roads, cut streets at a new bank and re-seat every building on changed ground.

The check found **ten new problems**, one of them serious: **a river move cannot be committed as the steps are now
ordered.** The geometry library only lets new water meet an existing street at points prepared for it beforehand, and
the river edit digs the new channel first and prepares the bridges afterwards; the fix is to use the order the canal
edit already uses (prepare, then dig, then re-route). The other nine are small: harbour moles break the new "roads cross
water only on bridges" check; undo, reload and "who is undoing" need two precise rules; three lists of what can be
re-rolled disagree; tree and roof patterns repeat from one seed to the next; one list of what a town is made from omits
setting packs; a user's own name list can still slip into a shared link; the file's sharing of unchanged pieces is less
exact than claimed and three speed bars lack a fallback; and whether a re-rolled street keeps its generated name is
stated two ways. No deferral wording remains in these sections.
