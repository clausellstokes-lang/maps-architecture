### PRB7D-TIM-02 — MAJOR · (a) (and (b) on reading A) — what of a `cleared` wall record stands

**Law or term.** *Anything across a strip*: "a run of a wall record neither `dismantled` nor cleared (an absorbed record's
among them)" (adj l. 260–262); law 1 (2)–(6), which read "an enclosing wall's line", "its band", gates, towers and fixed
places "as a function of what stands" (adj l. 230–231, 270–281); *the band*, "its section there, by §4.8's rows along its
stretch profile" (adj l. 238) whatever the run's condition.

**The case.** W8 (`absorbWall`) and its pathway's dismantling at an epoch; a replay's burial (W-row none; law 4 lists it);
C1 and C11 of W8 (a raised way founded across an absorbed ring).

**Claim.** `absorbWall` leaves the Enceinte "existence `cleared` (cause `convert:absorbWall`)" (V06 l. 717), its runs on
"the `cleared` record" (V06 l. 1492); a replay's burial "clears the listed surface records (existence `cleared`, cause
`rule:burial`)" (V06 l. 3223), the destroyed ring's runs left `breached` (V06 l. 3218–3220); `Existence` has one `cleared`
(base l. 2893). So the term contradicts itself and the law has no word for either record:
* **Reading A** ("cleared" = the record's existence): an absorbed record's runs do not lie across — the chamber is laid at
  `absorbWall`'s commit, against the parenthesis, W8·C2 (adj l. 945), R7-04 (b) (adj l. 1135–1145) and veto 9 [15] (adj
  l. 418–421, "an absorbed ring's run not yet dismantled"); and an aqueduct founded across an absorbed ring's `breached`
  run may stand a pier on it, the run not standing — the breach of item 110 (b) as the program reads it that PRB7-18 was
  flagged for (adj l. 2215–2223, 2255; W8·C1).
* **Reading B** (the parenthesis governs): a cleared record's undismantled runs lie across — then a replay's buried ring,
  `cleared` with its runs `breached` and never dismantled after burial, keeps every `vault` span over it `open` for ever,
  against PRB7C-TIM-09's closure, "it clears the runs, so nothing lies across (law 1 (8)) and the chamber is laid again in
  the burial's commit" (adj l. 2377). PRB7C-TIM-09's closure fails here.
* Law 1 (3) (adj l. 274–278) bars a road deck across "an enclosing wall's line … laid before" its course; whether the
  absorbed record's line stays such a line once every run there is `dismantled` decides whether a viaduct founded across it
  is built then (W8·C1; E-V6-3's addition, adj l. 1935–1937, "until that run is `dismantled`") or waits for ever. And the
  band term gives a dismantled run its band by its profile, so W8·C1/C11's "a `dismantled` one none" has no law.

**Required change (Part L).** Terms, after *The band* (adj l. 242), add: "* **What stands of a wall.** A wall record whose
existence is not `cleared` stands whole — every run, whatever its condition, with its line, band, wall strips, gates,
posterns, towers and fixed places. A record `absorbWall` cleared (cause `convert:absorbWall`) stands only in its runs whose
condition is not `dismantled`, each with its line, its band as it stood (§2.7's support step (3)) and the gates, towers
and fixed places on it, its wall strips merged into the blocks; a `dismantled` run of it stands nowhere. A record cleared

a wall*)". Law 1 (3) (adj l. 274): "an enclosing wall's line" → "the line of an enclosing wall's runs that stand".
