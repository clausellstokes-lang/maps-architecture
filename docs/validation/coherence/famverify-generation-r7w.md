### VF7w-GEN-02 · major · TWO READINGS (law 1 (9), law 2 (a)) · owner pipeline

**The pull list's new widening reads over a held building's strip as well, a strip the laws have already widened. Read
so, the pull widens it twice and searches fewer shapes than law 1 admits.** The table and tests lenses raise the same
sentence as VF7w-TBL-02 and VF7w-TST-01. This lens found it on its own, on the generation and regeneration paths.

*The text.* The fix pass's P2 wrote at §4.4 S5a *Trace* (2) (l. 6602): "every held building's strip and a shape-locked
raised way's `vault`-use spans' strips, each widened all round by the wall strips' width there (law 1 (10): no line, band
or wall strip across one)". Its "each" can cover both items or only the `vault`-use spans' strips.

*What the laws say.*
* *A held building's strip* is already "widened all round by the meeting wall's strips' width" (l. 2642–2643).
* Law 1 (9) bars a wall's **line** from that strip (l. 2736–2738).
* *beneath* (l. 3073–3074): "a held building's widened strip and the bent group's strip each count the band's half
  already, so a line is kept out of them as they are drawn". *beneath* (l. 3124–3128) adds: "a trace laid by the pull or
  a cost field treats it as impassable".
* The pipeline seat's own note says "never twice" (`assemble-r7w.md` §8). Those words are not in the design.

*Two builds.*
* **Builder 2** widens only the `vault`-use spans' strips, as law 1 (9) and (10) require. Its pull searches exactly the
  shapes law 1 admits, "its own cost choosing among them" (law 2 (a), l. 2812–2813).
* **Builder 1** widens the held building's strip a second time, by up to about 20 m more each side. Its pull searches a
  strict subset of those shapes. Near a held building in an arch vault, it lays a different trace.
* Builder 1's search also comes up empty sooner. Take a short aqueduct, or one whose other spans are low or shape-locked:
  the doubly widened strip can close the last lawful crossing. The stage then goes to F-WALL's raised-way rung, and
  sometimes stays unwalled, at generation from a creation prefix and at a regeneration. Through V06 §3.7.6 (5) (V06 l.
  1611–1613), which takes this list "each widened as it says", an epoch circuit waits. In both cases a lawful trace
  existed.

Both builds keep law 1 and pass §5.3's law-1 row and T110-4. Under the T110-2 lead's class rule (l. 19256–19263), no bar
pins a pulled trace's place. Builder 1's empty-search outcome is the one PRB7H-GEN-04 (i) ruled against J144 as the
program reads it, the class of VF7w-GEN-01 and the regraded VF7v-GEN-03. So this is no valid choice: major. Reordering the
sentence closes it.

**Required change** (pipeline, §4.4 S5a *Trace* (2), l. 6602): "every held building's strip and a shape-locked raised
way's `vault`-use spans' strips, each widened all round by the wall strips' width there (law 1 (10): no line, band or wall
strip across one)" → "a shape-locked raised way's `vault`-use spans' strips, each widened all round by the wall strips'
width there (law 1 (10): no line, band or wall strip across one), and every held building's strip as its term widens it,
never again (law 1 (9))".
