## Verdict

Run as one resolver over this lens, the refined laws decide most of what the earlier passes found open: an epoch is one
commit ending at `under.extend`, so the arch vault's chamber has its provider on every path (PRB7D-TIM-01 holds); decay and
repair of a run (W4, W5, W10), the landfall (W12) and the hazards are *unchanged meetings* or kept off held parts by
*Entry*; an absorbed ring's runs stand until dismantled; a village enceinte VS3 lays and a town ring handed down keep their
own bands across the hand-off; the frame growth's bound is law 2 (c)'s; *Order* dates every crossing at an epoch by the op
order. They are not yet complete and determinate in five places. Two terms still split over time: *what stands of a wall*
decides by the record's existence and cause, so a ring `wallToWay` leaves `converted` stands whole — its former band
across every arch it crossed for ever, every road deck barred from the ring road — and a burial that lists runs leaves a
ring standing whole or nowhere (PRB7E-TIM-01, a blocker on the common epoch path; PRB7D-TIM-02's closure fails); *anything
across a strip* takes in the plot and building a declared placement seats in a chamber (no fixed point) and another raised
way's reservation (two fixed points where decks cross) (PRB7E-TIM-02). *The band* by its derivation still names the VS3
stage alone, so every village-kind enceinte `drawWall` lays in VS3's form takes two widths (PRB7E-TIM-03; PRB7D-TIM-04's
closure fails). The shape lock, applied by J147, holds the readings law 1 (7) and (8) give, while those readings follow
what stands of themselves: every decay, fall, repair, dismantle, delete and burial beside a shape-locked structure either
applies or gives way, and the one op that cannot wait has no outcome (PRB7E-TIM-04). And law 1 (8)'s chamber, after an op
outside the family, is checked by no row or bar (PRB7E-TIM-05). Each closes by one sentence in Part L — the term run by
run, the term's exclusions with a creation order between raised ways, VS3's construction named, the readings held one way
only, the check's queried set — and none needs an owner rule changed. Three minors correct examples (PRB7E-TIM-06 – 08).
**Majors remaining in this lens: 5 (1 blocker, 4 major); minor 3.** The laws should be amended at these five places
before any repair seat writes against them.
