## Verdict

Within this lens neither table is complete yet. The crossing table has no row for three hand events that re-trace or move
a wall — the castle's keep and bailey grips, a terrain stroke on a cliff anchor, a `warp` (PRB7C-LCK-04) — and its C13
names three of the raised way's own ops where §7.1 binds several more, `revert` among them, so PRB7B-05's "no other op moves a
span" does not hold (PRB7C-LCK-06); W14 has no outcome for the run's own fixed places its wider strips bring into a zone
(PRB7C-LCK-08); W1·C8 denies held things at a first generation, where a skeleton's held wall and raised road meet in
stage order against the log order of the hand map (PRB7C-LCK-10); a raised road's carried way has no rule at a second
held wall (PRB7C-LCK-12); and a span's use under a wall is refused on the canvas and applied by the clerk, under two path
forms (PRB7C-LCK-07). The way-and-channel table's hand row (b) holds in this lens. Of the three cross-cutting sentences:
the lock sentence's (G2) fate has no type and its change list no fixed-place move (PRB7C-LCK-01), its guards do not say
whether a hand stroke's own moves and a drawn wall bent in its author's op are a bend or a fate (PRB7C-LCK-02), its (G3)
splits on hand ops that run F-WALL (PRB7C-LCK-05), its retaining-wall clause denies the towers the record carries and
binds only a drawn stroke (PRB7C-LCK-09), and its held-building refusal offers an Unlock that releases nothing
(PRB7C-LCK-11); the bent-group sentence does not say which law lays a `drawWall`'s points form (PRB7C-LCK-03); the
fixed-place sentence names "a drawn castle", an op the set does not have (PRB7C-LCK-04), and a zone whose widths change
under W14 (PRB7C-LCK-08). C8's other cells, §7.1's lock row as E-D1 amends it, §7.2's protections and undo hold. Twelve
majors remain in this lens, none a blocker; each closes locally with the amended words above, and none reopens J144's
rule or a Part P decision.
