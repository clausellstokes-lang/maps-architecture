## 3. Issues

### VEL5-r5-01 — MAJOR, EDGE-UNDEFINED, owner pipeline

**A late change folded at `W₀` + 1 moves the document's current week back, although the text says it never falls back.**

§4.11 *The clock* (1) (base l. 8453–8454) defines the current week as "the week of the last folded event that carries
one". §2.9's `EpochRecord` comment (l. 3140) and J-A67.45's index row (l. 22742: "the last folded event's") say the same.

R5-03's (4) (l. 8475–8487) says something different. A late change "never folds at a week before `W₀`, so the
document's current week ((1)) never falls back", and "every other late change folds after the last epoch at `W₀` + 1
where `W₁` is after `W₀`, else at `W₀`". V08 §3.6 (6) (l. 1944–1947) repeats the claim.

The claim holds against `W₀` only. Between epochs an absorb with no year end, crossing or calamity folds its element
events standalone at their weeks, after `W₀` (V08 §3.6 (3), (6)). That is the usual weekly sync. Take this sequence:
1. An absorb at `W₀` + 3 folds a change that begins a Works.
2. The next absorb carries a change dated at or before `W₀` (a back-dated decree, the case R5-03 rules).
3. That change is appended at `W₀` + 1. By (1), the current week is now `W₀` + 1.

The drawn present (J-A67.45; *Works* (9)) then shows the Works before its start: the house staked a sync ago vanishes.
Every export draws that week too. A clock turned back after such an absorb does the same at `W₀`. So does a change dated
in (`W₀`, the current week).

Two builders differ. One derives (1) literally and the present regresses. The other keeps "never falls back" with a
maximum, or folds at the current week. The bars do not tell them apart:
* T-V8-49 (g) asserts only "the document's current week after the absorb not before `W₀`" (V08 l. 3311).
* T76-1 asserts "never before it" (base l. 18245–18247).

Both pass the regression.

**Required change.**
1. §4.11 *The clock* (1): "The document's current week is derived — the week of the last folded event that carries one
   — never a stored counter (§2.9)" → "The document's current week is derived — the greatest week the folded events
   carry, so an event folded after one of a later week (a late change at `W₀` + 1 or `W₀`, (4); a dossier change dated
   before an event an earlier absorb folded) never moves it back — never a stored counter (§2.9)".
2. Ends:
   - (model) §2.9's `EpochRecord` comment: "the week of the last folded event that carries one" → "the greatest week the
     folded events carry".
   - (scope) J-A67.45's index row: "(the last folded event's)" → "(the greatest folded week, §4.11 *The clock* (1))".
   - (V08) T-V8-49 (g), after "the document's current week after the absorb not before `W₀`", adds: "; and on a copy, an
     absorb past `W₀` with no year end, crossing or calamity folding a standalone change at `W₀` + 3 that begins a Works,
     then an absorb carrying a non-calamity change dated at or before `W₀` — folded at `W₀` + 1, the document's current
     week still `W₀` + 3 and that Works drawn at its stage there".
   - (scope) T76-1's late non-calamity clause: "the document's current week never before it" → "the document's current
     week never before it nor before the week of any event an earlier absorb folded".

(The adjudicator may instead keep (1) and fold every newly carried change dated before the current week at that week,
its tick in its receipt. The bars above then read the same.)

### VEL5-r5-02 — MAJOR, EDGE-UNTESTED, owner V08

**A held late calamity across an absorb that folds no epoch has no fixture.**

R5-03 holds a late calamity carried by an absorb whose week is not after `W₀` "for the first epoch a later absorb folds"
(base §4.11 *The clock* (4), l. 8483; V08 §3.6 (3), l. 1871–1876; §7.7's comment, l. 11825).

V08 §3.6 (4) (l. 1903–1908) writes the whole held list in every `dossierFacts` event: "the binding's list less each
calamity the first epoch this absorb folds carries, plus each late calamity this absorb holds".

So a later absorb past `W₀` that folds no epoch (no year end, no crossing, no new calamity) must:
* write the list unchanged;
* fold no epoch for the held calamity, unlike a newly carried late calamity with `W₁` after `W₀`, for which "the port
  folds one at `W₁`";
* leave the calamity waiting, up to the next year end.

That is the common path. Weekly syncs between year ends fold no epoch.

The only fixtures do not reach it. T-V8-49 (g) (V08 l. 3311) and T76-1 (base l. 18242–18245) hold the calamity, then
make the very next absorb fold an epoch (a population move past the tolerance). Two wrong builds pass every bar:
* one that writes `lateCalamities` from this absorb's new holds alone, so the list empties at a no-epoch absorb and the
  calamity is silently lost;
* one that folds an epoch at `W₁` for a held calamity.

T-V8-5's fold-equals-block check passes both. VV78-r5-01 names this wait in its claim but tests only a forked copy.

**Required change.** T-V8-49 (g), after "the next absorb past `W₀` folding the crossing and the calamity's strike at its
first epoch's first week, its deaths in that epoch's burial need", add:

"; and on a copy, between the hold and that absorb, an absorb past `W₀` with no year end, no crossing and no calamity
(one institution's `state` changed) — no epoch folded, its `dossierFacts` event listing the held calamity in
`lateCalamities`, the calamity not struck — then an absorb past the next year end folding the year-end epoch, the
calamity riding it at its first week `W₀` + 1, struck once, its deaths in that epoch's burial need, `lateCalamities`
empty after".

### VEL5-r5-03 — MAJOR, CATALOGUE-GAP, owner V08

**A line migration re-keys the binding rows and carried facts, but not the keys the binding's held lists hold.**

R5-03 adds `DossierBinding.lateCalamities: string[]`, "the binding keys of late calamities held" (§7.7, l. 11825). A
held calamity's `CalamityRef` is built "from its carried `calamity` fact under the held key" (V08 §3.6 (3), l.
1894–1895). The calamity's key is a binding key the port mints (`calamity:<week>:<slug(name)>`, V08 §2.3).

At a line change, "B's `fromLines` entry for A re-keys the binding rows (a `rebind` `setAttr` per changed key …)". A
`fromLines` entry may be `{ rule: 'table'; rows: { from; to }[] }` (V08 §2.4 l. 587; §3.8 l. 2042–2047). §2.3's row life
(l. 566) says "a line migration re-keys rows by V08 §3.8". Nothing re-keys `lateCalamities`, or `quarantined[].key`,
which has the same gap.

Suppose the migrating absorb, or a later one, folds the epoch the held calamity rides. A builder who re-keys rows and
carried facts alone then looks up the old key and finds nothing. The `CalamityRef` cannot be built: a crash, or the
calamity silently lost against R5-03's "never lost". A quarantined record's old key likewise stops shielding it from the
diff.

Two builders differ: one maps the lists through the same entry, the other does not. The case is not in the canon's
catalogue (edges.md lists no migration entry for V08 §3.8's binding lists). T-V8-11 (l. 3273) runs only `identity` and
the missing-entry hold.

**Required change.**
1. V08 §3.8's line-change bullet, after "re-keys the binding rows (a `rebind` `setAttr` per changed key, V08 §3.6 (5),
   no address changes)", add: ", and every binding key the binding's lists hold — each `quarantined` entry's `key` and
   each `lateCalamities` key — by the same entry in that absorb, before the diff, the absorb's `dossierFacts` event
   carrying both lists under the new keys (V08 §3.6 (4)); a key the entry does not map keeps its key".
2. V08 §2.6's `DossierBinding.lateCalamities` row, its migrate cell, adds: "; a line migration re-keys it by the
   `fromLines` entry (V08 §3.8)".
3. T-V8-11 adds: "on its test line with a `fromLines` table that re-keys calamity keys, a document holding a late
   calamity (T-V8-49 (g)'s second absorb) and a quarantined record, migrated by an absorb that folds an epoch — the
   calamity riding that epoch under its new key, struck once, `lateCalamities` empty after, and the quarantined
   record's key re-keyed, its carried state still skipped by the diff".

### VEL5-r5-04 — MAJOR, EDGE-UNDEFINED, owner determinism

**A hand way or carve across a locked or pinned raised way: refused by the lock row, or bent under a span?**

Before round 5, §2.7's support step (6) ended (`3310b53`, l. 2615): "A lock or pin on the raised way holds what (b)
holds; … so a locked or pinned raised way is crossed as an unlocked one is (§7.4's lock column; T110-2)." R5-02 replaced
(6). The live text (base l. 2587) says only "A lock or pin holds the same". §7.4's raised-way lock column (l. 11403)
reads "its deck and piers held, as every raised way is from its creation".

Meanwhile:
* §7.1's general row (l. 10587) refuses `refuse.locked` "a map event … [that] crosses, targets or moves a locked or
  pinned entity … — a drawn way across a locked entity".
* §7.2's test (l. 10815) refuses "a drawn way across a locked footprint".
* `bend.raised.underSpan`'s and `bend.raised.around`'s row (l. 10632) and `bend.wall.raisedCrossing`'s (l. 10631) say
  "a standing raised way" with no word on locks.

So a ground `drawWay`, a moved way or a hand carve across a locked raised way matches both a refusal and a bend.

T110-2 (4) (l. 18491–18500) runs the hand `drawWall` "again with the raised way locked" and expects the bend, which
settles the wall case. Neither T110-2 (5)'s hand roads (l. 18500–18503) nor T-V9-52 (V09 l. 4404) runs a lock. So
one builder refuses the road and the other bends it under a span or round the raised way. Both pass every bar.

A way passing beneath changes none of the raised way's parts, which is what "holds the same" means. The text no longer
says so where the dry run looks.

**Required change.**
1. §7.1's lock row, cause: "— a drawn way across a locked entity," → "— a drawn way across a locked entity (but not a
   ground way, a carve or a wall stretch passing beneath a locked or pinned raised way's deck, which changes none of its
   parts and is bent by the `bend.raised.underSpan`, `bend.raised.around` and `bend.wall.raisedCrossing` rows as
   beneath an unlocked one: §2.7's support step (6), a lock or pin holds the same),".
2. `bend.raised.underSpan`'s row, cause: "meeting a standing raised way's supports" → "meeting a standing raised way's —
   locked, pinned or neither — supports". `bend.wall.raisedCrossing`'s row: "across a standing raised way" → "across a
   standing raised way, locked, pinned or neither".
3. END (scope): T110-2 (5), after "a hand road wider than every `open` span's clear width drawn across one", add ", each
   hand road run again with the raised way locked and with it pinned — the same outcome, no `refuse.locked`".
