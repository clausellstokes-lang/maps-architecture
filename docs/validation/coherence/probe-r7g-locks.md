## Verdict

Within the locks and hand edits lens the laws are not yet complete and determinate: **six majors remain, no blocker,
and one minor.** Pass 6's refinements closed what they were written for in this lens — law 1 (11) gives every hand op one
side for a Tier-1 thing, "lawful" takes a gate's way, (4) keeps a band out of one raised way's strip but to cross it,
*Carried way*'s gate has one class and one lifetime, a re-shape cuts no standing deck, and the check is two-sided — and
none of pass 6's locks findings is re-raised but where its closure fails. But **newly**, the one term pass 6 put in place
of every date, opens two classes. Read against "what stood as a commit began", it makes an exact undo or redo re-create
lawfully stood meetings "newly", so a lock made before the target leaves law 4 with nothing to name and ctrl+Z with no
outcome (PRB7G-LCK-01); read per point, it bars a line moved or widened within a meeting that stood and passes a strip
grown onto a line that did not move, where (9) and (10) speak of relations (PRB7G-LCK-02). The gate *Carried way* makes in
a placement-locked wall is laid by an operator that re-traces the wall's run and adds a vertex to the very fields the
check compares (PRB7G-LCK-03, PRB7F-LCK-02's closure failing in part); (4)'s new band clause meets the Terms' union of
strips with "its deck line" and "long sides" that two raised ways together do not have (PRB7G-LCK-04); (10)'s repair
exception, reworded without "it had", caps a run shape-locked while its span lay fallen, against J147 (3) (PRB7G-LCK-05);
and law 2 (a) names a hand castle siting both a search and a given value (PRB7G-LCK-06). Each closes with the words given,
none reopening J144, J147, J148 or J149; the minor is an example and an END line corrected to PRB7F-LCK-04's law.
