**Case.** (i) A ring the user has shape-locked runs beneath a standing road deck, its run capped at the soffit (law 1

## Verdict

Within the locks and hand edits lens the laws are not yet complete and determinate: **seven majors remain, no blocker,
and three minors.** The pass-5 refinements closed what they were written for — (a)'s reach is a bound with local
re-lays, (b)'s passable set is what (b) may move, a span's own reservation and seat are never across its own strip, and
law 1 (9) reads only the lines a commit lays, so no lock order is needed — and none of pass 5's locks findings is
re-raised. But three of the new texts open new ambiguities. Law 1 (10)'s date test lets a shape lock be reached by any
meeting made or deepened without a new date — a value edit, a re-section, an undo or a redo — against *Entry*'s own
words and J147 (PRB7F-LCK-01); *Placement*'s field list makes the one gate *Carried way* makes in a placement-locked
