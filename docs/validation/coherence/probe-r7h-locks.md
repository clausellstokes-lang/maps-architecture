## Verdict

Within the locks and hand edits lens the laws as pass 7 left them are not yet complete and determinate: **four majors
remain, no blocker, and one minor.** Pass 7's refinements closed what they were written for in this lens — one undo,
exact or refused with its reason; **newly** compared meeting by meeting; placement as a curve, so the gate *Carried way*
inserts in a placement-locked ring moves nothing; one split of law 2 (a) into given and searched ops, the castle's grip
among the given; law 1 (4) read raised way by raised way — and none of pass 7's locks findings is re-raised but where its
closure fails. But the clauses pass 7 merged or struck to keep Part L from growing opened two classes in the one
shape-lock rule: its lead bars every meeting made with a shape-held thing while its own list and every example bar only
the meetings that change it (PRB7H-LCK-01), and with "its readings then following what stands" struck, its first half
freezes a shape-held thing's soft readings and chambers against a removal of the other side (PRB7H-LCK-02, PRB7E-LCK-04's
closure failing). The union the Terms give a move now names a different end point from law 1's per-raised-way admission
under an aqueduct's clearing arch (PRB7H-LCK-03), and *Carried way*'s new place for its gate binds the hand path to a
point no standing wall farther on passes and pins it where law 2 (a) would move the crossing (PRB7H-LCK-04). Each closes
with the words given — together they leave Part L a word shorter than pass 7 did — none reopening J144, J147, J148, J149 or J150; the
minor corrects a Part P row to correction 7. Nothing here touches a lock's behaviour under an undo, a redo, a re-grade or
value edit within a standing meeting, or a repair (J150), nor any text Part A hands to round 8.
