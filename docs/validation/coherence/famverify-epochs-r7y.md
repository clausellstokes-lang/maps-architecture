### VF7y-EPO-01 — minor — READERS (a founded course meets a wall "as an unheld one", a shape lock J147 honours) — owner V06

**Claim.** V06 §3.19 (5), the founding's course (V06 l. 2844–2847): "Locked, pinned and declared entities — but a wall, a
retaining wall and another raised way, which a course meets as unheld ones (§2.7's laws, law 2 *Entry*; its worked
example the crossing table's lock sentence: a wall by §2.7's laws, …) —, … are **impassable** to the course's supports,
ramps, stairs and feet and to its clearing". The gloss was written before J147 split the lock (PRB7-20, R7-02: "both are
now met as unheld ones"). Since J147, the laws' *held* is "locked, on the axis named, and nothing else", and "**held in
shape** is shape-locked and holds its outline and size" (l. 2653–2656). The law the gloss cites says otherwise for a
shape lock — law 2 *Entry*: "No placement lock or pin on a wall-like structure, a raised way or an arch vault refuses,
skips or alters a meeting (J144: it holds placement, not shape); a shape lock is honoured (law 1 (10))" (l. 2758–2760) —
and law 1 (10) forbids any op to make "an aqueduct merged into" a shape-held thing (l. 2739–2743). The owner's rule is the
same, J147 (3): "where a raised way meets a wall with a shape lock, the shape lock is honoured — the shape-locked wall never
changes; the newer thing bends itself, else gives way (… at an epoch it waits)". "Meets as unheld ones" reads a
shape-locked wall as an unlocked one, wider than the law it cites and than J147 (3).

The step's deciding clause holds the case: "its course searching only the courses those laws admit against every
standing curtain (law 1 (2)–(6), (9)–(11), read with their terms; law 2 (a)): an aqueduct *over the band* …; a candidate
with no such course … not founded: it waits, `raised.waited` (law 2 (c))" (V06 l. 2855–2860), with law 1 (10) in F1's set
and *over the band* saying "a shape-locked one never merged into (law 1 (10))" (l. 3032–3033). The gloss sits in the
impassable list, which governs supports, ramps, feet and the clearing — and the support step's (3) keeps every station
off every enceinte's band whatever its lock (l. 2539–2547) — so no built outcome turns on it. T110-4 (c) runs the exact
case ("the ring both meet shape-locked before an aqueduct stroke and a raised road are drawn across it by hand and an
aqueduct is founded across it at an epoch", l. 19695–19697), and §5.3's law-1 row checks law 1 (10) at the epoch's
commit (l. 9950). Graded minor: a gloss contradicting the law it cites, the step's own search and named outcome deciding
the case, its wrong build failing T110-4 (c). It concerns the meeting itself, not a lock's behaviour under an undo, a
redo, a re-grade or value edit or a repair (J150).

**Required change.** V06 §3.19 (5) (V06 l. 2844–2846): "but a wall, a retaining wall and another raised way, which a
course meets as unheld ones (§2.7's laws, law 2 *Entry*; its worked example the crossing table's lock sentence:" → "but a
wall, a retaining wall and another raised way, which a course meets by §2.7's laws, a placement lock or pin deciding no
meeting and a shape lock honoured (law 2 *Entry*, law 1 (10); its worked example the crossing table's lock sentence:".
