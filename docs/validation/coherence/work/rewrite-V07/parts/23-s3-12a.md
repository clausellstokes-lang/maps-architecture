
### 3.12a A village over a former city, and refounding at village rungs (file g item 113)

The history replay is the base's (§4.11 *History-first generation*) and V06's engine (V06 §3.20); this section is its
village-rung reading. It adds no record: the remnants are the replay's own standing, ruined and buried records, read by
the village's stages; its cells are `rules.formerCity` (V07 §2.2a).

**(1) Which product runs the replay.** Every replay epoch's product and rung are V07 §2.1's fold. A peak at TOWN or above
runs the first generation on the base product at the peak's rung, and the village product takes over at the replay epoch
whose target first falls below the hand-off band — V07 §3.12's hand-off down, run as any epoch; a peak inside the village
range runs the village product throughout; a founding below 1 500 with a peak above runs the village, the hand-off up,
then the hand-off down. The replay epochs are the log's creation prefix (§7.4; never undo targets), so no hand-off inside
the replay is undone alone. A reader without the village product refuses to replay a log holding a village-rung epoch,
naming the product (§7.5), as it refuses a village creation (V07 §2.6).

**(2) The remnants at village rungs** (item 113 (a), (c); §4.11 *History-first generation* (3)):
a. **The old circuit.** At the hand-off down the derived plan's palisade is the innermost standing enceinte only when the
   area it encloses is at most `formerCity.wallAreaShare` (4) × the area of the village's perimeter at that epoch; a larger
   circuit is a **remnant wall** — never the village's palisade, standing fabric whose runs decay by its stretch-state
   profile (`Enceinte.condition`, §2.2 rule 5), never repaired by the village's fortification phase (a village gains a ring
   only by the dossier, V07 §3.12). Its breached and dismantled runs and its gates are no obstacle to the trackers (3) c.
b. **The old streets.** The streets the village no longer uses become hollow ways and effaced traces by the street row
   (V07 §3.12 *Decline and desertion*); outside the perimeter they are the field lanes and field boundaries V07 §5.1's
   `hollow_way` role draws. Nothing new is written.
c. **The old quarters.** A district of the former town in which no dwelling stands holds no village fabric: its vacant
   plots take `plotToGarden` (§4.11) as the hinterland step turns land to the households' use (V06 §3.5.3, read through
   the village's `epoch.rows.hinterland`, the `perimeterPoorest` order read outward from the village's perimeter), so the
   old town's plots are gardens and closes beside the fields; the district record stays (the village rules never re-cut a
   district, V07 §3.12).
d. **Buried remnants.** The replay's burial (V06 §3.20.4 (5); V04 §3.5.5 (b)) lays the buried former settlement under the
   fields and the village at its old ground level. At village rungs it is a ruin for V07 §3.16's access rows — a house's
   dig, a well, a sinkhole or a ruined-landmark entrance — and a void whose every open link closes is LOST until refound
   (V04 §3.5.7 (g)); the ruin marker (V07 §3.16 (6)) may stand on the old market's site.
e. **The story.** V07 §4.5's why rows read the `HistoryRecord` (§2.7; V06 §3.20.6): "these banks are the old town walls",
   "this lane follows a street of the old city", "the church is too big for the village"; under a symbolic peak "its exact
   size is not recorded" (V10).

**(3) Refounding at a village rung** (item 113 (b); V06 §3.20.4 (4)). When the refounding epoch's target lies in the
village range the fold gives the village product. The plan is created, or revived, by the hand-off down's rule from the
ruins (V07 §3.12): the heart at the old principal market's centroid, else the ruins' densest point on dry ground; the roads
from the traces of the old arterials; the form drawn from `forms × cultureForms[refound.culture]` (V07 §3.1 (2)) on
`village.plan` keyed by the epoch — a refounded village may follow a new plan — in place of the living town's `nucleated`.
The village siting reoccupies nothing (no house stands) and rebuilds on the ruins first:
a. **Foundations first.** Regrowth tier (3)'s `rebuild` and `reuse … build` (V06 §3.8) seat the refounded households on the
   ruined footprints nearest the heart, each on its old plot as a toft (S10's `toft` strategy re-cuts an old plot to the
   band's toft-and-croft depth only where its frontage allows), before (6v)–(9v) run.
b. **The old roads.** (8v)'s extension trackers treat an effaced trace within `network.lookAheadM` ahead as a merge target,
   as a laid way (V07 §3.5 step 4): the point attracted onto it, a `T` junction recorded, the trace reopened
   (`reopenWay`) in that batch; S4's router over VS1's lattice prices a trace at V11's `history.reuseCost` through
   `existingFabricCost` (§4.11 *History-first generation* (4)).
c. **Gate gaps and bridges.** A tracker whose next step crosses a remnant wall ((2) a) is steered to the nearest gate or
   breached run within `formerCity.gateSnapM` (60 m) of its heading and crosses there, else crosses straight through the
   nearest breached run; a standing run it cannot reach within that distance stops it as a wall stops a `track` (V07 §3.5
   step 3). A ruined part-0 bridge deck within `network.bridgeSnapM` of a heading is taken as a crossing site (V07 §3.5
   step 2) and repaired to `built` in that batch; a raised part is never repaired by a village (V07 §3.5a).
d. **Salvage.** Every build of the refounding credits the ruins' stone within the haul radius (§4.11 *Salvage*).
e. **Walls.** A refounded village has no palisade unless a dossier wall record gives one (V07 §3.11 DR2); the old circuit is
   a remnant wall by (2) a.
All draws are on the village's streams keyed by the epoch (V07 §3.14); with the replay off the village is byte-identical to
the build without this section (T-V7-60's no-op pin).

**(4) Edges.** A refounding target below 1 is a desertion (V07 §3.12); a destruction in the last epoch refounds there at
the present population (V06 §3.20.1 (7)) and the dossier's present records are met by that epoch's `dossier` step (V06
§3.20.7); a refounding with no ruin in the frame (a history whose town lay outside it) creates the plan by VS1's own score;
a peak above the rung table's top is sized at the cap (§4.11 (7)); a history whose founding lies after its present replays
nothing (V06 §3.20.1 (1)).
