## 2 Data model

All lengths in metres (`M`) or centimetres (`Cm`), angles in centidegrees (`Cdeg`), areas in m² or ha as named, as V07 §2.1
declares. The product lives in its own package, `@plat/village` (file f item 505), which depends on `@plat/layout` and
`@plat/plots` through their interfaces only, as `@plat/hood` does (§14), and registers its stages and strategies by
declared I/O (§4.12). Nothing in this section restates a base record; base records gain fields only through V07 §10's
requests.
