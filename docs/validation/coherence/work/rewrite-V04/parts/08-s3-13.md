
### 3.13 Edges — every mechanism at its limits (principle (11))

Every mechanism of V04 §2–§5 has a defined outcome at the limits of its inputs, a rung on §5's failure ladder (check,
repair locally, retry that piece, simpler shape, substitute, leave out and report — never a whole-map retry) and a test.
Four rules cover whole edge classes; the tables give every other case, each row naming its home.

**(a) Degenerate geometry.** Every step of this volume that commits geometry — U-NET's runs and junctions, U-SYS's
galleries and chambers, U-ACCESS's links and headings, U-FRONT's and U-CITY's blocks and plots, U-DIG's pieces, the carve,
`BuriedFormer.lay` — commits through kernel transactions: a degenerate result (zero-length, zero-area, self-touching,
collinear, coincident) is refused by the kernel (§3.1's `E_DEGENERATE` family; §5.1's general rule) and taken by the
step's failure class on its ladder (F-NET, F-VOID, F-UNDER, F-PLOT, F-SITING, F-EDIT; V04 §3.11). The cases met in this
volume's inputs: a run whose centreline simplifies below `minEdge` carries no tunnel and its served area passes to its
receiver (N6); two runs meeting at one point are one junction (N10); a gallery clipped by the frame or a chamber to less
than its row's `minM2` is not laid, counted (F-VOID); a self-touching cave path or fissure is cut at its first touch and
its remainder dropped; a link whose two nearest points coincide is laid as a `breach` of zero length at that point, and a
link whose end finds no admissible vertex is skipped (V04 §3.11); an access whose ground point is coincident with another
access of the same element is the same access (its key, V04 §2.5); a degenerate former record (a way trace shorter than
`formerMinGalleryM`, a footprint under `formerStoreyMinM2`) is not laid (V04 §3.5.5 (b)). Steps that write attributes
only (U-FORECAST, the excavation reading, T8, the opening pass, `workedOut`, `gradeFaces`) meet degenerate geometry only as
input: a zero-length run adds no length, a zero-area element no capacity and no side. The view's derivations (programs,
labels, exports) skip a degenerate mark — a zero-area chamber, a zero-length band, an empty label box draws nothing — and
never fail a tile. Test: T-V4-80 (a).

**(b) Rungs.**

| a standalone neighbourhood document | the hood line (V04 §3.1) at its `contextRung` |

**(e) Combinations — where the underground meets another unit at its limits.** Each outcome's home is named; each is a
clause of T-V4-81.

| where they meet | outcome |
|---|---|
| **a settlement that grows over a sealed void** (V06, V04) | each new building over the void draws its dig (V04 §3.5.7 (d)), each growing system tests overlap ((a)); the void is found, or stays sealed, by those draws; a lost void is refound the same way ((g) (3)) |
| **a catacomb growing into a sewer** | the catacomb's growth meets the run at a shared depth: an overlap link (`via: 'breach'`), its kind by `LinkRule.kinds` (a builders' grille reads `gated`), neither element moved (V04 §3.5.7 (a)); at different depths the catacomb node is refused (`system`, V04 §3.5.1 (4)) and the two stay apart, save the overlap shaft's chance |
| **a raised way over a harbour mouth** (the route is the base's) | a span over water is `open` (`water`, item 244), so no arch vault stands in the harbour mouth; its piers' footings are foundation obstacles only on land |
| **a deck plot on a bridge over a moat** | a deck plot has no underground part (V04 §1.3); the moat is water: no vault, no access, no catacomb node under it; a bridge vault only at a part-0 bridge's deck ends over a bank (V04 §3.5.4 (a)) |
| **a retaining wall on a cliff edge** | its footing reaches the low side's ground less `footingBelowCm` (V04 §3.5.0); a sewer crossing it drops (N7 (3)); a street vault never on its stair interval (V04 §3.5.4 (b)) |
| **an epoch undone after a save** | the epoch's commit is one event: its undo restores the underground's replaced scope, `ExcavationRecord`, vacated dwellings and places byte for byte, before or after a save (§7.4) |
| a cliff or steep ground over the sewer | N3's adverse cost and N7's falls hold; a crown deeper than `coverMaxCm` takes F-NET's ladder; a way that is a stair keeps its spine and the run copies it |
| a cliff or steep ground over a cavern | a gallery whose crown cover falls below `mouthCoverCm` at the cliff face is a `mouth` candidate (the cliff exposes the cave); fissures start at the steepest cells (V04 §3.5.2) |
| a cliff or steep ground over a ruin | the ancient ground follows the smoothed terrain; an element whose ceiling would stand within 100 cm of the present, unsmoothed ground at any vertex is created `collapsed`, cause `decay` (eroded out at the scarp) |
| a cliff or steep ground over a catacomb | the level-floor test refuses nodes beyond `depthM.max` (`depth`); the complex stops at the scarp and may stagnate |
| a cliff or steep ground over the undercity, a void generally | a cluster's floors keep their own heights; a carve stays inside its part's depth band; a laid-after-town element whose cover at any vertex falls below its row's `coverM` is not laid (catacomb `depth`, quarry cell dropped, vault not laid) |
| a cliff or steep ground under an access | an access's ground point on a `cliff` terrain cell slides to the nearest admitted point within its search (N11's 5 m steps; L1's `lifelineReachM`), else it is skipped, counted |
| a cliff or steep ground under a vault | a stair way is no street-vault stretch; a span over steep or tall ground is `open` (`slope`, `tall`) |
| the coast and an access | no surface-breaking access stands on a water face but an outfall's `mouth` and a cavern `mouth` at the bank; a candidate on water moves to the nearest land point within 10 m, else it is skipped, counted |
| a moat and a cavern, ruin or catacomb | an older element under the moat below the water table is `flooded`; a laid-after-town element never lies under a water face (the shallow-element rule) and a catacomb node there is `wet`; a moat is an outfall only last (N2) |
| a moat and an access or vault | no access on the moat's face; a bridge vault at a moat bridge's deck end over its bank as at any part-0 bridge |
| a bridge and an access | a bridge deck is never an access host (its point lies over water): the site slides along the run, else it is skipped |
| a deck plot and the sewer, a void, the undercity, an access or a vault | the ground beneath the deck hosts as usual (the way beneath is the corridor; V04 §1.2); a deck building never digs, never hosts a basement access and never a crypt join; pier footings block catacomb growth and later systems (V04 §3.5.0); L1's points never inside a pier's footprint |
| an oasis and the sewer | an oasis's pond (item 111) is never an outfall candidate — it is the settlement's water; with no other water, the moat or field outfall (N2) |
| an oasis and a void, the undercity, an access or a vault | elements under the pond below the water table are `flooded`; `damp` ground around it refuses catacomb nodes (`wet`); no access on the pond; the pond counts as water for the cistern test (`dist.water`) |
| the frame and every system | every part's L0 margin ring is the ground's frame plus its context margin: an element beyond it is clipped at the ring (a gallery cut there, a chamber clipped below `minM2` not laid); a ruin outline moves inward (V04 §3.5.0a (3)); a catacomb node beyond `reachM` or the frame is not admitted (`reach`); a forced-vault grid stops at the frame; no access point outside the frame |
| a hood document and the vaults | the arch vaults and terminal cistern of a declared raised way only (V04 §3.1) |
| V01's terraced ward and the sewer, a cavern, a ruin, the undercity, an access or a vault | its retaining walls give the wall drops (N6–N7), footing obstacles for the older voids and later systems (V04 §3.5.0), no street vault on a stair interval; a grating's site on a stair interval slides to the nearest level corridor point |
| V02's composed building and the sewer, a void, the undercity, an access | one plot, one drain need (N8); one dig draw per void kind for the building, a wing V02's mutator adds carrying its wing key (V04 §3.5.7 (d)); its basement is the building's one basement ring; a church-class composed building joins only by the crypt join |
| V02's stepped tiers and an access | the dig floor and the access head are the basement's under tier 0 (V04 §3.5.7 (d)) |
| a cavern or ruin and undo | the undo of a `regenerate`, an excavation or a closing commit restores the elements, openings, places, `ExcavationRecord` and dwellings byte for byte (V04 §4.4) |
| an arch vault and a lock | a lock or pin on an arch vault holds it: an edit whose fate would remove or collapse it (its span set `open`, its raised way deleted) is refused naming the lock (§7.3's lock row, `refuse.locked`); at an epoch the reconcile leaves a locked vault unchanged and reports the held lock |
