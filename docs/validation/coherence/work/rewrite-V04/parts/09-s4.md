
## 4 Editing

The edit kinds are §7.1's (the underground tools row of its edit-target table), their lifecycle rows §7.4's and the
closure rule §7.3 item 9's. This section adds what they need to be built: the points forms, the attribute paths with
their ranges, the locality of each, and what the clerk sees.

### 4.1 Declarations — points in, shape out (item 101 (i); item 355)

**Two reads every points form shares.** A ground point is read on ground part 0: a point on a raised deck resolves to the
ground beneath it, a click on the sky layer resolves to ground part 0 beneath it (no island building or face is ever a
host, V04 §1.2), and one inside a raised-way pier's footprint moves to the nearest free part-0 point, the move reported in
the dry run. An access from a church-class building (its class's crypt column `always` or `basement`) to a catacomb
element — by `placeAccess` in it or `addUndergroundAccess` naming a catacomb target — is a hand crypt join: source
`crypt`, cause `{ rule: 'crypt', system, event }`, its head in the crypt; on a building without `Basement.crypt` the
join is bent: the dry run adds the crypt (`setAttr(basement)` on `basement/crypt/ring`, V03 §4.2) in the same event,
then the join, naming the bend (J-V4.89; J-VI.4).

**Bends, and degenerate and empty forms (J-VI.4).** A declaration is refused only where it meets a lock or a dossier
fact (§7.3's outcome table); every other infeasible form is bent to the nearest lawful one, the dry run naming the bend in
one line: a `drawChamber` whose size falls below its row's `minM2` is enlarged to it, a self-touching polygon is replaced by
its convex hull; a `placeAccess` with no element within 10 m below it reaches the nearest element below it; an `addLink`
whose two points lie on one part takes, for its second end, the nearest element of another part within 5 m. Two coincident
points (`drawTunnel`, `addLink`) declare nothing: the dry run is empty and says why, and nothing commits. Test: T-V4-80 (a).

A dry run shows the derived shape as a ghost with its fates; accepted, the shape is stored resolved and no later
derivation moves it (declared means held, §7.2; N3a). The toolkit's layer: showing the underground layer makes it the
active layer until the user picks another (the active-layer control is V09's); while it is active the road tool T4 draws
a tunnel or a link, the place tool T6 a chamber or an access, the shape tool T9 every attribute below (V04 §1b.3) — no
thirteenth tool (§13.2's elegance bars).

### 4.4 Undo and the closure record

Every row is §7.4's; the closure record (§7.3 item 9 (c)) names U-DIG's pieces. Additions: the outfall re-seat's former
`at` is in the water edit's undo record; a depth offset's undo restores the knots of every run N7 re-laid; an
excavation's undo removes its access and restores the former reading, the `ExcavationRecord` entries it added and the
vacated or opened state by the replaced scope; the undo of a closing commit restores `accessible` and the dwellings byte
for byte; an opening place that an edit's opening pass made or tombstoned returns with the edit's undo (a place's undo is
its host's event, V01 §2.7); a catacomb delete's undo restores the galleries, `extentFloorM2` and the tombstones; a raised
way's removal's undo restores the way, its arch vaults, their doors, links and accesses with the same ids (the replaced
scope); a monument count's undo restores the former lay. **Held pieces:** L5's pieces for a subterranean dossier
institution are held while that institution is live: a `delete` of one returns F-EDIT `dossier-owned` with `rejectSite`
offered (the institution moved, its underground re-made; item 20); once the dossier removes or closes it, its pieces are
released (V04 §3.7 L5) and edit like any generated `dug` piece. Edit-made `dug` pieces are held (§7.3 item 9 (c));
generated ones re-derive with their owner (V04 §3.8). The replay's burial epochs are part of the creation prefix, never an
undo target (§7.4); a later edit on the buried former settlement's elements undoes as any.

A ruin, a cavern cluster and every unexcavated element are known to a `secret` key only (V04 §5.2); a lost cluster to

### 4.6 Migration (§7.5)

A document saved before U-NET's output version holds `Features.sewers` true and an empty part 0. On first opening under
that version the tail runs as part of the version's `upgrade` event (§7.5): the diff lists the underground records made,
declared as the release's shift of the underground layer; the ground's digests are unchanged. Such a document had no S7b
(6)–(7) allocations, so the upgrade makes no sewer-front plots, void dwellings or undercity; its diff says so and offers
**Re-derive from S7b** to make them. A stored `flowToward` is dropped (item 208); a `Chamber` without `kind` takes its
row's first kind; a `Tunnel` without `outfall` takes the nearest `Terrain.outfalls` entry to its downstream end, reported.
A document saved before item 109's version, in the same `upgrade` event, with a declared shift (§13.3):
1. Every existing catacomb complex is kept — the ratchet outranks the count — and gets a `CatacombComplex` with
   `extentFloorM2` equal to its laid extent and `fill` 0, flagged `overCount` where the count is exceeded; no complex is
   added until the count allows. A complex whose source is a church building (the old crypted-church row) is kept whole,
   re-sourced to that church plot's `churchyard` part where one exists, else `legacy` naming the church, reported; no
   complex is ever created under a church.
2. Every existing access and link keeps its record, with cause `legacy`. A dwelling or undercity in a cavern is kept where
   any existing access makes its cluster accessible; otherwise its plots vacate as the upgrade's fates, listed.
3. The access pass (U-ACCESS) runs; it adds accesses and never removes one.
4. No ruin is created (g item 109 (h)); `recipe.layers.ruins`, absent, reads `off`.

A document saved before item 110's version, in the same `upgrade` event, with a declared shift: every `vault` span of a
raised way the document holds gets its arch vault and every `cistern` terminal its terminal cistern, the diff listing
them; a ruin whose culture row names a monument gets it; the foundation reading, the wall-crossing vertices and drops and
the church-class host rule apply; and the entrances of its underground buildings are rewritten to `faces: 'tunnel'`.
Nothing is removed but what the foundation reading collapses, each listed. A document saved before item 113's version
gains an `ExcavationRecord` per covered system built from its current reading (every element of an accessible cluster
entered), so it holds no lost cluster, and no buried former settlement (the replay runs only at generation); the diff
lists the records made. Migration of a document at every rung and preset is tested by T-V4-80 (d).

### 4.7 Read-ahead — the skeleton file's underground bones (item 101; §4.1 *Declarations read ahead*; item 281)

A skeleton file, or any log whose creation prefix holds underground declarations, is read ahead by the tail stages, each
declaration by the stage that owns its subject and as a constraint of that stage:
* **U-NET** reads the prefix's `drawTunnel` without `system` as held runs (N3a (1)).
* **U-SYS** reads `drawTunnel` with a `system` and `drawChamber` as held elements of their kind; a declared element makes
  its system exist whatever its chance draws (a declaration is a fact), and the system's layout grows around it (a ruin's
  plan on the declared elements' axis, a catacomb's grid from the declared gallery's axis). A raised way the prefix
  declares (the base's) has its arch vaults and terminal cistern laid by V04 §3.5.4 (d), (e) like any.
* **U-CITY** reads a `quarter` or `drawBoundary` on a void part as a required neighbourhood, as `require` places one (V04
  §3.6 C0), in the declared cluster; on a cluster still `unexcavated` after T2b its way in is the dwelling closure's (V04
  §3.8), run at C0 before C1's lifeline test with the declaration's `seq`, never `require`'s lifeline stair of cause
  `dossier` (J-V4.120).
* **U-LINK** reads `addLink`, `placeAccess` and `addUndergroundAccess`, whose closures run at their prefix positions after
  T5, in prefix order, each folding nothing the derivation has already made (§7.4) — save an `addUndergroundAccess`
  grouped before a dwelling declaration on a sealed cluster, which runs with that declaration's closure at U-CITY C0
  (above; V04 §3.8).

A declaration a stage cannot honour is bent to the nearest lawful one, the bend named at its dry run; one contradicting a
dossier fact or a lock is refused with the reason and the closest alternatives (item 20 (g); J-VI.4);
a prefix with no underground declaration reads nothing here. The skeleton file, the canvas and the tool API give one model
digest for the same declarations (T-V4-40).
