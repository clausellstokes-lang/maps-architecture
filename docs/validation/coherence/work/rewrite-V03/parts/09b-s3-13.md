### 3.13 Edges — every mechanism at its limits (principle (11))

Every mechanism of V03 §2–§5 has a defined outcome at the limits of its inputs, a rung on F-PLAN's ladder (§5.2; check,
repair locally, retry that piece, simpler shape, substitute, leave out and report — never a whole-plan or whole-map
retry) and a test. Five rules cover whole edge classes; the tables give every other case, each row naming its home.

**(a) Degenerate geometry.** Every step that commits geometry — the seed clip, the anchors, the heads, `slice` and
`strip`, P0, P5's read-back, the crypt's ring and columns — commits through `transientArena` under kernel transactions: a
degenerate result (a zero-length cut, a zero-area face, a self-touching or collinear ring, coincident anchors) is refused
by the kernel (§3.1's `E_DEGENERATE` family; §5.1's general rule) and taken by the step's row of V03 §3.9 (the next
position or candidate, the next attempt, `bisect`, then tier 6 for the soft rooms left), never by a retry of the plan.
As input: a wing whose clipped extent on a storey is empty or narrower than `minEdge` gives that storey no piece (A5 (e)
(6) sends its targets to the other pieces; a storey left with no piece holds no room, its rooms falling by A5's rules and
reported); a wall shorter than a door (O1 (a)) takes no door and a ring side shorter than a door run is no door side
(V03 §3.7.1); collinear walls merge (P5); two heads whose `ground.at` coincide take candidates in access-id order (V03
§3.7.2 (5)); a terrace `T(x)` the stacked wings cover whole is no terrace; a room whose free floor (F2) is empty places no
item, each counted as skipped (F3). A carried or edited ring that the change makes degenerate is orphaned and reported
(§7.4's plan-event row; V03 §4.3), and an edit that would make one bends to the nearest lawful module position (V03 §4.2).
The draw and the exports skip a degenerate mark — a zero-area room or a zero-length wall segment draws nothing, writes no
Foundry wall, no `.dd2vtt` segment and no CityJSON room — and never fail a tile or a file. Test: T-V3-37 (a).

**(b) Rungs.** A plan is derived per building on a click, so no plan rule scales with the rung; what the rungs change
is which buildings and records exist:

| rung | the plan rung |
|---|---|
| THORP, HAMLET, VILLAGE (the village product) | every building resolves by its `v.` key (V03 §2.4's village rows, else its `composition.classFamily` key); no generated raised way and so no grade face; a crypt only where volume 11's column gives one (no village class is `always`), so the rank finds no principal where no `always` building stands and trims nothing; decks and heads wherever the base and volume 4 lay them |
| TOWN | the plan rung whole; no generated raised way (the base's rung gate), so a grade face only on a way an epoch carried down or an author drew |
| CITY | whole: the cathedral's crypt the principal, raised ways with their arch vaults and grade faces, the undercity's buildings below ground where volume 4 lays them (V04 §3) |
| METROPOLIS, to its population cap | whole; the crypt rank over at most §6's 60 crypts in S11's closing pass, `view.planCap` and §6's view fallbacks, the longest re-cut chain row of §6; at the cap no plan bound changes |
| a standalone neighbourhood or village document | its product's rung selects the rows above; the plan rung is the same call |

Test: T-V3-38 (a).

Test: T-V3-37 (b).

**(d) Missing, empty or contradictory data, the dossier included.** A missing table row falls to A1's next level
(reported by `programmeSource.level`); a class without a heights row takes the class-less storey rule (V03 §3.3); a
`FurnitureRow` whose glyph the role names but the catalogue lacks is skipped and counted (a table edit that makes one is
refused by `editTable`'s schema check, T-V3-1); a `Room.subLocation` naming a sub-location the building no longer holds
is dropped at the next re-derivation; a stored plan naming a household no longer housed re-cuts its unit rooms (the
kept-room test). A dossier crypt record binding to a `never` class makes it `always` (V03 §3.7.4); one binding to a deck
building meets the deck gate, which wins — no basement and no crypt on a deck — reported `W_DECK_ACCESS` noted `crypt`.
**A dossier change removing what an edit pinned**: a locked or named unit room whose household the dossier removes keeps
its ring, name and lock, loses its unit (scope `service`) and is listed in the change's fates (§7.4's plan-event row); a
declared room whose use the dossier removes stays declared (a building attribute) and is delivered as any hard room; an
edited crypt ring on a building whose dossier crypt record is removed stays the author's (V03 §3.7.4). Test: T-V3-39 (a).

**(e) Save, load, undo, redo, regenerate and migrate.** Every stored plan part persists as V03 §2.5 states; every derived
piece — the terraces (V03 §3.4a) among them — is re-derived at read after a load and after an undo or redo of the edit
that moved its inputs, never stored; every edit of V03 §4.1's table commits, saves, loads, undoes and redoes
byte-identically, and a `regenerate` redone draws on the counter it drew on first. After a load a building's state is its
stored plan's (`edited`) or what the cache restores (§2.9: `current`, `stale` or `absent`); queries and `why` log nothing,
so undo and redo never touch them, and a query after an undo answers the undone state. **An epoch undone after a save**:
the epoch's event re-cut the stored plans it touched (V06); loading the save and undoing the epoch restores each stored
plan byte for byte from the event's undo record (§7.4). A document from before a field existed takes V03 §2.5's migrate
column. Test: T-V3-39 (b).

**(f) Toggles and presets.** `exposureAllStoreys` (T-V3-34), `sunTieBreak`, the `roomNumbers` view key, the JSON and
Foundry numbering option and the glTF `interiors` option (default off: the default file and its budget unchanged) each
change only what their rule names. The `hatched` family draws the plan as `ink`'s; a parity preset changes no plan rule, the plan drawing in the
preset's family as §8.2's table maps it. With the underground layer active an
underground building's plan draws at its band's depth (V03 §3.12 (8)); with the layer that draws a building off, its plan
and switcher are hidden (view state; nothing is derived or logged). Test: T-V3-38 (b).

**(g) Where the plan meets another unit's feature at its limit.**

| combination | outcome | rung and test |
|---|---|---|
| a cliff at a building (the base's cliffs) | a cliff between an exterior wall and W2's sample point is read on the wall's side (the ledge the building stands on), never across the drop; a wall standing on the edge, with no ledge, takes no door and is no door side for `basementDoor(b)`'s rule 3, its windows kept by the exposure gate | the door bends or is not placed (reported); T-V3-40 (a) |
| steep ground | the storey run, datums and steps (V03 §3.3, O1 (c)), the walk-out by the fall (V03 §3.7.1 rule 3), entrance storeys and the uncapped stoop (V03 §3.6, O1 (e)) | `joinsRefused` and `stoop` reported; T-V3-18, T-V3-30, T-V3-40 (b) |
| the coast, a moat, a river beside a building | a sample point over open water reads the water's surface (W2): no door or entrance opens onto water, windows stand by the exposure gate; the basement is the terrain gate's (a crawl space or none on wet ground; the raised crypt for an `always` class, V03 §3.7.4) | a door bends to the nearest wall with walkable ground (V03 §4.2); T-V3-40 (c) |
| a deck plot on a bridge over a moat | `onDeck(b)`: no basement, head or well (V03 §3.7.2 (0)); a basement request bends to the nearest ground building (`bend.deck.basement`); the deck's ruin or removal takes the building (V03 §5.1) | T-V3-31, T-V3-40 (c) |
| an oasis or any high water table | the terrain gate's wet-ground rows: no full or half cellar; a `basement`-class church has no crypt (ledger slabs), an `always` class the raised crypt (V03 §3.7.4 *Wet ground*); a kitchen well heads on level 0 | T-V3-23 (k), T-V3-40 (d) |
| the arena frame (the kernel's frame and its growth) | a W2 sample point outside the arena's frame reads `levelledGroundCm` at its nearest point inside the frame; a frame's growth changes a plan only through the outside digest (§2.8); a building cut by the drawn view keeps its whole plan (the model holds whole buildings; the view clips the draw and the picture exports only) | T-V3-40 (e) |
| a retaining wall on a cliff edge | the wall's far side is read (V03 §3.6), and the cliff beyond it by the cliff row above: the walk-out's door opens only onto the lower terrace within the tolerance, else the reported `trapdoor` fallback (§2.6 step 3) | T-V3-30, T-V3-40 (a) |
| a settlement that grows over a sealed void; a catacomb growing under a church | the plan reads only `BuildingInput.accesses` and `Basement.crypt`: a sealed element's heads read `bricked` (V03 §3.7.2 (7)); a catacomb's crypt join heads only in the crypt (V03 §3.7.2 (6)); a church-class building never heads a `system` catacomb access (`W_CHURCH_CATACOMB_HEAD`) | T-V3-22, T-V3-23 (d) |

Test: T-V3-40.
