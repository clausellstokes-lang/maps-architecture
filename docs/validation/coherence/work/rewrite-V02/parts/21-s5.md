
## 5 Rendering, labels, exports

### 5.1 What each band draws (§8.5's bands, with stage 3)

| band | stage 3 draws | from |
|---|---|---|
| **city** | the massing as masses — the base's closing per block (§8.5 (2)): a courtyard building's court stays open when it is wider than the closing's 1.5 px (a court is a yard part, `ground` at this band); towers and cross-wings show in the mass's silhouette; no roof lines | model (eager) |
| **district** | footprints with outlines and entrance ticks (the base's), and the **roofscape of the massing** (V02 §5.2): ridges, hips, valleys, walls, tower roofs and spires, the cupola — drawn by the style's construction; an `arcade` wing's ground-storey outline dashed; no dormers or porches | model, drawn lazily per tile |
| **neighbourhood** | the district band's, plus — where the building's `wings` rung is current or stored — dormers, the entrance gable, the porch, and §8.10's decorative chimneys at the façade-bay lines (`hearths` of them; none once the plan rung exists, J-A28.85); the change marker on a stale building (§8.5) | model + the elaboration cache |
| **building** | the neighbourhood band's at full detail, and the plan storey by storey (V03); the other storeys' wing outlines faded beneath (the base's) | model + cache |

Under a Works (item 67) a building draws its wings at their stage heights and §8.2's `states` rows: `wallsRising` draws no
roofscape facet, `roofFramed` draws the roofscape's lines through §8.2's `frame` program without fill, a `decayed` or
`ruined` building (roof kind `none`) draws no facet — its walls by the `states` rows (a ruined landmark, V02 §3.14.2,
included). **A stack under Works** takes its stage heights from item 318's `heightsAt` — the stack rising in stack order,
a solid wing by courses of `storeyCm.upper`, no wing drawn or cast before its support reaches its eave, each stair rising
with the tier whose top it reaches, a demolition lowering in reverse (V02 §5.6); V06's Works read the same order (X23). A
building whose roof kind is `none` takes no dormer, porch or cupola drawing. Condition (`worn`, `neglected`) and material
stay texture and colour on the same facets (item 19).

### 5.2 The roofscape — one roof plan per building (item 406)

`roofscape(building, composition?)` returns planar, pairwise non-overlapping facets that together cover the footprint
(and a porch outside it). It is derived at read in the tile worker (drawing, float64, the renderer's CPU reference) and
in the generation worker (exports); it stores nothing and draws nothing from any stream.

1. **Ends (`resolveEnds`).** Each wing with a ridge has two **ends** — the ring sides its ridge meets (sides 1 and 3 for
   `ridgeAxis 0`, 2 and 4 for 1). Each end is classed, in this order: `open` if a tower ring covers it; `party` if it lies
   on a party-wall edge of the plot (a row quad's side); `mitre` if it is shared vertex for vertex with another wing's
   end; if it lies along another wing `Y`'s side (a butt joint): `wall` when `Y.eaveCm ≥ X.ridgeCm − 10`, `gable` when
   `X.ridgeCm > Y.ridgeCm + 10` (the end wall rises above `Y`'s roof), else `valley`; a free end is `hip` on a `hip`
   roof and `gable` on every other kind. A block's side partly shared with a piece's end is classed on the shared part.
2. **Ridge lines.** The base's ridge (for a `rect`, the midpoints of its ends; for a `quad`, the arc-length midpoints of
   the chains its `ridgeAxis` names, §2.6), with each end adjusted: `hip` pulls the ridge end inward along the ridge by
   `WingRoleRow.hipInset × span` (0.5 — the plan hip at equal pitches — for ranges; a block's `FM` entry, V02 §3.10.8's
   measured-rate cell, 0.25 until calibrated, keeps a ridge half its short side long, never a pyramid; the hip facets' own
   pitch then follows from the inset and the rise), collapsing to the centre only when the wing is no longer than
   `2 × hipInset × span`; `valley` extends it into `Y` by `valleyReach(X, Y, 'float')` (V02 §3.7); `gable`, `party`,
   `mitre`, `wall` and `open` leave it at the end's midpoint (a mitre end's midpoint is the path vertex).
3. **Facets per wing.** `gable` / `hip` roofs: the two long facets from each eave to the ridge, a hip triangle at each
   `hip` end; a `valley` end's facets continue into `Y` up to the **valley lines** — the plan projections of the lines
   where `X`'s facet planes meet `Y`'s near facet plane, clipped to `Y`'s footprint (where `X`'s eave is below `Y`'s, the
   valley starts inside `Y` on the line where `X`'s facet reaches `Y`'s eave height). `shed`: one facet from the low eave
   to the `high` side. `pyramid` and `cone`: one triangle per ring edge to the apex over the ring's centroid. **Pent band
   facets** (J-V2.75): for a wing `x` with a pent roof and the wing `y` standing on it — their rings concentric with equal
   insets and the same vertex count (ST3, ST4) — the facets are the band between the two rings: for each side `i` of `x`'s
   ring one planar facet from that side at `x.eaveCm` to the matching side `i` of `y`'s ring at `x.ridgeCm` (two parallel
   edges, so the facet is planar), neighbouring facets sharing the edge from `x`'s vertex to `y`'s matching vertex as
   their hip line; a round or octagonal ring is treated per edge (a cone's band); nothing is built inside `y`'s ring. So
   `ridgeCm = eaveCm + p × d` is exactly the height at which the skirt meets the wall of the tier above, and the casters and
   the glTF and CityJSON roofs read the same facets. A pent-kind roof on a wing with nothing standing on it (a truncated
   stack's former lower tier) is laid as the top's roof instead (ST4). `tent`: as a cone at its pitch; a spire is a
   `cone` or `pyramid` at its spire pitch. `flat`: one facet at eave height, with an inner parapet line where
   `parapetCm > 0`. `dome` (a template part's roof): four latitude bands of planar quads over the ring's area centroid
   (triangles at the apex), rise = the ring's mean radius × `domeRise` (1.0; own), each band's ring the base ring scaled
   toward the centroid by `cos` of its latitude (15°, 37.5°, 60°, 82.5° by `dmath`). `plain` (the F-SKELETON downgrade):
   one flat facet at eave height with no lines. `none` (the decline's roofless state): no facet — the wing is excluded
   from the tiling check below and drawn by §8.2's `states` rows. `skeleton` (a drawn footprint, a template polygon): the
   base's validated straight-skeleton faces (§3.8), with its plain-roof downgrade. **The cupola** (M12): a small solid at
   its range's ridge midpoint — a square plan of side `0.3 ×` the range's width, a drum of `0.25 ×` that side, and a
   `dome` (as above), a `tip` (a cone of rise 2 × its half side) or a `pyramid` — standing on the ridge; its facets are
   extra (`part: 'cupola'`), painted over the roof, never in `Heights`. Every facet vertex carries its height `z` (cm above
   the building's lowest `baseCm`), so each facet has an exact plane and normal.
   **Chimneys** (both kinds; §8.6, §8.10). A decorative chimney (no plan) at its host point of V02 §3.7, on its host's
   facet. A **plan chimney** (§2.6's `Chimney`, with a plan) is drawn on the roof or terrace facet over `Chimney.column` of
   the wing that holds storey `Chimney.levels[1]` and whose ring contains the column — so a lower tier's stack carried up
   through the tiers above (V03) rises from the top of the stack, never from a roof the tier above covers; a chimney whose
   column lies inside the ring of a wing standing on that wing (a stored plan not yet re-cut) is not drawn and is reported
   (`chimneyCovered`) until the plan re-derives its levels (V03).
4. **Occlusion, so facets never overlap.** Each valley region (the part of `X`'s extended facets inside `Y`) is removed
   from `Y`'s facets; each tower ring is removed from every other facet (a tower always covers what it overlaps —
   V02 §3.5 M11 guarantees its eave is above every roof it meets); each ring of a wing that stands on another is removed
   from the facets of the wing it stands on (V02 §5.6); a dormer's plan rectangle is removed from its host facet and
   replaced by the dormer's own facets (a `dormer` or `wallGable`: two facets from its front edge to a short ridge
   perpendicular to the host's, `depthShare` up the slope; a `hoist`: the same with a flat front; the `entranceGable`:
   two facets whose ridge runs to the host's ridge); the porches, outside the footprint, add their own facets (a hood: one
   thin facet; `gabled`, `twoStorey`: two facets with a ridge square to the wall; `halfRound`: the half-cone's triangles,
   one per arc segment, to the apex on the wall line; `recessed`: none — a notch line in the eave). The result tiles
   footprint ∪ porches, less the wings roofed `none` (T-V2-9); the cupola's facets lie over it and are excluded from the
   tiling.
5. **Lines.** Ridges, hips (the hip facets' shared edges and a mitre on its convex side), valleys (valley lines and a
   mitre on its concave side), eaves (the footprint outline, the base's), a `wall` end's line, tower seams (every other
   ring vertex's edge to the apex for a cone with 16 or more sides, every edge for a pyramid).
6. **Order.** Within a building: porch facets, then wing facets, then dormer facets, then tower facets, then all lines —
   the same painter's order the reference uses (porch under the eave, the tall parts last), which with non-overlapping
   facets only decides the order of anti-aliased edges. Wing facets go in stack order — a wing's before those of every
   wing standing on it.

The **surface layer** of §8.4 (a) takes its roofs from these facets (the "roof construction" it names), so the light
pass lights the true roof planes and the tower cones. **The shadow casters are the base wings' prisms** (V02 §3.14.1):
each wing's ring from `baseCm` to its eave, and its roof's rise over its ring (a tower's cone or spire only over its own
ring) — model heights, so **no shadow of the model's masses depends on a click** (J-V2.22); they are also item 102's epoch
casters, and never a generation-time caster. The surface-layer shading of the porches, the dormers and the cupola
(rungs 1–2 and the roof ornament) appears with their drawing — view only, in no digest and no caster.

### 5.3 Style rows and paint programs

`StyleSheet.roofConstruction`'s `'roofscape'` (§8.2; item 406) is the default construction of every shipped family from
stage 3's landing; the older constructions remain for the parity presets (under `MFCG` and `FTG` the massing is the
base's, and their sheets keep their constructions). Paint programs added to §8.2's list (each numeric parameter is a cell
of the program's entry in the sheet's `programs`, with its value in the table below):

* `roofscape(tone: 'twoTone' | 'mono' | 'facet')` — fills every facet in the `roof` role: `twoTone` by `faceLight` per
  facet (`lit`), `mono` one tone with the light pass's shading (`ink`, whose light is value-only, §8.4), `facet` the
  per-facet diffuse-and-specular shading of the reference look (the `FM` preset's building band; §8.4 (h), P24); then the
  lines of V02 §5.2 step 5 in `ink` at `ridgeWeight` (ridges and seams) and `hipWeight` (hips and valleys).
* `dormer` and `porch` — their facets through the same tone rule, outlined at `partWeight`; `towerRoof` — a cone's facets
  with the seam lines of step 5, a pyramid's with its hips, a spire's the same at its pitch; the cupola through
  `towerRoof` at `partWeight`.
* **Roof decorations** (§8.10 rows, view data, keyed by the render key): `sails` — a windmill's four sails on its tower's
  roof, turned to the settlement's prevailing wind where the site states one, else to `+y`; `smokeStack` — V02 §3.14.6's
  added decorative stacks; and, **only in the `FM` building look**, `stepStones` (a stone path from the main door to the
  frame's access side, stones `stoneM` at `stonePitchM`) and `wallGrass` (grass tufts at re-entrant corners); with the
  `FM` look's per-building roof hue (`weathering` on hue at `fmHueW`, §8.2) and cone facets doubled for drawing only.
* **V01's NG draw-style bars** (the `hatched` family's two tapers per wing, §13.5): the NG parity sheet's roof
  construction is V01 §5.1's sentence (X14).
* `hatch(ridge | eave, pitch, taper)` (§8.2): the `hatched` family's hatch program is V01's (V01 §5.1.2), run per
  roofscape facet; the `FM` look hatches parallel to the eave at the paper pitch `fmHatchPx`.
* The `lit` family's roof tint by band (§8.2, item 19) and the material's `pattern` (texture) apply per facet, never
  changing a facet (J-A19.42).
* The **contrast matrix** is unchanged: the roof role's pairs are the building role's (§8.2).

| cell (program) | value (own) | read by |
|---|---|---|
| `ridgeWeight` (`roofscape`, `towerRoof` seams) | ½ of the band's building outline weight | the line pass of step 5 |
| `hipWeight` (`roofscape`) | ⅓ of the band's building outline weight | hips and valleys |
| `partWeight` (`dormer`, `porch`, the cupola's `towerRoof`) | ½ of the band's building outline weight | their outlines |
| `stoneM`, `stonePitchM` (`stepStones`, §8.10) | stones 0.5–0.8 m, at 1 m pitch | the `FM` look's path |
| `fmHueW` (the `FM` look's roof hue) | 0.15 | §8.2's `weathering` on hue |
| `fmHatchPx` (`hatch`, the `FM` look) | 0.9 px at 1× | the `FM` hatch |
| `treadWeight`, `treadStopShare`, `treadMinPx` (`stairFlight`, V02 §5.6) | ⅓ of the band's building outline weight; 0.1 of the flight's width at each side; 2 px of plan per tread | the tread lines |

### 5.4 Labels

Stage 3 adds **no** `LabelTable` row and no S13b candidate: a building keeps the base's labels (a landmark's point label
at its footprint, an address). The room labels are the base's (§8.5, from the plan rung). Hovering a wing at the
district band and finer names it — its role in our own words from the naming slice **`building.wingRole`** (one entry per
`WingRole`, authored in V11's naming tables, X6: "the main range", "the gate range", "a cross-wing", "a pavilion", "a rear
range", "a service range", "a tower", "a stair turret", "a part", "a shelter"; the role id is the fallback) and the
building's name — through the reader (§11.2), under the building's `knownTo` (§9.2); nothing here is concealed. An
`ovalCourt`'s solid ranges hover as "the seating" and its `gate` passage wings as "a gate passage" (two entries of the
slice, keyed by the family and the wing's `solid` flag; X6).

### 5.6 Stacks, stairs and deck buildings on the map

* **Bands.** City band: the footprint (tier 0 ∪ stair 0, and any ground wings added beside it) as a mass like any
  other. District band and finer: the roofscape with the stack's occlusion and order (V02 §5.2 steps 4 and 6) — terraces
  as flat facets with their parapet lines, pent skirts as their band facets meeting the wall of the tier above (V02 §5.2
  step 3), the top's roof or the shrine's, each stair's shed facet painted by `stairFlight`; a sealed stack (`none`)
  draws no stair; chimneys only on exposed roof (V02 §3.7, V02 §5.2). An `ovalCourt` draws its façade ring's flat roofs with
  their parapet lines and its seating ring's shed facets falling toward the floor, which is drawn as a court (a yard
  part). Neighbourhood and building bands: V02 §5.1's, the plan per storeyed tier (V03).
* **`stairFlight`** (a paint program added to §8.2's list with item 406's): the stair's facet filled by the roofscape's
  tone rule (its slope lit by `faceLight` like any facet), with tread lines across the flight, perpendicular to its
  climb, by `stairTreads` (V02 §2.6): `n − 1` lines at `goingCm` intervals from the foot, in `ink` at `treadWeight`,
  stopping short of each side by `treadStopShare` of the flight's width; below `treadMinPx` of plan per tread the lines
  are omitted and the shaded facet reads as a ramp.
* **The surface layer and the shadows.** Each wing's facets enter §8.4's surface layer at their true heights (a terrace
  at its deck level, a stair's slope), so the light pass lights every terrace and step face; the casters are the wings'
  prisms from their own `baseCm` (V02 §3.15.3), kept current on every re-basing path by `rebaseStack` (V02 §3.15.2), so
  each tier's shadow falls on the terrace or the ground below it; under a Works, the stage heights of item 318.
* **The per-wing constructions** (the parity presets' sheets, and the base outline builder's drawing from M3a until the
  roofscape lands at M6a): a wing that stands on another is drawn after it, its ring clipping the lower wing's roof, and a
  stair's shed with its treads (item 419).
* **Labels.** No new `LabelTable` row. Hovering names the roles from `building.wingRole` (X6, with "a tier", "the
  shrine", "a stair"). A deck building keeps the base's labels.
* **Exports** (V02 §5.5's rows, extended): **JSON** — each wing's `on` and `solid`, the massing block's `stack`, and for a
  deck building a derived `deck` block (`kind`, `host`) read from its plot at export; **glTF / OBJ** — each wing extruded
  from its own `baseCm` (a tier stands on the tier below at its deck level), a solid tier as a closed prism, each stair as
  a stepped solid of `stairTreads`' `n` risers (the k-th top at `⌊k × rise ÷ n⌋`) and treads of `goingCm`, the head tread
  taking the remainder; **CityJSON 2.0** — tier 0 is the `Building`, each tier above and the shrine a `BuildingPart` with
  LoD2.2 surfaces (a terrace a `RoofSurface`, the walls `WallSurface`s from the part's own base), each stair a
  `BuildingInstallation` (an outer stair, stepped by `stairTreads`) with `plat:role 'stair'` (J-V2.77); **GeoJSON**'s
  `wings` layer — `on`, `solid` and `baseCm` per wing; **VTT** — the ground scene's walls from the footprint (the base's),
  a stair's outer sides not walls. A deck building exports as any building, its `baseCm` the deck's.
