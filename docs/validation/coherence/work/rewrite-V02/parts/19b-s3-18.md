
### 3.18 Edges — every mechanism at its limits (principle (11))

Every mechanism of V02 §2 – V02 §5 has a defined outcome at the limits of its inputs, a rung on its failure ladder (F-MASSING
and F-COMPOSE, V02 §3.13; §5's rungs: check, repair locally, retry that piece, simpler shape, substitute, leave out and
report) — never more than the building, never a whole-map retry — and a test. Four rules cover whole edge classes; the
tables give every other case, each row naming its home.

**(a) Degenerate geometry.** Every ring the massing, a mutation or an edit lays is snapped to the lattice and checked by
M13: an edge shorter than `minEdge` (§2.2 `LatticeSpec`) merges its end into its start, so no zero-length or collinear
edge survives; a ring that is not simple and counter-clockwise (zero area, self-touching), a wing below 4 m², and two
non-tower wings that overlap or coincide fail M13's checks, each an M14 attempt (retry that piece, then a simpler
shape). The slot hint `H` is a committed face of the plot's arena (§2.5 `SlotPlan.hint`), so a degenerate `H` never
reaches `outline()`; where `H` is narrower than a lattice step in one direction, `lir` and `obb` return `E_DEGENERATE`
`collinear` (§3.4), every family fails M3's geometry test and M14 takes rung 2, then rung 3 (`baseOutline`), and where the
base outline builder too finds no rectangle, S11's F-SITEPLAN ladder (§5.2) leaves the slot vacant, typed yard, reported.
The composer adds no ring it does not test: `facadeBays` returns no bay on a side whose run is not positive (V02 §3.7),
so no dormer stands there; a porch whose side is shorter than its width plus 0.6 m takes F-COMPOSE (2); a room ring the
seed contract would refuse takes F-COMPOSE (3). The roofscape drops a facet of zero area (never drawn, cast or
exported); a `valley` end whose `valleyReach` is not positive is classed `wall` by V02 §5.2 step 1's order (`Y.eaveCm ≥
X.ridgeCm − 10` holds), so no inverted valley exists. An edit whose points coincide bends (below, V02 §4.2). Tests:
T-V2-5, T-V2-9, T-V2-17, T-V2-18, T-V2-54 (a).

**(b) Rungs.** The massing runs wherever S11 raises a building, from THORP to the METROPOLIS cap. At THORP, HAMLET and
VILLAGE the village classes compose through their `v.` keys (`composition.classFamily`, V02 §3.2 step 2 (3); a town class
there plays its `villageForm`'s `v.` class, V11 §3.22); a stepped building arises at a village rung only by V11's region
and era specialisations under V07's `rules.tiers.capPerSettlement` (X26), none by default; deck plots exist only where
the base offers them (item 110 (d)), raised ways are generated only at CITY and METROPOLIS and declared at TOWN (item
110 (b)), and catacombs, sky islands and the underground layer arrive only where their owners' rungs admit them (V04,
V05). At the METROPOLIS cap the budgets of V02 §6 hold with their fallbacks, and a village rung's few buildings meet the
same bars. Tests: T-V2-29, T-V2-33 (every rung's classes), T-V2-22 (every rung), T-V2-54 (g).

**(c) Toggles and presets.** `fantasyShapes` off (the default) and on (V02 §3.3); `massing.composed` off under `MFCG`
and `FTG` (T-V2-26); the `FM` preset and its building look (T-V2-31, V02 §5.3); `sunBias` at its parity-neutral 0.5
(T-V2-37); item 110's off forms (T-V2-50); the `elaborate` modes `click` and `view` (V02 §3.11); every style family's
`roofConstruction` — `plain`, `skeletonHip`, `skeletonGable`, `fakeGablePerWing`, `roofscape` (V02 §5.3; T-V2-9 on all
constructions); the export option `compositions` (`asDrawn`, `derive`, `none`; T-V2-24); the underground layer hidden (its
default) — its buildings keep their model and are not drawn, and the exports write them by §11.1's *Layers* rule,
whatever the view shows.
A table override reaches a building only through the slices it read (V02 §3.11's `wingsInputs`), so an edited cell
re-derives exactly the buildings citing it (§7.6). Tests: the ones named, T-V2-54 (f).

**(d) Save, load, undo, redo, regenerate, migrate.** Every persisted field's cells are V02 §2.5's and both edit kinds'
§7.4's (V02 §4.4); every derived piece (`roofscape`, `facadeBays`, `stairTreads`, `storeyRange`, `deckLevel`,
`stairChain`, `landingSide`, the approach strip) is recomputed at read and never stored, so none goes stale in a save;
the elaboration cache is non-canonical and dropped on mismatch (§2.9), an unstored composition re-deriving at the next
click; a document without stage-3 fields loads by V02 §2.5's fallback migration row; a bent op replays from a skeleton
file to the same bend. Tests: T-V2-6, T-V2-14, T-V2-20, T-V2-24, T-V2-34, T-V2-48, T-V2-54 (h).
