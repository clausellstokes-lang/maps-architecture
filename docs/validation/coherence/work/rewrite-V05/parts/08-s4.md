
## 4 Editing

### 4.1 The declarations — item 101's twelve tools, no thirteenth

Every island edit is an existing tool with at most one new parameter (§7.1's elegance rule):

| tool | on the sky | the events it emits |
|---|---|---|
| T1 `intend` | the islands setting (off / on) and the count (`auto`, 0–3) | `adjust('layers.islands')`, `adjust('islands.count')` — both Re-derive from S7b with their fate report (§7.8) |
| T2 `ground` | an island's terrain, water and rim, with the `key` parameter naming the island; a terrace stroke on the island, whose edge lays an island `RetainingWall` (V05 §3.22 (4)) | `setField(terrain)`, `drawWater`, `moveCourseKnot` on its river, `warp` on its key (`fromLevel 0` moves the rim); item 110 (a)'s terrace form with `key` |
| T3 `wall` | a wall on an island, with `key`: in an enclosure kind a barrier-closure enceinte walling only the landing yards (V05 §3.21); in the `retaining` kind an island `RetainingWall`, bent inward past the rim walk when drawn on or along the rim (V05 §3.22 (4)) | `drawWall` with `key` (its kind item 110 (a)'s) |
| T4 `road` | a stroke from a ground point to an island's rim; with `raised`, bent into an access; with the aqueduct class, ended at its last ground vertex outside the footprint; on an island's key, `raised` dropped and the aqueduct class made a fountain pin (J-VI.4 (d)–(e)) | `placeSkyAccess` (the kind from the stroke's class, or chosen by the dry run by V05 §3.10's rules); a bend emits the bent declaration |
| T5 `quarter` | a floating quarter: the `sky` parameter | `quarter` with `sky` — a sugar event expanding to `convert raiseIsland` (declared), an `assign` of the kind to its district and `setAttr(name)` |
| T6 `place` | an institution onto an island | `assign` with `criteria.sky` (and optionally the island) |
| T8 `fill` | the prosperity, density and greenery brushes on an island, with `key` | `setField` with `key` — stroke overrides on the island's lattice (§7.2) |
| T9 `shape` | the island's altitude, area, position, occlusion, underside and field (a target mean); an access's kind, role, state, knowledge and foot; the rim's handles | `setAttr`; `moveVertex` on rim vertices |
| T10 `hold` | the island or its paths; New random on the island; **Update shade now**; the delete of an island or an access (volume 9's delete row) | `lock`, `pin`, `revert`, `regenerate` with the island's scope; `regenerate` with the scope `shade` and the island; `delete` (folded as `convert removeIsland` on an island) |
| T11 `name` | the island's name and label | `setAttr(name)`, `moveLabel`, `hideLabel` |
| T12 `dress` | the sky layer's visibility and occlusion | `setLayer('sky', …)` |
| history | the island conversions by hand | `convert` (`resizeIsland`, `waneIsland`, `settleIsland`, `fallIsland`, `refloatIsland`, `raiseIsland`, `removeIsland`); `reclearIsland` is the epoch's own and has no tool |

Every declared thing is held (§7.2): a declared island's outline and position, a declared altitude or area, a placed
access.

**No tool for the shade or the route.** The strips, the sky receivers' shade and the route distance are derived and carry
no edit kind. §4.5's `w_sun` and this volume's `shade`, `routing` and `casterAllowanceM` cells are table values
(`editTable`, §7.6, re-deriving from their first reader stage — SKY for `casterAllowanceM`, S10a for `shade`, S10b for
`routing`); T8's prosperity brush (`setField`) overrides the field beneath an island as it does anywhere (item 102 (f));
and **Update shade now** (V05 §4.2) brings the ground under one island's old and new strips up to date at once.

### 4.2 The events and their semantics

The events are §7.1's — `placeSkyAccess`, `quarter`'s `sky` parameter (its points world points: the island's projected
outline or seed), the `key` parameter of the point-form events (`drawWay`, `drawWater`, `drawGreen`, `drawBoundary`,
`drawFootprint`, `drawWall`, `placeLight`, `setField`; their points in that arena's frame, the tool converting a world
point by the island's anchor at the dry run, so a later move never invalidates the event) and `regenerate`'s scope
`{ kind: 'shade', island }` — with §4.11's `ConversionOp` island members (`resizeIsland`, `waneIsland`, `settleIsland`,
`fallIsland`, `refloatIsland`, `raiseIsland`, `removeIsland`, `reclearIsland`); their lifecycle rows are §7.4's. Their
semantics:

* **Pointing at an island.** Every sky hit test and every tool point on the sky layer converts the pointer's world point
  to the island's frame by the anchor — and, under `offset` occlusion, first un-displaces it (the world point less the
  island's `elevation × offsetPerMetre` offset vector, §8.4) — so a click on a lifted island's drawn house or retaining
  wall selects it and a stroke drawn over the lifted island lands where it is drawn (volume 9's hit testing, V09 §5.4).
* **Moving an island** (`setAttr(anchor)`, T9's drag; J-V5.20). The arena is untouched. The dry run re-reads
  `groundRefCm` and the altitude floor at the new position, re-checks V05 §3.7's gap and frame, and re-lands every access:
  a vertical access's foot follows its top's new world point; a slanted one keeps its foot while it stays within the
  row's run, else is re-sited by V05 §3.10 (4). A foot that leaves its landing plot closes that plot's `sky_landing` use
  (item 17's `closeUse`, the house left vacant) and opens one on the plot under the new foot by a positive placement
  (§7.7's `placeAt`). The fates list both plots. `placement.byAssignment` becomes true.
* **Resizing** (`setAttr(areaHa)` or `resizeIsland`; J-V5.7). The rim is eroded or dilated by the kernel's buffer (§3.7)
  on the island's arena to the new area within 1 % (bisection on the distance, ≤ 12 steps), keeping its shape — never
  re-drawn from the stream; a growth past 1.5 × `areaHaMax` first grows the frame (V05 §3.8 (1)). The tiers re-scale
  (`depthCm` by the square-root ratio of the areas). **The rim's L1 pieces follow the rim in the same commit** (J-V5.77).
  One island L1 batch re-lays the rim strip `rimStripM` inside the new rim and re-claims each landing yard at its top, the
  top re-seated where its direction ray `d_k` now leaves the land face (V05 §3.10 (2), (5)) and its foot re-landed as a
  move re-lands it. Each re-claimed yard is served in the same commit: its `sky_landing_top` plot re-cut on the new yard
  (the old one tombstoned) and its use opened there by a positive placement (`placeAt`), the plots the yard covers
  re-derived by §7.3's local operator on the island's arena, and SKY-F's checks and repairs (V05 §3.12) run on the
  island, so a way meets every attach. The institution claim is kept where it still fits the land face less the re-laid
  strip and yards; else it is re-searched by V05 §3.10 (5)'s score and crypt re-test over the sites inside the district
  that holds it — so every district keeps its kind — and that district is re-derived by its scoped re-derivation from S8
  with the new claim a hole and the old claim's land its own (V01 §3.10.3 (6) (c)'s claim re-search, which releases the
  institution from V01 §3.9's fixed inputs), whose S10b places the institution on the new claim: its use relocated there
  (a dossier-backed one by the solver's `dossier` request, V05 §4.4), its old building demolished, a listed fate. That
  district is re-derived whether or not it receives a band face, and once where it does. **A resize never leaves the claim
  without a site:** the area is floored at the least area (the same bisection) at which some site in that district still
  fits; a hand request below the floor is bent to it (`bend.island.claimFloor`), and an epoch's `resizeIsland` (V05 §3.15
  (2)–(3)) stops at it, listed in the epoch's report. **Shrinking:** every plot that loses at least half its face to the
  eroded rim, the re-laid strip or a re-claimed yard is cleared (its households relocated on the island first, then to
  the ground), the rest re-cut with their neighbours fixed (§4.7 S10 (7)); every way that crosses the re-laid strip line
  is cut back to it (the strip is never cut); no district is re-derived but the claim's where the claim is re-searched;
  and volume 1's `plantRimWalk` (V01 §3.10.3 step 4) re-plants the rim walk on the re-laid strip in the same commit.
  **Growing:** the land between the old and the new strip lines — with whatever an old yard held that its re-claim does
  not, less the re-claimed yards — is cut into faces by extending, in the same L1 batch, each district boundary way that
  meets the old strip line across the band to the new one along the band's normal there (the same class); each face
  joins the district of its nearest rim stretch (ties by district address), and every district that receives a face is
  re-derived by its own scoped re-derivation from S8 (V01 §3.10.3 (6) (c)) — never a unit run, which would re-cut every
  district, and never a change of the island's district count; `plantRimWalk` then re-plants the rim walk whole, once
  for the resize, since the walk is one island-level pair that no district's re-derivation writes.
* **Altitude** (`setAttr(altitudeCm)`): the record alone; a value below V05 §3.9's floor is bent to the floor
  (`bend.island.altitude`).
* **Underside and occlusion** (`setAttr(underside.*)`, `setAttr(occlusion)`): the record alone (`underside.source` becomes
  `edit`); an underside change that would break the floor raises the altitude, listed as a fate.
* **Field** (`setAttr(field.prosperity)` — a declared target mean, `declared.field`: the gradient shifted uniformly to
  meet it, V05 §3.11 (5); `setAttr(field.density)`, `setAttr(field.greenery)`; and the island brushes, `setField` with
  `key` and a prosperity, density or greenery stroke, as §7.2's stroke overrides on the island's lattice over the
  gradient): the island's plots whose value crosses a cut past τ re-derive (§7.2's rule for a field brush), on the island
  only.
* **Reshaping the rim** (`moveVertex` on a rim vertex, `warp` on the island's key with `fromLevel 0`): §7.3's local
  operator on the island's arena moves the rim, and the rim's L1 pieces follow it as a resize's do (above; J-V5.77) — the
  strip re-laid inside the new rim, each landing yard on a moved stretch re-claimed at its top (a pinned `sky_access`
  vertex never moves), the institution claim kept or re-searched inside its district; a reshape that would leave the claim
  no site there is bent back along each moved vertex's drag to the furthest position that keeps one (bisection, ≤ 12
  steps; `bend.island.rimStop`). Each stretch that moved outward is a growing resize's band — its faces, cut at the
  extended district boundary ways, joining the district of their nearest rim stretch (ties by district address), every
  district that receives a face re-derived by its own scoped re-derivation from S8 — and each that moved inward a
  shrinking resize's, which gives no face and re-derives no district but the claim's, where the claim is re-searched (V01
  §3.10.3 (6) (c)); `plantRimWalk` then re-plants the rim walk whole, once for the reshape; `areaHa` is rewritten to the
  new land area.
* **New random on the island** (`regenerate` with the island's scope, `newRandom`): the outline's displacement, the
  terrain (with an `own` relief's class), the underside (unless edited) and the river re-derived under the next reroll,
  SKY's island steps re-run (the rim strip, the landing yards and the institution claim re-laid, V05 §3.10 (5)), the
  island's districts re-laid by volume 1's unit run (V01 §3.10.3 (6) (a)–(b); each district's template kept where its
  lineage is, V01 §3.3.4), then SKY-F (V05 §3.12), which rewrites `SkyIsland.districts`; the anchor, the area, the
  altitude, the allocation and every access's foot kept; each vertical access's top re-seated at the new rim's point
  nearest above its foot, and where the new rim no longer overhangs it the access becomes a `ropeway` from the rim point
  nearest the foot. The altitude floor (V05 §3.9 (4)) is re-checked after the underside is re-drawn: a deeper root that
  breaks it raises `elevationCm` to the floor (a listed fate). The ground is byte-identical (the seam exception is
  empty); `shadeBasis` is untouched.
* **Declaring an island** (T5 with `sky`). At its dry run: the gate (V05 §3.2 (1)) must hold — where the rung admits no
  island or the setting is off, the declaration is bent to a ground quarter of its kind (`bend.island.gate`; a dossier
  whose `site.magic` resolves `none` refuses it, V05 §4.4) — and a declaration where `h_town` = 0 is allowed only with the
  fate `held against its supply` confirmed (J-V5.10); the points form gives the projected outline (a lasso), a disc of the
  kind's area at one point, or the routine fitted to two to four corners; the slot is the lowest free (the lowest held
  slot when none is free, released by this event, V05 §3.2 (6)); the fill is V05 §3.5's, taken by **relocating** the
  richest households from the ground (never re-deriving it) — each household moved up is a relocation, its ground
  building left vacant — all listed as fates; V05 §3.8–§3.14 then run on the new island, which has no `shadeBasis` until
  the next shade evaluation: it casts and receives no strip meanwhile, and Update shade now on it writes one at once. In
  the creation prefix the declaration is read ahead by S7b (5) as a forced slot (V05 §3.2 (3)), so no relocation is
  needed.
* **Placing an institution on an island** (T6: `assign` with `criteria.sky`): the solver places it on the island named,
  else on the island whose reason is `institution` and whose row matches, else the dry run offers T5's declaration. A
  custom kind is named by its `localUid` (item 104 (a) (1): the map-side declaration, stored in the map document, never
  in the dossier); where it is a resource or processing node of an active chain, the dry run lists the fate "this chain
  now crosses the hoist" and the route lengths of its links before and after (V05 §3.20 (9) (e)).
* **Accesses.** `placeSkyAccess`'s dry run checks the kind against the `magic` circumstance and the row's limits (a kind
  that does not fit is bent to the nearest admitted kind in V05 §3.10's order, `bend.access.kind`), inserts the top vertex
  on the rim, claims the island's landing yard (the plots it covers re-derived by §7.3's local operator on the island's
  arena, V01 §3.10.3 (6) (c)) and opens the ground landing by a positive placement on the plot under the foot. `delete`
  of an access reverses all three; deleting an island's last access is bent into a move to the next admissible site
  (`bend.access.last`). **A deleted generated access stays deleted**: the delete's fold appends its ordinal to
  `SkyIsland.removedAccesses`, which every later re-siting skips and counts (V05 §3.10 (1)); where it was the `main`
  access, the lowest surviving generated ordinal takes `main` in the same event (a listed fate), so an island always has
  a main access while it has any; the delete's undo removes the ordinal. A placed access's delete leaves no ordinal (it
  was declared, and nothing re-sites it). The foot of a placed or moved access meets the standing raised parts by V05
  §3.22 (3) (a)–(b) at its dry run, a foot they block moving to the nearest admissible foot (`bend.access.foot`).
  **`setAttr(kind)`** on an access re-runs the same check at its dry run — the `magic` circumstance, the row's altitude
  and horizontal-run limits, a `guarded` access a `hoist` only — a kind that does not fit bent to the nearest admitted
  kind (`bend.access.kind`); an admitted change re-seats nothing but the landing buildings (the top's and the foot's use
  rows take the new kind's building). **`setAttr(ground.at)`** re-lands the access as a move does: the old landing plot's
  `sky_landing` use closed, a new one opened by a positive placement on the plot under the new foot, both plots listed as
  fates, the horizontal run re-checked. An island wall's circuit follows every access change (V05 §3.21 (3)).
* **The shade beneath follows** (item 102 (e); J-V5.27). Every event that changes what an island's strip would read — a
  move, an altitude, a resize, a rim reshape, an underside, New random on the island, a declaration, a delete, a
  `convert` — leaves `shadeBasis`, the ground's field and every strip where they were: its dry run lists the fate "The
  ground beneath will catch up the next time the town changes over time. Update it now?", and the shade map view draws
  the new strip as pending (V05 §5.1). A Re-derive from S10a or an earlier stage runs a shade evaluation (V05 §3.19 (6)).
* **Update shade now** (`regenerate` with the scope `{ kind: 'shade', island }`; T10, and the fate's own button) — the
  shade evaluation `update` of V05 §3.19 (6) (d), for that island alone: its `shadeBasis` is written from the island as
  it stands (`by: 'update'`, the document's current epoch), its strips onto the ground and onto the islands beneath it are
  recomputed — and, where it had no basis, the strips onto it from the islands above it — and the ground's field is
  recomputed over the union of the old and new strip windows — the cells of the other islands' strips re-read from their
  unchanged bases — with the blocks there whose value crosses a cut past τ re-derived by §7.2's rule, their fates listed.
  It writes only its island's basis. The prosperity brush stays for hand changes.
* **Deleting an island** (T10's delete on an island, volume 9's delete row; the clerk; J-V5.21, J-V5.62). The dry run
  turns the delete into **`convert removeIsland`**: the island is removed without a landfall — its households relocated
  to the ground by §4.7's overflow pass (in an epoch's history, as any hand conversion, inside the event), its landing
  uses closed, its arena archived as `fallIsland` archives it, a split shop's use relocated to the ground (V05 §3.20
  (11)) — and its record stays live at its address in state `removed`, `removed` written (`seq`, `generated` from its
  lifecycle cause) and its `slot` held closed (V05 §3.2 (6)). It draws, labels and exports nothing but its `.plat` record
  (V05 §5.1, §5.5). Where the ground cannot house its households, the dry run offers instead `adjust('islands.count')`, a
  Re-derive from S7b. The fate list says, in volume 9's shared words, "The town won't raise this island again; undo
  brings it back". `convert fallIsland` is the ruinous alternative. A removed island is undone only by the removal's
  undo; a T5 `sky` declaration re-fills its slot with a new island only when no slot is free, never resurrecting it, and
  no `adjust('islands.count')` re-opens it (§2.8's lift rule). A floating, waning or settled island may be removed; a
  `fallen` one may not — its slot is already free and its landfall is ground content, which the ground's own edits
  clear; a `removed` one is already removed (the delete is a no-op, listed).
* **Bringing an island down** (`convert fallIsland`, the history tool; the clerk's "bring it down"). The dry run lists
  V05 §3.16's fates, one per covered entity; a covered way's reads "rubble; a track until the town clears it (was
  <class>)", `<class>` the class the fall writes to `Way.formerClass`, or the one the way already holds there. The
  event's undo restores V05 §3.16 (8)'s state.
* **A raised or aqueduct stroke to an island** (J-V5.61). A T4 stroke that ends on an island's rim with `raised` or the
  aqueduct class never makes a way (II.1: no roads in or out). Its dry run bends it (J-VI.4 (d); item 101 R2 refuses only
  a dossier fact or a lock): a `raised` stroke becomes `placeSkyAccess` with the stroke's ends as top and foot and the
  kind V05 §3.10's rules admit there — a `ropeway` for a slanted stroke, a `hoist` where the foot lies under the rim, a
  `lightBridge` where `magic` admits it (`bend.raised.island`); an aqueduct stroke ending on a rim ends at its last ground
  vertex outside the island's footprint (`bend.aqueduct.island`). A `raised` or aqueduct stroke wholly on an island's key
  is bent — `raised` dropped, the way drawn on the island's ground (`bend.raised.onIsland`); the aqueduct made a fountain
  pin at its end (`bend.aqueduct.onIsland`; J-VI.4 (e)). A `raised` stroke on the ground whose course would cut an
  access's column or pass within `lineClearM` under its rope line is re-routed by the dry run through
  `skyAccessObstacles`, or, where no re-route exists, ended at its last ground vertex before the column
  (`bend.raised.hoist`; J-VI.4 (f); V05 §3.22 (3)).
* **A retaining wall or terrace on an island**: V05 §3.22 (4) (`bend.retaining.rim` on or along the rim).
* **A wall on an island** (T3 with `key`): V05 §3.21 (3) (`bend.wall.drop` along the rim away from a landing).

**The bends** (item 101 R2; J-VI.4 (a)–(g); every one a bend with its line, never a refusal — the one refusal is
DR27's dossier-owned one, V05 §4.4). Each key is a row of volume 9's `bend.*` table (V09 §4.9) and of volume 10's `bend`

### 4.3 Locality and undo

| edit | effective scope | outside it, byte-identical |
|---|---|---|
| altitude, occlusion, underside, field and the island brushes, name, terrain and water on the island, a wall on the island, New random on the island | the island's arena and record | the ground and every other island (seam exception empty) |
| a resize, a rim reshape | the island's arena and record, plus the ground landing plots its re-landed feet name (their designations only) and the ground buildings that take the households a shrink relocates | everything else; every other island |
| an access placed, moved or deleted; an island moved; an island deleted | the island's arena and record, plus the ground landing plots its fates name (their designations only) | everything else |
| an island declared after generation | the new island's arena, plus the ground buildings its relocations vacate and the landing plots | everything else |
| a ground edit whose effective scope meets an island's projected footprint — a raised way, a retaining wall or a building added, raised or removed beneath it included (its dry run reading `skyAccessObstacles`, V05 §3.22 (3)) | the ground edit's own scope, plus that island's `groundRefCm` and, where V05 §3.9's floor requires it, its `altitudeCm` (a listed fate); a removal beneath never lowers the island — its `elevationCm` kept, `altitudeCm` re-read | the island's arena |
| `fallIsland` | V05 §3.16's covered set and the island's arena, plus another island's accesses the fall re-lands (V05 §3.16 (3)) | everything else |
| `removeIsland` | the island's arena and record, plus the ground buildings that take its households and its landing plots (their designations only) | everything else; the other islands' slots and draws |
| `reclearIsland` (an epoch's) | the island's record (`groundRefCm`, `altitudeCm`, `elevationCm` where raised) | everything else, the island's arena and `shadeBasis` included |
| Update shade now | the island's `shadeBasis`; the ground's field over the union of the island's old and new strip windows and the blocks there that cross a cut past τ; the strips onto the islands beneath it and, where it had no basis, the strips onto it | everything else, every other island's basis included |

No island edit writes the ground's field or any `shadeBasis`: the strips keep their basis (V05 §3.19 (6)), so every row
above holds for the field's rasters as for the ground's geometry, before and after a save and a load (§2.9); Update
shade now is not an island edit but a scoped shade evaluation. Every event undoes by its inverse through §7.4's fold, by
§7.4's rows for `placeSkyAccess`, `regenerate` with the scope `shade`, `convert removeIsland` and `reclearIsland`.
`Document.nextSkyPart` never decreases, so an undone island's part stays spent.

### 4.4 The dossier — DR27 and coherence

§7.7's row **DR27** (J-VI.13; ID-DR) traces the islands to the dossier — its rule V05 §3.2–§3.10, §3.15 (1b) and §3.20
(9)–(12), its stream `island.place`, its totality bar §7.7's. What each of its inputs feeds here: the population the
rung (DR23) and so the gate; `wealth.label` the field (DR6) and so `S_town`; the households' bands `S_carried` (DR7);
the institutions (DR9, DR24) the reason rows — a palace, cathedral, great temple, prison or mage's instance, a custom
institution by its resolved scene archetype and `landmarkLevel` (item 104 (a)); the institution ladder records, by their
step type, what may go up (item 108 (a): a singular or SIZE record; COUNT, GUILD and QUARTER members counted across both
arenas); the district-defining records the fill's held shares (item 72); `government` a palace's eligibility (DR11);
