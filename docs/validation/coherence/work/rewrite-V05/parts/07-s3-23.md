
### 3.23 Edges — every mechanism at its limits (principle (11))

Every mechanism of V05 §2–§5 has a defined outcome at the limits of its inputs, a rung on its failure ladder (V05 §3.17;
§5.2's order: check, repair locally, retry that piece, simpler shape, substitute, leave out and report — never a
whole-map retry) and a test. The rules below cover whole edge classes; the tables give every other case.

**(a) Degenerate geometry** (§5.1's general rule). Every sky step that commits geometry — the outline, the arena's L0
batch, the rim strip and claims, the ground landing claims, the tops' `splitThrough`, the resize's buffer, the landfall's
state writes — commits through kernel transactions: a degenerate result (a zero-length or collinear rim run, a
self-touching outline, a zero-area face, coincident points) is refused by the kernel (§3.1's `E_DEGENERATE` family) and
taken by the step's row of V05 §3.17: an outline by F-OUTLINE's rungs, then F-SKY-PLACE's shrink and redraw, then the
slot closed; a rim strip that would self-touch (a crescent's horn narrower than 2 × `rimStripM`) is laid on the
horn's inner offset where it exists and omitted along the horn where it does not, the strip face then several faces,
none cut or plotted; a landing yard of zero depth (a top on a horn) by F-SKY-ACCESS's next direction step; a claim with
no site by the institution floor (V05 §4.2) at SKY by F-SKY-FILL's next reason candidate. **As input:** an underside tier
whose ring falls below 1 % of the land's area ends the stack (V05 §3.9 (2)); a `flat` underside has tier 0 alone; a
tier ring the erosion empties is no tier; a strip with no cell passing the pre-test is an empty strip (`cells` 0,
`window` empty) and sets no bit; two feet that coincide keep `footGapM` apart by the gap rule; a raised stroke of zero
length makes no way and emits nothing; a landfall footprint wholly over water leaves nothing on the ground ("sank in
<water body>") and its covered set is empty — the island still falls (V05 §3.16 (7)); a footprint partly beyond the
ground frame covers only what lies inside it, the rest reported as over water is. **The draw and the exports skip a
degenerate mark** — a zero-area land face, a zero-length rim run or access line, an empty strip — and never fail a tile
or a file. Test: T-V5-33 (a).

**(c) Toggles and presets.** The islands **setting** off: no allocation, SKY writes nothing, the ground byte-identical to
the realism golden (T13-1); on with the gate failed: the report's sentence (V05 §3.2 (5)). The **view toggle** (the sky

the field's strips (model data) are unchanged and no digest moves (T15-1). **Occlusion** `opaque`, `ghost`, `offset`
and a per-island override: V05 §5.2–§5.4. **The parity presets** (`MFCG`, `FTG`) hold the setting off (J-V5.25); every
shipped sheet still resolves every sky semantic. **The no-op pins:** `w_sun` 0 (the strips computed and held, the field
unchanged; T-V5-19 (c)); item 103's coverage weight 0, items 105 and 107's weights at 0 (T-V5-22 (b), (k)); item 106's
closure off (T-V5-27); item 110's rows off (V05 §3.22 (9)); item 111's oasis chance, water pull and climate cell at their
neutral values (T-V5-37); the sampler at four points per cell (§4.5's fallback: the strips' `bits` and the OR follow,
T-V5-21 (f)). **The shade map view** on and off: the view reads the held strips and computes a pending strip only while
shown (V05 §6). **The underground layer** on or off changes nothing of the sky (item 2). **Exports:** the model and
interchange exports (`.plat`, JSON, the compact twin, CSV) carry the islands whatever the view; the picture and scene
exports take the layer set the export request names (§11.1), the view's visibility by default. Test: T-V5-34 (b).

**(e) Missing, empty or contradictory data — the dossier included** (items 20, 20 (h), 22). No dossier: no node term, the
magic circumstance the recipe's; a dossier with no `site.magic` resolves `magic` by §7.7's default and the gate reads it;
`site.magic` `none` with the setting on: the setting refused (the one dossier-owned refusal, V05 §4.4); a dossier change
to `none` at an absorb: every island's target 0 at the next epoch — they wane, never vanish; a `magical_node` whose state
is unknown to the port is on §7.7 item 1's ignore-list and adds nothing; two nodes add one bonus; a dossier palace whose
government admits no palace (`requires.government` fails): the palace stays on the ground, its row not a candidate —
never a contradiction, since the island moves where a dossier institution stands, never whether it exists; a custom
institution whose archetype matches no row stays on the ground; `activeChains[]` empty: no chain rule acts; a dossier
population below the CITY band: the rung gate, `gated: rung`; the dossier removing an island's institution: V05 §3.15
(1b), under a lock J-V5.82; the dossier removing a building an author pinned on an island: the use leaves (truth), the
pinned building stays vacant where it is (a pin holds geometry, never a dossier use), listed. **An older document**: every
missing field reads as V05 §2.6's migrate column states. **A missing table cell** reads its default. **Contradictory
edits**: a lock on an island and an epoch's wane — held (J-V5.10); a declaration where `h_town` = 0 — held against its
supply, listed. Test: T-V5-35.

**(f) Save, load, undo, redo, regenerate and migrate at each state.** Each island state (`floating`, `waning`, `settled`,
`fallen`, `removed`) and each edit of V05 §4.2 persists by V05 §2.6's rows: a save and a load reproduce the model digest,
the field's rasters and every strip byte for byte (the strips from the persisted bases, never the live island); undo and
redo of every event restore the model and the field exactly, a redo re-running the event's fold; an **epoch undone after a
save and a load** restores every island's record, basis and strips, the landfall's writes and the households' channels
from the epoch event's undo record (§7.4); a Regenerate and a Re-derive from S7b, SKY or S10a keep every held value
(declarations, locks, removals) and rewrite the rest by V05 §2.6's regenerate column; a migrate from an older schema runs
§7.5's chain, the island-basis step once. The unit inputs, the route and the obstacle list are derived and never
persisted. Test: T-V5-35 (b).
