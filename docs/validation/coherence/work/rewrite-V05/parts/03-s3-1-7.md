### 3.2 Existence and count

1. **The gate.** Islands exist only if all hold: `recipe.layers.islands = 'on'`; the rung admits them
   (`RungProfile.layers.islands`, §1.1); the recipe is not a parity preset's (`MFCG` and `FTG` hold `off`, J-V5.25);
   and, for a dossier-bound document, the circumstance `magic` that DR27 derives from `site.magic` is not `none`
   (V05 §4.4). A failed gate makes no allocation and reports `islands: off`, `gated: rung` or `gated: dossier magic`.
2. **The town supply** `S_town` and `h_town` (V05 §3.3).
3. **Forced slots.** Every declared island of the creation prefix (T5 `quarter` with `sky`) and every assignment whose
   criteria carry `sky: true` (V05 §4.2) forces the lowest free slot open, in log order. A fourth is bent at its dry run
   to join the nearest open island as a district on it (`bend.island.fourth`, V05 §4.2; J-VI.4 (a)).
4. **The count.** `recipe.params['islands.count']` — `auto` (default), or 0–3, the `adjust` of V05 §4.2. At an integer
   `n`, exactly `n` slots are open (the forced ones counted first, a slot held by a removal counted as one of them, (6);
   `n` below the forced count is bent up to it at its dry run, `bend.island.count`), with no draw. At `auto`, each slot k
   above the forced ones, in order, opens with probability `chance[k] · h_town` and only if slot k − 1 is open: one
   uniform on `island.place` keyed by the town's lineage with creation index `slot:k` (V05 §3.18).
5. The report lists the count, the forced slots and each slot's outcome (V05 §3.4–§3.7), a held slot's outcome being
   `removed by you` (6). With the setting on, the gate passed and no island present, the report carries one plain
   sentence for the author and the clerk — "Islands are on, but the city is not prosperous enough to keep one aloft" when
   `h_town` is 0 or every slot closed `no supply`, "… none rose this time" when the slot draws came up empty — and names
   both remedies: raise the town's wealth (T1 `intend`), or place an island by hand (T5 with `sky`, held against its
   supply, J-V5.10).
6. **Removed slots** — the island form of §2.8's suppression (J-V5.62; E-02 keeps the two forms apart). A slot held by an
   island record in state `removed` whose `removed.releasedBy` is absent (`convert removeIsland`, V05 §4.2 — a generated
   or a declared island alike) is **closed with the outcome `removed by you`**: no draw on its `slot:k` or `reason:k`
   index, no fill, no outline, no placement, no SKY run. It **counts as open and filled** — for the slot chain of (4),
   so a higher slot draws on its own creation index exactly as before the removal, and toward an integer
   `islands.count`, so the removal is never replaced by an island in another slot (the owner removed that island, not the
   town's quota of islands). It is released by §2.8's one lift rule alone: the removal's undo, or a T5 `sky` declaration
   when no slot is free — the user's re-add serving that key, which takes the lowest held slot and sets
   `removed.releasedBy`. An integer `islands.count` counts every held slot as filled and opens only free slots, and
   `auto` never draws a held slot; an adjust whose integer exceeds the slots it can open opens what it can and lists the
   fate "slot k is held by your removal of <name>; undo the removal or draw an island to use it". The removed island's
   households were relocated by its removal, so S7b (1) allocates them to the ground at every re-derivation and the
   population stays whole (T-V5-29).

### 3.3 The two supplies, on absolute values

A mean over the town compared with its own per-town quantile measures the distribution's shape, not the town's wealth,
and never reaches the field's top (RV-pc-3, `docs/retrovalidation/pipeline-content.md`); both supplies are therefore
read on the field's **absolute** value, which the dials, `wealthLevel`'s character offset (§7.7 layer 1) and the epochs'
drivers move (J-V5.3):

* **`S_town`** — the mean prosperity value `P` over the settlement's built land: the cells of §4.5's 32 m field raster
  whose centres lie in a district face (the hinterland, underground, undercity and sky pseudo-districts excluded), less
  the cells in water and in reservations of kind `park`, `harbour_basin`, `moat` or `canal`; at S7b the value after field
  pass 1, at an epoch the island-free value after that epoch's re-evaluation (V05 §3.19 (7)).
* **`S_carried`** — the mean, over the households an island carries, of their band's value `v(b)`: the midpoint of the
  band's two cuts on the absolute value (`FieldBands.cuts`), the lowest band's lower cut 0 and the top band's upper cut 1.
  An institution island's `S_carried` is `v(requirementBand)` of its row.
* `h_town(S_town)` and `h_carried(S_carried)` interpolate the rule's `townSupply` and `carriedSupply` rows linearly along
  the value, 0 below the first row and 1 above the last. The **supported area** `A` and the **law's altitude** `E` are
  II.6 (5)'s.
* **The magical node** (J-V5.41). Where the dossier digest's `site.deposits` carries a `magical_node` in state
  `available` — or `discovered`, from the epoch after the one that discovered it — `h_town` is `min(1, the interpolated
  value + node.bonus)`. It is one addend however many such deposits the dossier lists; `depleted` and `removed` add
  nothing. It acts only where the interpolated value is above 0 — the owner's "if there is no prosperity, there is no
  floating islands" — and reaches every reader of `h_town` (the slots, `A`, `E`, the epochs). A map without a dossier has
  no deposits and no node term; the report and the clerk's `why` name the deposit when it acts. **Where the node stands
  is not this volume's:** item 109 (f) puts it at the heart of an underground ruin where one exists (its position volume
  4's and volume 8's), and the term stays positionless: it reads the deposit's state alone, never the underground arena
  (item 2). The clerk names "the node beneath the old ruins" from the report's `ruin.node` line (V04 §3.5.0a (9): the
  deposit, the ruin system and the ruin's `NameRef`, so a renamed ruin reads its new name), never by reading the ruin;
  with no such line it names the deposit alone.

**Calibration ⟨P0⟩** (V05 §8). On 200 CITY and 100 METROPOLIS seeds at their targets, at `wealthLevel` ∈ {0, 0.25, 0.5,
0.75, 1} with the setting on, record `S_town`'s median `m_w` per level; set `townSupply` to (m_0.25, 0) (m_0.5, 0.5)
(m_1, 1). Bars: the median `h_town` at `wealthLevel` 0 ≤ 0.05, at 0.5 within [0.35, 0.65], at 1 ≥ 0.9, all on
standalone intents, so with no node term. **Pre-decided fallback:** if `m_1 − m_0` < 0.10 (the field does not tell a
rich town from a poor one), `S_town` becomes `0.5 · (the field mean) + 0.5 · w`, `w` the intent's `wealthLevel` (with a
dossier its derived value, DR6), the rows are re-fitted by the same rule, and the change is recorded as a declared shift.
`carriedSupply` is checked at the same run: `h_carried(v(rich))` ≥ 0.7 and `h_carried(v(modest))` = 0 at `wealthLevel`
0.5 on ≥ 90 % of seeds; else its rows are moved so that the midpoint of `v(modest)` and `v(comfortable)` maps to 0 and
`v(rich)` to 1, re-measured once.

### 3.4 Purpose — a prosperity island or an institution island

For each open slot, in slot order (J-V5.4):

1. **A forced slot** takes its declaration's kind (an institution kind makes it an institution island; any other kind a
   prosperity island) or its assignment's institution.
2. **Otherwise the candidates are:** `prosperity`, weight 1.0, where the roster's households of the bands with
   `h_carried(v(b)) > 0` not yet carried by an earlier slot give at least `minAreaHa` of plot land; and each
   `IslandInstitution` row, at its `weight`, where the roster demands at least one eligible instance of a class carrying
   one of its `tags` (the first tag in the row's order that has one, its lowest roster ordinal) — or the dossier carries
   a custom institution whose resolved profile's scene archetype is one of the row's `archetypes` (a `landmarkOnly` entry
   admitting only `landmarkLevel: landmark`; item 104 (a) (2)), the lowest `localUid` first — that no earlier slot carries
   and no creation-prefix `placeAt` holds on the ground, its `requires.government` (when present) holds for the town's
   `Government.kind`, its `requires.magic` (when present) admits the recipe's `magic` circumstance, and `areaHaMax ·
   h_carried(v(requirementBand)) · h_town` ≥ `institutionFit.fitFactor` × the row's `areaHa`. **An eligible instance is
   one building or one compound** (item 108 (a); J-V5.57): a singular record, or a record whose ladder step is SIZE, or a
   roster instance bound to neither — never a COUNT, GUILD or QUARTER record, nor a member `#k` of one. A dossier
   institution counts as a roster instance (DR9, DR24): the island moves where it stands, never whether it exists (§7.7).
   The archetype is read at this draw only: a later revision of a custom kind re-resolves its profile and keeps it on its
   island unless a hard row of the new profile fails there, when item 104 (e)'s least disruption re-sites it on the
   ground; the island keeps its reason and its claim, the vacated building steps down the base's building ladder, and
   the regrowth-first order re-occupies it with the next instance of one of the row's tags or its archetype before any
   ground site (§4.11; V05 §3.15 (4)).
3. **The draw:** one weighted choice on `island.place` (town lineage, index `reason:k`). With no candidate the slot
   closes, reported `no supply` or `nothing to carry`.

### 3.5 The sky allocation (S7b (5) at depth)

**A prosperity slot.**
1. The eligible bands are those with `h_carried(v(b)) > 0`, richest first.
2. Households are taken from the roster's residential demand of the richest eligible band, in batches of
   `fill.batchShare` of that band's demand (at least one household), each batch split over the classes by the band's
   class mix at its centre (item 71; largest remainder); after each batch `S_carried` and `A` are recomputed. **Held
   shares are never taken:** the households a dossier district-defining record's raised share holds on its ground
   district (item 72) — a QUARTER record of item 108 (a) among them — are subtracted from the band's demand before the
   fill, so the island never drains a quarter the dossier names. The fill stops at the first of: the carried plot land
   (Σ over the carried rows of `plotArea(c, b)` × count, S2's plot areas at the band centres) reaching `plotShare · A`,
   `plotShare` volume 1's `hood.constants.sky.plotShare` (V01 §3.14; §4.5 S7b (5)), the one share the island's district
   count divides by, so an edit of it moves the fill and the count together (J-V5.45); the carried households reaching
   `fill.householdCapDistricts` × `H_d`, `H_d` the town's households ÷ its district count after S6; or the band's demand
   exhausted, when the next band is taken only if the batch would leave the carried plot land within the recomputed
   `plotShare · A`. `A` falls as poorer households join, so the fill converges (II.6 (5)).
3. **What goes up with them — the carried workplaces, read through `IslandRule.carry`** (J-V5.32, J-V5.39, J-V5.51). For
   each workplace or institution class demanded at the carried bands, the carried households' share of their band's
   households × its count, by largest remainder, except:
   (a) **never carried** — the `IslandInstitution` tags (their own slots); the town-unique classes (§10.2's `unique`
   column: the principal market, the castle, the government's seat); every class whose balance profile (item 107 (a)) is
   in `carry.never.balance` — the heavy processors and wholesale and storage, which belong by the gates and quays; every
   class whose siting profile (item 103 (a)) emits one of `carry.never.emitsTopStep` at the profile scale's top step,
   since nothing on an island can lie downwind or downstream of its homes; every class with a hard NEEDS row naming a
   `carry.never.hardNeeds` feature — the conjured river and the spring pond satisfy no NEEDS row (V05 §3.20 (1)); and
   every resource or processing node of an active chain — custom (item 104 (b)) or one of the dossier's `activeChains[]`
   with its processors resolved by kind family (item 105 (i)) — the island institution of V05 §3.4 excepted. Their
   demand stays on the ground, where the chain keeps its working steps together (V05 §3.20 (9)).
   (b) **carried by share** — every class (a) does not exclude: the everyday services and the town-wide specialists of
   `carry.share.balance` above all, a chain's final seller and its consumers among them.
   (c) **split across the arenas** — a class whose row declares `split` (item 105 (h)) and whose works (a) excludes (a
   butcher's slaughterhouse emits at the top step; a brewer's brewhouse is a heavy processor): with
   `carry.share.splitShop` on, its **shop** may go up by the share while its **works** stays on the ground — one item on
   two plots on two arenas, the shop's building carrying a `Use` whose `branchOf` names the works' use. Item 105 (h)'s
   split threshold is read with the route distance (V05 §3.20 (1)) between the island's demand and the works' ground
   site; below it the class stays whole on the ground. Every split is reported; off in the parity presets, as the
   islands are.
   (d) **collective members** (item 108 (b), (c)) — a member `#k` of a COUNT, GUILD or QUARTER record carried by the share
   keeps its record and its key; the record's range check (T73-4) counts its members on both arenas together; a lesser a
   greater subsumes is carried as that greater's member, never as an addition.
   **Coverage** (item 103 (f); J-V5.37). For a class with a coverage row, the island counts as **k units** for a row
   counted by district or neighbourhood — k from `skyDistrictCount(plotLandM2, districtHa)` (V01 §3.16) on the
   allocation's plot land, the call the unit runner makes — **one unit** for a row counted by walking radius (the route
   to the ground passes every walking radius, V05 §3.20 (3)), and **⌈its carried households ÷ N⌉ units** for a row counted
   by N households. The proportional share above stands, and the island's units then join S7b (1)'s coverage step as
   candidate units beside the ground's districts, which may add an instance of the class to this allocation from the
   demand it distributes — never remove one — unless the add would carry the plot land past `fill.coverageOvershoot` ×
   `plotShare · A`, when that unit is skipped for that class (reported). Inside the island, the unit run's S10b spreads a
   coverage class over its districts and spaces its members by item 107 (d)'s same-kind row (V05 §3.20 (12)).
4. If `A` < `minAreaHa`, the slot closes (reported) and its rows return to the demand.
5. The island's district count and its partition are volume 1's (V01 §3.10.3 (2), read with `districtHa`): this volume
   counts none (J-V5.28).
6. One `Allocation` (level `district`, target the island's sky pseudo-district) holds the rows; `S_carried` and `A` are
   written to the slot's record-to-be.

**An institution slot.** Its row's instance (V05 §3.4 (2)) with `claim` set once SKY makes the claim (V05 §3.10), plus
`staffHouseholds` households of the requirement band; `A` = min(the law's `A`, `institutionFit.areaCapShare` × the row's
`areaHa`) — a compact island, its surround the unit run's gardens, close or yard; its claim's face is typed by the row's
`districtKind` (V05 §3.12). Then, in order:
1. **A greater goes up alone** (item 108 (c); J-V5.57). A greater that subsumes lessers — a cathedral over the parish
   church, the resident priest and the wayside shrine — takes only its own building up. Its lessers stay separate
   buildings, members of its collective each keeping its `#k`: on the ground by their own rules, and on an island only by
   a prosperity slot's share; each counts once, never as an addition.
2. **The ground reservation's hand-over** (J-V5.46). Where the instance holds an S3 reservation — the temple or cathedral
   precinct (§4.2 S3 (2)), or the reservation a palace takes by the castle's siting rules (§4.2 S3 (8)) — the
   reservation stays exactly as it is (no L1 change) and passes to the row's `handover`: the first of its `tags` the
   roster demands on the ground, its largest instance (V05 §2.4), which S10b places into it as its claim (the
   reservation's `owner` becomes that instance and its `handedFrom` the institution that went up, §2.5); a reservation
   sized for the greater keeps its size, the surplus becoming the incoming instance's churchyard, close or garden by its
   template's yard rows (§4.9). With no such tag demanded, `else`: `park` — re-typed `park`, owner `commons`, laid out by
   S12b as the old palace gardens or the old close's green — or `none`. An institution the creation prefix sends up (a
   T5 `sky` declaration of its kind, a T6 `criteria.sky` assignment) leaves nothing to hand over: S3 never reserves it
   (§4.2 S3 (2)). After generation — a T6 assignment or an epoch's `raiseIsland` taking a ground institution up — its
   vacated building steps down the base's ladder, and the regrowth-first order re-occupies it with the row's hand-over
   classes first. The report and the clerk name the hand-over ("the old cathedral close — now the mother church").
3. **Burial and the crypt** (item 109 (k) (2)–(3) as revised, J111; item 2; J-V5.43). The churchyard part the base gives
   every church and temple plot (§4.9's churchyard row) — the island cathedral's, and any church's a prosperity island
   carries — is a surface burial ground only: it is left out of item 109 (k) (3)'s catacomb candidates and out of its
   "largest cemetery" ranking (volume 4's candidate list skips every sky-arena face). Each church on an island still
   counts once toward the catacomb count (V04 §3.5.1 (3), §2.3), since the dossier's church records and the roster set
   that count wherever the churches stand. The island cathedral's crypt — always the settlement's largest (volume 11's
   crypt column, `always`) — is a vault cut into the island's rim slab under the cathedral (V05 §3.10 (5)): part of its
   building, never an `UndergroundSystem` part or an item 109 (j) (3b) link, and no catacomb grows beneath it. The ground
   church that takes the old close keeps its own crypt by the crypted-class rows and is never ranked above the
   cathedral's.

**The rest.** S7b (1) allocates the remainder to the ground's districts, so the population stays whole (II.6).

### 3.6 Shape (S7b (5), per open slot, in the island's local frame)

1. **Template.** A declaration's points form gives it (a lasso is the outline itself, one point a disc of area `A`, two
   to four corners the routine fitted to their hull); otherwise one weighted draw of `outline.templates` on
   `island.place` (the island's lineage, index `template`).
2. **Bearing.** The long axis takes the ground's principal river's bearing (its course from inlet to outfall) where the
   site has a river; otherwise a uniform bearing on `island.place` (index `bearing`).
3. **The routine.** §4.2 S1 (2)'s closed-outline routine on the template's base curve, with the rule's detail row and a
   uniform envelope (no town side), from `island.place` (index `outline`); the base curve's scale is set by one secant
   step so that the displaced outline's area is `A` within 1 %. F-OUTLINE's ladder applies (§5.2).
4. The outline's centroid is the local origin, and the arena's frame is sized from the outline now drawn (V05 §3.8 (1)).

### 3.7 Placement — where the island floats (S7b (5), per open slot, in slot order)

1. **The class raster.** A 64 m raster over the ground frame classes each cell: `water` (its centre in an L0 water face,
   an oasis's included — item 111's oasis is water like any other); `core` (in a district of growth stage 1);
   `outskirts` (in any other district); `fields` (land outside the settlement perimeter). It is built once per S7b from
   the ground plan that exists then (L0, L1, the districts, the perimeter).
2. **Candidates.** The centroids of a `latticeM` lattice over the frame whose translated outline lies inside the frame
   inset by `frameInsetM`, does not meet any placed island's projected outline dilated by `gapM`, and **can be reached**
   (J-V5.54): at least one of its rim's cells on the class raster lies on admissible ground (for a hoist or a stair) or
   within the horizontal run of an admitted slanted access row (`ropeway`, and `lightBridge` where the `magic`
   circumstance admits it) of admissible ground. Admissible ground is the raster's land cells outside water,
   reservations and wall strips, and outside every committed raised part's raised-way pier footprints, its deck's plan
   dilated by `accessSiting.deckGapM` and every deck face of item 110 (d) — V05 §3.10 (3)'s admissible faces at the
   raster's cell (a cell is admissible when its centre is); the test reads one chamfer distance transform to it (integer,
   3–4 weights on the 64 m cells), built once with the class raster (J-V5.60).
3. **The score** of a candidate, over the outline's cells on the class raster: (a) the mean class weight — the rule's
   `prefs`, with an institution row's `overWeights` over them, and with `core` at 1.0 when the island is placed by
   assignment or declaration — × 1.0; (b) proximity, − 0.5 × min(1, d ÷ `nearM`), `d` the distance from the outline to
   the settlement perimeter (0 over the town); (c) purpose, 0.5 × one term, read from the row's `near` or `far` (V05 §2.4)
   for an institution island: `near: 'plaza'` 1 − min(1, d ÷ the row's `nearM`) to the plaza; `near: 'precinct'` the same
   to the precinct where S3 reserved one (then the hand-over church's close, V05 §3.5), else to the plaza; `near:
   'richest'` the same to the district of highest prosperity mean; `far: 'plaza'` min(1, d ÷ the row's `nearM`) from the
   plaza; a prosperity island 1 − min(1, d ÷ `nearM`) to the district of highest prosperity mean (ties by district
   address) (J-V5.34).
4. **The draw.** The `topK` best (ties by lattice y, then x), then one draw weighted by score − min score + 0.01 on
   `island.place` (index `place`). The chosen centroid is `anchor`; `placement.over` is the class of largest share under
   the outline (ties in the order water, fields, outskirts, core); `byAssignment` is set for a forced slot (J-V5.5).
5. **F-SKY-PLACE** (V05 §3.17) when no candidate passes.
