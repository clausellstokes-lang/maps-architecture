a settled island's are 0.

### 3.15 Over time — the island laws an epoch applies (`IslandEpochStep`)

`IslandEpochStep.apply(doc, epoch) → ConversionOp[]` (`@plat/sky`; §4.11's island kinds) is called at each epoch after
S6's re-evaluation of the field and before S7b's delta allocation, so the households it releases or takes enter that
allocation. `@plat/sky` registers it in §4.12's `StrategyRegistry` as volume 6's `EpochParticipant` `sky.islands`, step
`afterField`, order 0, stage `SKY` (V06 §2.1, §3.2): its `run(ctx)` calls `apply(ctx.doc, ctx.epoch)` with `years` =
`ctx.years` and `rung` = `ctx.rung`, draws on `island.place` as below (never on `ctx.stream`, so a history with or without
the engine's other participants draws the same island values), and returns the conversions as `EpochOps.ops` in island
slot order. A second participant, **`sky.clearance`**, runs step 7 at `afterGrowth`, order 50 — after volume 6's
`hood.fabric` (order 40), so every raised part, retaining wall, building and massing tier the epoch committed is read —
stage `SKY` (V06 §3.2's table). **Households move through volume 6's typed channels** (V06 §2.1, §3.8.1): every household
the step releases — a wane's leavers, a settle's remainder, a fall's covered homes when the fall happens in an epoch —
enters volume 6's displaced pool with cause `island` (placed first at the `siting` step, their addresses kept, each
placement a `relocate`); every household a refloat or a raise takes up from the epoch's delta demand is reserved by
`EpochOps.demandTaken: { band: string; count: number }[]`, which `siting` subtracts from the delta demand before it houses
the rest; existing households relocated up are `relocate` ops in `EpochOps.ops`. So ground plus sky households equal the
epoch's target after `siting` (T-V5-21 (g)). A fall in an epoch commits its ruined raised spans and raised-way piers in
the epoch's lifecycle, so volume 6's `EpochScope.raised` carries them to that epoch's raised-way pass (V06 §3.19.2 (4)).
`years` is the epoch's span (§4.11 (5)); every rate below is per year, applied over it. For each island that is not
fallen or removed:

1. **Evaluate.** The rung row of the epoch's rung — the fold's (`ctx.rung`, V06 §3.1 (3); §4.11 *The rung at an epoch*;
   J-V5.74): `none` makes the target area 0. Otherwise `S_town` from the re-evaluated field's island-free value (V05 §3.19
   (7): the supply law never reads the islands' own shade), the node term from the deposits as they stand at the epoch
   (V05 §3.3), `S_carried` from the island's households' bands under the new cuts (an institution island's requirement
   band), and the targets `A_t` and `E_t` by V05 §3.3. `supply` is rewritten.
   1b. **The institution has left** (J-V5.53; item 20 (h): a dossier removal is truth). If the island's reason is
   `institution` and its institution is gone — removed by a dossier event at an absorb (the institution leaves at once:
   its building vacated, its uses closed, §7.7), its custom kind deactivated (item 104 (e)), or closed with no successor
   for a whole epoch — the island re-reasons now, in order: (i) the next eligible instance of one of the row's `tags` or
   its archetype (V05 §3.4 (2)) relocates up into the vacant claim (a `relocate`; its ground site handed over by V05 §3.5's
   rule); else (ii) the island becomes a prosperity island where `h_carried(v(rich))` > 0 and the rich band's demand
   admits it — the claim's building re-let as a mansion (a `relet` to the `rich` band's residential class), rich households
   taken up by the delta (`demandTaken`) and by relocation within the churn cap as a refloat takes them, the claim's face
   keeping its `sky-*` kind until a re-derivation re-types it `sky-island`; else (iii) `A_t` = 0 and the island wanes by
   step 2. `reason` and `institution` are rewritten by the conversion; the report and the clerk name the cause. **Under a
   lock** (J-V5.82): the institution still leaves at once (a lock never holds a dossier fact, §7.2); a locked island
   keeps `reason: 'institution'` with `institution` cleared and its claim standing vacant — the fate "held without its
   institution" listed at the absorb and at every epoch — and (1b) runs at the first epoch after the lock is lifted; a
   lock on the `state`, `areaHa` or `altitudeCm` path alone does not hold (i) or (ii), which write none of them, while
   (iii)'s wane is held as any wane is (below).
2. **Floating.** If `A_t = 0`: `waneIsland` (floating → waning), `dwellUntilWeek` = the week + a draw in
   `dwellYears.waning` × 52 on `island.place` (the island's lineage and the epoch component, index `dwell`). Otherwise, if
   |`A_t` − `areaHa`| > `hysteresis` × `areaHa`: `resizeIsland` toward `A_t` by at most `growthPerYear · areaHa · years`
   either way (magic accretes and erodes slowly), and its altitude toward `E_t` by at most `altitudeRateMPerYear · years`
   metres, never below V05 §3.9's floor; a resize stops at the institution floor of V05 §4.2, listed in the epoch's
   report.
3. **Waning.** Each epoch the altitude falls linearly from its value at the wane's start to V05 §3.9's floor (with
   `E_min`) by the dwell's end, and the area toward half its value at the start, both by `resizeIsland`; the households
   leave in reverse ordinal order (the suffix, as §4.11's step (c) empties buildings; J-V5.22), ⌈H · years ÷ dwell⌉ each
   epoch, each a `relet` down or a `vacate` on the island and the household entering the displaced pool with cause
   `island`; the island's vacant buildings then step down the base's building ladder. If `A_t > 0` again:
   `refloatIsland` (waning → floating), the dwell cleared. When the dwell ends: `settleIsland` — the altitude at the floor,
   every remaining household into the displaced pool, the magical lights at 0, `dwellUntilWeek` = the week + a draw in
   `dwellYears.settled`.
4. **Settled.** If `A_t > 0` (and the rung admits islands): `refloatIsland` (settled → floating) — regrowth-first: its
   vacant stock is reoccupied before any ground plot, by the rich bands' delta demand first (reserved by `demandTaken`)
   and then by relocating existing rich households up, within the epoch's churn cap (§4.11) — its altitude climbing at
   the rate. When the dwell ends: `fallIsland` (V05 §3.16).
5. **Empty slots** (J-V5.13). A slot holding a live, unfallen island is never redrawn. An empty slot re-opens with II.6
   (4)'s per-epoch probability `(1 − (1 − redrawPerYear)^years) · chance[k] · h_town`, drawn on `island.place` with the
   epoch component, only if every lower slot is filled. **A slot a `removed` record holds is not empty** (V05 §3.2 (6)):
   never re-opened by an epoch, it draws nothing and counts as filled for the slots above it. A slot that opens refloats
   a settled island first (the settled island with the highest `h_carried` for its households, ties by slot, then
   address) and otherwise `raiseIsland`: S7b's delta fill for the sky (V05 §3.5, taking the rich bands' delta demand first,
   reserved by `demandTaken`, then relocating existing rich households up within the churn cap), then V05 §3.6–§3.14 on
   the new island, its districts laid by the unit run at the epoch; it has no `shadeBasis` until the next epoch's
   evaluation (V05 §3.19 (6)).
6. **A rung crossing downward** makes every island's target 0: they wane, settle and fall over their dwells, never in one
   epoch (T8-6).
7. **Clearance after growth** (`sky.clearance`; J-V5.68). After the epoch's growth, every live island (not fallen, not
   removed) is re-checked by SKY-F's clearance rule (V05 §3.9 (5)) against the casters the epoch committed under its
   projected solid: the raised parts of volume 6's raised-way pass and of a new stage's S4 (their deck tops with parapet
   or channel wall, their raised-way piers), the retaining walls' high-side tops and the levelled platforms, and the
   buildings — a stepped one at its stack envelope's top, and a building whose Works starts later in the epoch's weeks at
   its planned finished height, since it is committed now (V06 §3.2's `works` step). It reads volume 6's
   `EpochScope.built`, `.raised` and `.levelled`. Where one stands above `groundRefCm`, the participant returns one op
   `{ op: 'reclearIsland'; island; groundRefCm; altitudeCm; elevationRaisedCm }` (V05 §4.2) that raises `groundRefCm` to
   it **keeping `elevationCm`** and, only where V05 §3.9 (4)'s floor then fails, raises `elevationCm` to the floor
   (`elevationRaisedCm` > 0, a listed fate: "raised clear of the aqueduct" naming the caster). It **never writes
   `shadeBasis`** (item 102 (e)'s lag, J-V5.27). A locked island, or a lock on its `altitudeCm` path, holds its
   `elevationCm`: `groundRefCm` and `altitudeCm` are still rewritten, and where the floor then fails the island stays
   where it hangs, the conflict listed (`clearance held by your lock`) and the island exempt from the clearance invariant
   as a held island is from the supply invariant (§5.3; T-V5-31 (f)). The step draws nothing, so the same history clears
   the same islands (T-V5-31 (b)).

A locked island, or a locked `state`, `areaHa` or `altitudeCm` path, is held by these steps (§7.2) — the report lists it
as `held against its supply` when its supply is 0 (J-V5.10).

**The shade over time** (item 102 (e)). Each epoch's evaluation (V05 §3.19 (6)) reads every live island as it stands when
the epoch begins — before these steps change it — so a resize, a sinking, a raise, a fall or an author's edit since the
last evaluation moves the field one epoch later, and the uses beneath follow by the engine's normal lag (volume 6
schedules the drift; §4.11's ladder moves the survivors). A fallen island casts nothing from the epoch after its fall; a
settled island casts from its floor altitude, so its strip draws in toward its footprint. An island that step 5 raises
has no basis until the next epoch's evaluation: it casts and receives no strip meanwhile, and a save and a load in
between compute none for it (J-V5.72); volume 6's building mask on its lattice likewise starts at the next field phase
(V06 §3.4 (8)).

**A removed slot draws nothing** (V05 §3.2 (6)): its `slot:k` and `reason:k` indices are skipped, and every other slot's
creation indices are unchanged, so removing an island never moves another island's draws. `sky.clearance`,
`skyAccessObstacles`, the removed-ordinal skip, the strips (V05 §3.19) and the route distance (V05 §3.20) draw nothing:
they are pure functions of committed state (the strips of the persisted `shadeBasis`, never the live island), the model
sampler and `dmath` (§6.5), the strips reading the tiers without their roughness. The island's gradient (V05 §3.11)
draws only its ε on `field.chance`. **Every choice by score or order breaks its ties the same way:** among islands by
slot, then address; among sites and lattice cells by lattice y, then x; among districts by address. The tiers'
roughness uses `dmath` noise keyed by the island's render key — view data outside every digest (§6.2) and the same on
every engine (§6.5). The part number is minted once and recorded in the address (V05 §2.1); `raiseIsland` keys the
island by the town's lineage, the slot and the epoch (J-A8.61), so the same history raises the same island. §6.1's
distinctness test runs a sky-island fixture; §13.3's goldens gain the two sky fixtures of T-V5-15.
