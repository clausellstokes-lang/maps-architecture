Measured on §12.1's reference hardware at the port's cadence (52 weeks), PLAUSIBLE until the E0 spike (V06 §8) times

**A century** (100 epochs at the port's 52-week cadence at a town growing 1 % a year, growth stages and participants
included; hand edits during the span excluded — each is an edit at its own row; item 110's rows add ≈ 0.7 / 1.3 / 2.6 s of
epoch work and at most one course solve per founding, ≤ 4 per century at METROPOLIS).
**The sky's island events** (J-V6.118) are priced at V05 §6's rows — "a resize, a rim reshape" for every resize (a
wane's included), "`fallIsland`" for a fall, a raise at `raiseIsland`'s parts bounded by "declaring an island after
generation" — at their CITY and METROPOLIS values (a TOWN reads CITY's, its islands no larger). With an island's buildings
≤ 5 % of the town's (V05 §6's unit-run row) at the default request's buildings (§12.5: ≈ 1 500 / 6 000 / 15 000), ≤ 300 /
750 at CITY / METROPOLIS, and ≤ 3 accesses (`accessCount.max`), those rows give a growing resize ≈ 228 / 255 ms (the fixed
5 ms; §12.6's scoped law over its three receiving districts, ≈ 36 / 63 ms; its yards' re-cut plots ≈ 7 ms; 3 × 30 ms for
the yard tops and as much for the re-landed feet), a shrink ≈ 209 / 227 ms (its claim's district ≈ 12 / 21 ms and its
rim ring ≈ 12 / 21 ms instead), a fall ≤ 80 / 110 ms and a raise ≤ 120 / 160 ms.
**The worst case is every island resized at every epoch with every foot re-landed**, because V05 §4.2's re-landing rule
bounds nothing below one re-landing per foot per resize: a resize moves the rim by at most `growthPerYear` · years · A ÷ P
≤ 0.025 · years · r (the disc's, the least perimeter for an area), ≈ 4.5 m a year at CITY's `areaHaMax` and ≈ 6.3 m at
METROPOLIS's, while a ground plot may be 3 m wide (§15.2's O5 (b)); over a century the rim can travel ≈ 450 / 630 m, more
than the century's 100 resizes' worth of plot widths, and a target moving back and forth carries a foot across the same
plot line at every resize. **A fall does not raise it:** a fall ends a settled dwell of at least 10 years without a resize
(V05 §3.15 (3)–(4)), and a fall with a raise in one epoch (≈ 200 / 270 ms) costs less than the ten resizes the dwell
forgoes. So at worst **CITY** 3 × 100 × 228 ms ≈ 68.4 s; **METROPOLIS** 3 × 100 × 255 ms ≈ 76.5 s; **TOWN**, whose rung
admits no island (V05 §3.15 (1)), each of ≤ 3 carried or declared islands waning once — a shrink at each of ≤ 15 epochs
of the longest dwell — then settling and falling: 3 × (15 × 209 + 80) ms ≈ 9.6 s. A history whose islands resize one
epoch in five or fewer (V05's hysteresis 0.05 against a town growing 1 % a year) spends about a fifth of these.
**The growth stages** at §12.6's law (§6.1): ≤ one stage an epoch at ≤ 6 ms each plus 60 µs × the buildings the century
adds (≈ 1.7 × the default request: ≈ 2 500 / 10 000 / 25 000) ≈ 0.75 / 1.2 / 2.1 s.
**The sum**: TOWN 100 × (103 + 34) ms + 0.7 s + 9.6 s + 0.75 s ≈ 24.8 s; CITY 100 × (343 + 75) ms + 1.3 s + 68.4 s +
1.2 s ≈ 112.7 s; METROPOLIS 100 × (1 150 + 151) ms + 2.6 s + 76.5 s + 2.1 s ≈ 211.3 s. **The bounds, each the sum rounded
up to the next 5 s: TOWN ≤ 25 s, CITY ≤ 115 s, METROPOLIS ≤ 215 s** (J-V6.118; D-18: if E0 measures a sum above its bound,
the bound is re-declared at the measured sum for the owner's veto). The century runs as a background job with progress, never blocking the picture or an edit on another
document; the map redraws each epoch's `DisplayDelta` as it folds (the user watches the town grow). `advance.maxEpochs`
(200) bounds one request's epochs; a longer span bends its cadence (V06 §3.3.2).

**6.3 Replay at load, and the history replay at generation.** Under the `'history'` strategy an epoch's replay cost `c(e)` in §12.6's law is its compute above
plus its participants' (it is recomputed, not read) — BCR-V6-22 states it in §12.6 — so a century-old CITY document
verifies within §6.2's CITY century bound after load (its island events included), off the path to the picture (§12.6, §2.9 step 3), rebuilding the history store as it goes.
A `'grow-by-stage'` epoch keeps §12.6's scoped bound.
**The history replay at generation** (item 113; V06 §3.20.5). A coarse epoch costs its §6.1 and §6.2 rows with `hood.fabric`
and the Works' draws removed (lazy detail), scaled for the decade's larger change — the strike candidates, placements and
stage work grow with the span while the field, strain and re-binding do not: ≈ 1.5 × a one-year epoch (PLAUSIBLE until
E9 times it). With `replay.maxEpochs` at the peak's rung: **TOWN** ≤ 60 × 1.5 × 137 ms ≈ 12.3 s, bound **≤ 15 s**; **CITY**
≤ 40 × 1.5 × 418 ms ≈ 25.1 s, bound **≤ 30 s**; **METROPOLIS** ≤ 30 × 1.5 × 1 301 ms ≈ 58.5 s, bound **≤ 60 s**; a
village-rung peak at V07 §6's epoch row × 1.5 × 80. A refounding epoch adds its stages at §12.6's law (§6.2's growth-stage
rate); a burial adds V04 §6's `BuriedFormer.lay` row. The replay runs in the generation worker with progress, the map
drawing each replay epoch's `DisplayDelta` as it folds (the past unfolding), the present's picture at its end. The bounds
are T-V6-75's bar; their register line is V06 §8's (V12 §3.11). *Pre-decided fallback:* a lower `replay.maxEpochs` row
for the rung (a coarser step, never a different outcome at the present facts), declared for the owner's veto (D-18).

**6.4 Scrubbing and frames.** A cached frame ≤ 50 ms to the drawn delta at CITY, ≤ 120 ms at METROPOLIS; the images path
≤ 15 ms per event applied at CITY, ≤ 40 ms at METROPOLIS, so any frame within `history.checkpointStride` epochs of a
keyframe ≤ 300 ms at CITY and ≤ 800 ms at METROPOLIS; the effective-week before-images (V06 §3.15.2) add ≤ 5 ms per frame
at CITY; the replay path ≤ the epochs' compute, with progress. Play at one epoch per 0.5 s holds at CITY from cached or
image-built frames and at METROPOLIS from cached frames. *Pre-decided fallback:* the stride halves (5) while the store's
memory allows; failing that, play steps through keyframes only.

**6.5 Memory.** The history store ≤ the device's setting (V09 §9's settings panel) in memory, the
rest on the app's disk cache. An epoch's images ≈ 0.5–2 MB compressed at CITY (pass ≤ 1.5 MB mean) and ≈ 2–6 MB at
METROPOLIS (pass ≤ 4 MB mean) — so the desktop store holds ≈ 150 CITY or ≈ 60 METROPOLIS epochs before spilling at the desktop default; a
keyframe is the chunks changed since the previous one (≈ 5–15 MB at CITY, ≈ 15–40 MB at METROPOLIS over 10 epochs,
copy-on-write); frames ≤ the device's frame setting × the model's changed chunks. The retirement rule's extra year of
finished Works (BCR-V6-2) adds ≤ 2 % to the model at CITY. The shade mask: one bit per sample point and sun per 32 m
cell, read from the sampler's layout — by default one point and nine suns, 9 bits a cell (V05 §3.19 (1),
J-V5.42): ≈ 11 KB at CITY (≈ 10 000 cells), ≈ 45 KB at METROPOLIS (≈ 40 000 cells), each island arena ≤ 5 KB; if item
102's application raises the sampler to four points a cell, the bound is 36 bits a cell — ≈ 45 KB, ≈ 180 KB and ≤ 20 KB. An advance group's undo holds one checkpoint and the latest epoch's record (V06 §4.1), inside §7.4's
cap. The per-epoch `why` rows add ≤ 5 rows × ≈ 24 bytes per use op to the identity layer (≈ 1–3 KB per CITY epoch).
*Fallback:* images kept only at every 5th epoch (the others by replay from the nearest), reported.
