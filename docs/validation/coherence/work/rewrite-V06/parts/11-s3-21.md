### 3.21 Edges — every mechanism at its limits (principle (11))

Every outcome below is defined, sits on §5.1's failure ladder as V06 §3.17 applies it — check, repair locally, retry
that piece, simpler shape, substitute, leave out and report; **never a whole-map retry** — and is tested by the clause
named — T-V6-71 takes every row of 3.21.1–3.21.2 as a clause named by its mechanism's section, T-V6-72 every row of
3.21.3 by its letter. No edge ends in a crash, a hang, lost data, a silent wrong result or a whole-map retry.

**3.21.1 Rules that hold for every mechanism.**
1. **Degenerate geometry** (zero-length, zero-area, self-touching, collinear, coincident). Every change the engine makes
   is an op committed through the kernel's transactions by the hand path's operator (V06 §3.1 (4)); a result the kernel
   refuses (§3.1's `E_DEGENERATE`, `E_BELOW_MIN_EDGE`, `E_NON_SIMPLE` …) rolls that op back alone and the phase continues
   (V06 §3.17 (1): leave out and report). Before the op: a `StretchRef` or `SpanRef` whose two ends resolve to one point
   names nothing and its op is a no-op, reported; a zero-area caster footprint covers no raster cell and casts nothing;
   a course whose two ends coincide is no candidate (its reading unmet); a trace that self-touches takes F-WALL's ladder
   (§5.2). T-V6-71 (a).
2. **Missing, empty or contradictory data.** An optional field or row that is absent reads its migrate default (V06
   §2.12's column) — an absent `rows`, the engine's own rule; an absent mask, zero building shade; an absent `spring`, no
   spring. A dossier input the port cannot date applies finished with no Works (§4.11 *The clock* (2)); a calamity with
   no severity reads `minor`, reported; a calamity target the dossier does not list is skipped by F5, reported; an event
   `params.week` outside the span is clamped (V06 §3.11.1); a population target outside every registered ladder never
   reaches an epoch (§7.7 item 15) and a standalone one is clamped (V06 §3.3.2). T-V6-71 (b).
3. **An epoch of zero years** (a dossier epoch folded in the same week as the one before — the roster tolerance crossed
   within the week). Every per-year chance is `1 − (1 − p)^0 = 0` and every cap scaled by `years` is 0, so only the
   population's own steps act: departures and regrowth-first siting take no cap (V06 §3.5.1, §3.8.2) and the delta is
   housed; no strike, conversion, draw of a span or Works start happens; the report says so. T-V6-71 (c).
4. **Every rung.** The engine runs at every rung a registered product covers — THORP, HAMLET and VILLAGE through V07's
   product (its phases, rows, participants and records placement, V06 §3.1 (4)), TOWN, CITY and METROPOLIS through the
   base's — each feature gated by its own row at the epoch's rung (raised ways and islands at CITY and METROPOLIS, the
   catacomb count, sewers by the TOWN law, V06 §3.2); a target at the METROPOLIS cap is clamped there. With volume 7
   unregistered the clamp is the town product's floor (1 500). T-V6-71 (d) runs a trajectory through every rung.
5. **Toggles and presets.** A layer shown or hidden (the underground, the sky, the shade map, `live.history`) changes no
   epoch's output — views are outside every digest. The parity presets set `decline.turnoverVacancy` 0, the web's off
   form (V06 §3.9.10), `history.replay` off and item 111's neutral cells, and each has its no-op pin (T-V6-44, T-V6-65,
   T-V6-70, T-V6-74); `evolution.hazards` `none`, `minor`, `full` each run their rows only (V06 §3.11.1). T-V6-71 (e).
6. **Save, load, undo, redo, regenerate, migrate.** Every epoch is one event: its lifecycle is §7.4's epoch row with this
   volume's rows of V06 §2.12 and §4.1 (an `advance` group, the undo after a reload and in a shared document), T-V6-2,
   T-V6-22, T-V6-23 and T-V6-53 its bars; a mechanism with a case of its own names it in 3.21.2.
