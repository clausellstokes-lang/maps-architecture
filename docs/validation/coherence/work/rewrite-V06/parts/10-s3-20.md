### 3.20 The history replay at generation (item 113)

§4.11 *History-first generation* states what a settlement with a known history becomes: generated at its peak (or its
founding), its recorded past run forward as coarse epochs to the present, the present facts met exactly. This section
is the engine that runs it. It runs at generation, after S0–S13a, when `history.replay` is on (the base's switch; off
under the parity presets) and the recipe carries a history (`Recipe.history`, a `HistoryHint`, §2.7: V08 fills it from
the dossier, V09's editor from the blueprint's history option, §4.2 S0) — at every rung a registered product covers
(THORP to the METROPOLIS cap; the village rungs by V07's product), dossier-bound or standalone. It never runs on a
standalone neighbourhood document (V06 §4.1).

**3.20.1 The plan** (`HistoryReplay.plan`, V06 §2.14; a pure function of the history and the pinned tables — no draw).
1. **No replay** (the plan is `null`; generation is the base's alone, the report's `replay` entry names the reason):
   `history.replay` off; no history; a founding after the present (bad data, reported); **no peak** — no evidence of a
   higher tier (DR36), or a peak at or below the present population (§4.11 *History-first generation* (7)). The history's
   events are then the base's soft hints only (§4.2 S2's stage count, II.7.6's catacomb age and buried old town).
2. **The peak** (DR36): SettlementForge's `peakTier` with its high-water population; else the **effective peak** — the
   higher tier the evidence shows, sized at V11's `history.symbolicQuantile` of that tier's population range and marked
   `symbolic`. A peak above the registered ladders' top is sized at the top (§1.1). **Its time** (`peakYearsAgo`): the
   years ago the history names for it — the demotion from the peak tier in `tierHistory`, the maximum of
   `populationHistory`, or the destruction for a peak ended by one — else `replay.peakAtShare` of the span from the
   founding (V06 §2.2).
3. **The start.** The replay starts at the **founding** (`startsAt: 'founding'`, `ageYears` before the present) when the
   history records the growth — a `populationHistory` or `tierHistory` point older than `peakYearsAgo` — and the founding
   lies at least one epoch before the peak; its first generation runs at the founding population (the oldest recorded
   point, else `replay.foundingShare` × the peak's population), clamped to the lowest registered ladder's floor. Otherwise
   it starts **at the peak** (`startsAt: 'peak'`; the span from `peakYearsAgo`), the first generation at the peak's
   population: no growth is invented that the history does not record.
4. **The epochs.** With `S` the span in years and `E` V11's `history.epochYears` (a decade by default), `R = ⌈S ÷ E⌉`;
   when `R` exceeds `replay.maxEpochs` at the peak's rung, `E` bends to `⌈S ÷ replay.maxEpochs⌉` whole years (`bent`,
   reported, and the creation card's line says so: "a 600-year history runs in 20-year steps"). Epoch `r` (1 … R, oldest
   first) spans `(toYearsAgo, fromYearsAgo]` with `fromYearsAgo = S − (r − 1) E` and `toYearsAgo = max(0, S − r E)`; a span
   shorter than one epoch is one epoch. **The peak epoch** is the one whose span holds `peakYearsAgo`.
5. **The targets.** Each epoch's `populationTarget` is the population at its `toYearsAgo`: inside the range
   `populationHistory` records, its linear interpolation in years; else, before the peak epoch, V06 §3.3.2's logistic from
   the start population to the peak's (`f` the elapsed share of the span to the peak); after it, the same logistic from
   the peak's population down to the present population; the peak epoch's target is the peak's population and **the last
   epoch's is the present population exactly** (the dossier's, or the recipe's). A `shock` effect (6) lowers its epoch's
   target by its share of the population before it, the curve rejoining at the next recorded point or target. Each target
   is clamped to the union of the registered ladders (V06 §3.3.2), a clamp reported; an **abandoned** epoch (7) has no
   target.
6. **The events** (DR37). Every history event — `history.events` (type, years ago, severity), `calamityHistory`'s entries
   and the recipe's `HistoryHint.scars` — goes to the epoch whose span holds its `yearsAgo`; an event older than the span's
   start is recorded as before the replay (not played: its scar is the base's soft hint) and one at `yearsAgo` 0 is the
   present's (its facts are the dossier's). Events are mapped by V11's event-to-epoch rows (`history.events`, keyed by
   type) to their `ReplayEffect`s (V06 §2.14) at the event's severity; an event whose type has no row is reported
   (`replayEventUnmapped`) and played as nothing. Several events in one epoch keep their order (years ago, oldest first,
   then type).
7. **Destruction and refounding** (DR38). An epoch holding a `destroy` effect — a terminal death, an ancient "Fall of"
   event on the site, or an event whose V11 row maps its catastrophic severity to `destroy` — is **destroyed**. When the
   history records a resettlement (or the settlement stands again at a later target), the epoch holding the resettlement
   carries `refound` with its target as the population and the history's culture row from then on; the epochs strictly
   between the two are **abandoned**. With no recorded gap, the destroying epoch also refounds. A destruction in the last
   epoch always refounds there, at the present population. Two destructions are two cycles, each by this rule.

**3.20.2 The first generation.** S0–S13a run once at the plan's start population and rung (the product V07 §2.1's fold
gives), on the present site (terrain and water are the present's; the replay's terrain phase changes them as live epochs
do), with the history's founding culture row where it names one, else the present's. The dossier's present institution
records are **withheld** from this generation: their `assign` events are carried by the last replay epoch's `dossier`
step instead (3.20.7), so the town of the past holds only what its own roster at that population derives.

**3.20.3 The epochs in the log.** Each plan epoch is one `epoch` event appended to the **creation prefix** (flagged
`creation`, §7.4 — never an undo target) and folded at once by this volume's runner (V06 §3.1) with `ctx.replay` set: its
`EpochInputs` carry `week` = the creation week − `round(52 × toYearsAgo)` (a week before the creation week, negative on a
standalone map whose creation week is 0 — every reader takes spans as differences, and the calendar row takes the week's
floor modulus), the target, the epoch's slice (the recipe's, with every earlier `slice` effect applied) and the events of
(6) as `EpochInputs.events` entries (3.20.4); `years` is the epoch's span. Every stream carries the epoch component
`0x8000 + r` (§6.1; V06 §3.16). The strategy is `'history'` whatever `evolution.strategy` says (a document created with a
replay is created at the output version that ships this engine). The last epoch's week is the creation week, so the
document's current week is the present's, the `HistoryIndex.replay` range lists the epochs, and the timeline bar scrubs
the past like any history (V06 §3.15.2, §3.15.4).

**3.20.4 What a replay epoch runs.** Every step of V06 §3.2 and every participant, as a live epoch, with these differences:
1. **Recorded events only.** Spontaneous hazards never run (as on a dossier-bound document, J-V6.19): the past holds the
   calamities its history records and no invented ones. The effects of (6) enter as follows: `hazard` — an
   `EpochInputs.events` entry of that `HazardRow` kind at the event's week with the row's params (a `drought` included,
   V06 §3.12.1); `calamity` — a `calamity` entry whose `CalamityRef` is synthesized from the event (its id the event's,
   no targets, severity by band, `deaths` = `deathShare` × the epoch's population × severity), typed by the calamity table
   (V06 §2.4) like any; `slice` — the epoch's slice from this epoch on; `demand` — the class's deficit raised by `factor` ×
   its roster count in this epoch's `uses` phase (a hostel for a pilgrimage surge), then the ordinary rules; `fortify` — a
   circuit (or a citadel, `kind` `citadel`) triggered at this epoch's `growth` phase by §3.7.6 (4)'s walls-record path
   (around the stage grown, else the present built boundary), a threat of at least `walls.threatMin` set in the slice;
   `shock` — the target of (5); `destroy` — (4) below.
2. **The dossier gate in the past.** On a dossier-bound replay the walls gate (V06 §3.7.6 (4)) admits the enceintes the
   present records list **including those listed ruined or remnant** (the history's walls: a village holding a ruined
   "City walls and gates" had them), so the peak's walls can stand; the present state of each record is applied at the
   last epoch (3.20.7).
3. **Lazy detail** (§4.1's contract). `hood.fabric` returns no ops: a close-up's fabric is a lazily derived rung, derived
   from the present state on demand. The `works` phase writes no Works: every change of a replay epoch is drawn finished
   from its effective week (the epoch's week, V06 §2.11), so nothing of the past is under construction today. Everything
   else — the shade mask, the field, the ladder, uses, walls, raised ways, hazards, terrain and water, the underground
   participants (V04's sewers reopening closed runs before digging new ones, item 113 (d), J-V4.29; a void whose last
   open link closes turning `lost`, item 113 (e), V04 §3.5.7 (g)), the islands — runs in full.
4. **Destruction** (item 113 (b)). In the destroyed epoch's `hazards` step, in one batch per kind, cause `rule:history`:
   every live building takes `ruin` (existence `ruined`), its uses closed and its households leaving with the population
   (the fall to the refounded target, or to none); every way takes `effaceWay` (existence `decayed`: its line stays as a
   trace, off every router); every enceinte takes `neglect` and a `breach` of every run (its `condition` all `breached`);
   raised parts, retaining walls and decks their pool strikes (`breach`, `ruin`); land uses and the underground stay as
   they are (the fade runs on them as on any epoch). An **abandoned** epoch runs only `prelude`, `decline` (the strike pool
   over the ruins with no salvage demand, so ruins stand; `under.fade`), `terrainWater`, the burial of (5) and `close`; no
   household exists and every other step runs nothing.
   **Refounding.** The refounding epoch's target is `refound.population`; its slice takes `refound.culture`; its `siting`
   reoccupies nothing (no standing house) and rebuilds on the ruins first — regrowth tier (3)'s `rebuild` and `reuse …
   build` on old foundations, the placement rule reopening each effaced way a placement needs (`reopenWay`, V06 §3.8.2) —
   and its `growth` grows new stages whose routes are priced by the lattice's `existingFabricCost` with a trace at V11's
   `history.reuseCost` (§4.11 *History-first generation* (4)), so the new settlement reuses the old roads, gate gaps and
   bridges where they serve and may follow its own plan elsewhere; the salvage ledger credits the ruins' stone to its
   building (§4.11 *Salvage*). At village rungs V07's product phases do this (V07 §3.12).
5. **Burial** (item 113 (c); DR39). Last act of every replay epoch's `decline` step (after the strike pool and
   `under.fade`): the **former fabric** — the records the replay left ruined or effaced that no later record reuses, as
   V04 §3.5.5 (b) lists them (a way trace no live way follows, a ruined building whose footprint no live building takes,
   a `dismantled` or ruined enceinte stretch with its gates, a former square) — whose ruin age (the years since its
   lifecycle's `ruined`, `decayed` or `dismantled` entry, at the epoch's week) has first passed V11's `history.burialYears`
   × the site's burial factors (shorter for floods, river silt and rebuilding on top, longer on bare rock) is passed, in
   address order, to V04's `BuriedFormer.lay(doc, epoch, former)`; in the same batch this volume tombstones those surface
   records (cause `rule:burial`) and adds the returned system id to the record's `buried` list. `null` (nothing layable)
   buries the surface records all the same and is reported (V04's `W_BURIED_FORMER_SKIPPED`). Live epochs after the
   creation never bury: a live history's ruins stand until the strike pool clears them (J-V6.131).
6. **Symbolic provenance** (item 113 (f)). Under a symbolic peak, every entity created by the first generation or by a
   replay epoch up to and including the peak epoch carries `Provenance.symbolic` (§2.8) — derived, like all provenance,
   from its creation event's place in the prefix and `HistoryRecord.peak.symbolic` — so the clerk says its exact size is
   not recorded, and every export carries the flag (V06 §5.3).

**3.20.5 The generation budget.** The plan fixes the replay's cost before it runs: `replay.maxEpochs` by the peak's rung
bounds the epoch count (4), and each replay epoch runs within V06 §6's per-epoch budgets at its rung with lazy detail
(3.20.4 (3)), so a replay's wall time is bounded by V06 §6.3's rows — a deterministic bound, never a clock reading, so
the replay stays byte-stable. The replay runs in the generation worker with a progress line over its epochs ("replaying
600 years: year 340"), the map drawing each replay epoch as it folds and the present's picture at its end (V06 §6.3). A cancelled generation cancels the
replay with it (nothing is created, as any cancelled generation). Failures are each epoch's own ladder (V06 §3.17): a
refused op is skipped and reported; a participant is isolated; **never a whole-map retry**.

**3.20.6 The record.** In the last replay epoch's `close`, the replay writes the `HistoryRecord` (§2.7): `peak` (the rung,
the population, `symbolic`, the evidence fields strongest first), `epochs` (one row per plan epoch: `epoch` = r, its
span, its events with type, years ago and severity), `refounded` (each destruction's years ago and cause) and `buried`
(the system ids of (5)). It is model data, one per document, written only by the replay (no edit kind); the chronicle
lines of the replay epochs (`destroyed`, `refounded`, `buried` among them, V06 §3.15.1) are its narrative, rebuilt by
replay. V10 tells the story from it (V06 §4.5).

**3.20.7 The present met exactly.** The last replay epoch's target is the present population (the roster meets it,
§4.11 (2)), and its `dossier` step carries every present record the first generation withheld (3.20.2): each is placed
by §7.7's *One path* — bound claims-first to what the replay built where the record matches it (a record listed ruined
binds the ruined church or walls the replay left, F5 marking it so; a remnant cathedral binds the oversized church) —
and the dossier's present facts win over anything the replay made (item 20: history shapes only what the dossier does
not say). The standalone blueprint's history option meets the recipe's present population the same way.

**3.20.8 Lifecycle.** *Persisted*: the replay epochs in the log's creation prefix and the `HistoryRecord`; the plan is
recomputed. *Load*: the log replays byte-identically. *Undo*: none (the creation prefix). *Regenerate* and *New random*
re-run the replay from the recipe's history — the same seed reproduces it, a new seed makes a different town with the
same past. *A later change of the dossier's past* (a history fact the host rewrites after creation) is a recipe input:
the port's diff offers "Regenerate from the new history" (V08 §3.5's history row) and nothing re-runs silently.
*Migrate*: a document created before this engine has no replay and no `HistoryRecord`; an upgrade never adds one.

**3.20.9 Edges.** Each is defined above and tested by T-V6-77: no peak, a founding after the present, a peak at or below
the present → no replay (1); a peak above the cap → sized at the cap (2); a span shorter than one epoch → one epoch (4); an
epoch count above `replay.maxEpochs` → the bent step (4); an event before the span or at the present → not played (6); an
unmapped event → reported, nothing played (6); a destruction with no recorded resettlement in the last epoch → refounded
there at the present population, and one mid-history with no later settlement → the epochs after it abandoned until the
last, which refounds (7); a refounded target below the only registered ladder's floor → clamped and reported (5); a
dossier present fact contradicting what the replay built → the present fact wins at the last epoch (3.20.7); a village
rung with volume 7 unregistered → targets clamped to the town product's floor, reported. The bars of the replay are
T-V6-73–T-V6-77 (V06 §7), with V04's T-V4-76 and T-V4-77 for the lost reading and the buried former settlement.
