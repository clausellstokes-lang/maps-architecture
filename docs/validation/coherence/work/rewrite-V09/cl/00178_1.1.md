### 1.1 What this volume builds

`@plat/app` at build depth: the standalone editor (entry `index.html`) and the **shell** that both it and the host mode
of V08 (entry `embed.html`, item 507) run on. The shell is one program with one set of screens; a host mode is a
`ShellMode` (V09 §2.2) that removes or locks what V08 §5.1 names, and adds nothing this volume does not declare as an
extension slot. Concretely, this volume settles:

1. **The screens** — the empty state and the new-town flow (V09 §5.2); the editor frame (top bar, tool rail, map region
   with its overlays, context panel, view bar, drawers; V09 §5.1); the panels (selection and its tabs, tool options, the
   report, history with restore points, the gazetteer and "features in view", the advanced panel and the tables editor,
   style and dress, names); the sheets and dialogs (confirm the fates, export in five families, share, settings, open and
   recent towns, the keymap overlay, about); the clerk drawer; the overlays (ghost, handles, count badge, snap
   highlight, the variations tray, the bend line, the refusal card, first-use hints, toasts); the thematic views the owner's
   file-g rules ask the interface for (the shade map, the supply links, the siting "Why here?" card).
2. **The flows** — boot and open; the first map; the declaration pipeline (gesture → draft → preview → ghost → commit or
   cancel) with its latency budget; confirmation of fates and renames; variations and scoped re-rolls; undo, redo,
   restore points and "open a copy from here"; autosave with crash recovery; export; share; settings; accepting or
   refusing a clerk's proposal.
3. **The input model** — pointer, pen, touch and keyboard, one gesture grammar across them; the responsive classes and
   what a phone may do (decided: V09 §4.12, J-V9.9); accessibility beyond §14's contract.
4. **The tool binding** — every edit event kind of the base and of volumes 1–8 reached from exactly one home tool of
   §7.1's twelve (and optionally a context-panel control), in one table (V09 §4.3), with a completeness test (T-V9-12).
5. **The words** — the message catalogue: every refusal, bend, conflict, fate, error and hint in plain words (V09 §4.9,
   §2.9).
6. **The acceptance machinery** — the first-five-minutes protocol in operational detail, the owner's UX sitting on
   §13.6's protocol shape, and the known-city fixture authored through the canvas by a gesture script (V09 §7).
7. **File g's interface parts** — the shade map view (item 102 (f)); the siting explanations (items 103 (e), 104 (a)); the
   link view and link edits (item 105 (j)); the wall tool's snapping to natural barriers and its barrier-arc ghost (item
   106 (g)); a collective record's member list (item 108 (a)–(b)); a chain named as a working quarter only past item 72's
   threshold (item 104 (h) (3) as revised by item 107 (e)). The base mechanisms of those items are file g's; this volume
   designs only their screens and gestures. The declarations the edits store are volume 11's document-only tables
   (V11 §4.2), which the screens write by `editTable` (V09 §2.6), and every answer a panel shows is volume 10's
   `WhyAnswer` (V10 §2.13), projected for the card (V09 §2.7).
8. **Item 109's screens** (file g, as revised ~20:05, J111) — the game master's view of sealed caverns and ruins (V09 §4.17.1); the system chip that draws a gallery or chamber into a named system and starts a new ruin, cavern or
   catacomb (V09 §4.17.2); how each way down came about and the path in (V09 §4.17.3); dwellings only after a way in (V09 §4.17.4); the catacomb, crypt and cemetery panels, with their bends (V09 §4.17.5); the dossier facts they show (V09 §1b).
9. **The other volumes' screens** — volume 10's drawer view model, key entry, data notice, usage meter, settings,
   creation preview, import critique and transcript export (V09 §4.11, V09 §3.12, V09 §5.10, V09 §5.12); volume 6's "Re-run from here",
   chronicle and use-history tables, animated history and history import (V09 §3.9, V09 §3.12, V09 §5.10); volume 2's massing
   handles and family strip (V09 §3.5); volume 5's shade strips and "Update shade now" (V09 §5.4); volume 7's whole-circuit
   view (V09 §5.4); volume 1's coverage and voids views (V09 §5.4); and the base's view keys, people search, family tree,
   clock, notes, pins and ruler that no other volume places (V09 §4.18, V09 §4.19).
10. **Item 110's tools** (file g item 110, as revised ~23:40; V09 §4.20; J-V9.58) — T3's **Retaining wall** mode and T2's
   **Terrace** mode (V09 §4.20.1); T4's **Raised** toggle and **Aqueduct** chip (V09 §4.20.2); removing a generated raised
   way, and building it again (V09 §4.20.3); T6 on deck faces and at a mole's landmark slots (V09 §4.20.4); T9's tier
   controls (V09 §4.20.5); the panels, handles, span and crossing controls and why lines of the new records (V09 §4.20.6);
   what else a change does, in words, on every preview (V09 §4.20.7; item 465); reaching what lies under a raised deck
   (V09 §4.20.8; item 466); item 110 on the Sky layer (V09 §4.20.9); the item-110 exports (V09 §5.10; item 447); an
   author's removals in the skeleton (V09 §3.12; item 455); and the second known-city fixture through the canvas (V09
   §7.2). The events these tools send are item 362's.
