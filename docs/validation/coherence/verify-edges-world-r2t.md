### VEW-r2-05 — MAJOR, EDGE-UNDEFINED, owner pipeline

A siege's places read "the outermost (standing) enceinte". That phrase is undefined, or wrong, wherever a place's
enceintes are not one nested series:
* `beyondWalls` (l.15376), `outside` (l.15383) and `edge` (l.15388) all read it;
* "a `siege` of a place with no standing enceinte draws the `blockade` row" (l.15408).

`Enceinte.kind` includes `bailey` (§2.7, l.2236; `Castle.curtain`), so:
* **An unwalled town with a castle** has a standing enceinte, the castle's curtain. Its siege draws the trench and
  earthwork line "round the walled arcs at the standoff", about 300 m round the castle, across the town's own plots and
  buildings. The owner's text says the opposite: "an unwalled place gets blockade camps on its approach roads"

  (F-SITE's "walls apart by the floor", l.8965), an `aroundStage` ring (§2.7, l.2242).
* **Extramural suburbs.** The siege line is kept off "water, a way's carriageway, a gate's passage or a label's keep-out"
  only ((d), l.15370–15371), so it is drawn across extramural ribbon plots and buildings within the standoff of an
  ordinary walled town.

**Required change.** §10.2 (d):
* "The walls a siege reads are the place's enceintes of kinds other than `bailey`. A castle's curtain counts only along its
  shared stretch with the town wall (`Castle.cityWeld`), and its keep is `gatesAndKeep`'s."
* "'Outermost' is the outer envelope of their walled arcs: for rings that are not nested, the walled arcs of each ring that
  no other ring encloses."
* "A place with no standing enceinte" reads "no standing town enceinte", so an unwalled town with a castle draws the
  `blockade` on its approach roads.
* The siege line and every camp are never drawn on a building footprint; burned farmsteads draw on their own. The line is
  broken where it would cross an extramural plot's building.

twin town with two rings; a walled town with extramural ribbons inside the standoff. Bar: 0 look pixels on a building
