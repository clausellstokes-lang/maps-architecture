## What was run and held

Each op of the lens was run through laws 1 – 4 as pass 7 left them; these decided one outcome and are no finding.
* **The form over time (W4, W5, W8, W10, W11, W12 × C2 – C6, C10).** Law 1 (2) reads the section's top, the deck's level
  and each span's recorded soffit, so decay, a breach, a dismantling, a landfall, a fall and a clearing leave every

  repair of a cleared pier in a merged crossing raises a new part whose supports (3) slides off the band, its arch merged —
  refused where (3) and (4) cannot place it (C10, the support step's own derivation).
* **Re-sections (W14).** A re-section moves the top, so the form is read again: over → merged with a gate under the arch
  waits or is refused; merged → over with a pier in the band is re-traced locally, else waits or is refused; the
  REPLACEMENT never falls to its "new ring" branch (V07 l. 2288 – 2291: its own rule did not pick it).
* **The frame growth (C12).** It runs at the epoch's prelude, before row 9 and row 14: the extension keeps the deck's course

  old course's date, so a ring the same epoch's growth lays later passes beneath a road deck's extension (law 1 (3)) or
  crosses an aqueduct's by level, the ring the newer.
* **Tier 1 inside an epoch.** Row 9's gates, sea gates and chains are held against rows 10a and 14 (law 1 (11) "laid by an
  earlier batch"); a burial, a `gateToJunction`, a `wallToWay` and an absorbed run's last dismantle remove their gates by
