## Verdict

Neither table is complete yet, though Part P closed most of pass 1. The crossing table still lacks five cases. Two raised
ways at one wall have no union rule (PRB7B-01). A held building's span carried under a wall by the raised way's own re-shape
has no outcome (PRB7B-05). C10's repaired road-deck span over a later wall is refused by C1 where V06 rebuilds it
(PRB7B-06). The amended *down before the wall* contradicts row (d) at an epoch's new walled stage (PRB7B-04). A retaining

amended `vault` reading is contradicted by its own home at §4.3 and by every reader (PRB7B-11). Of the three cross-cutting
sentences: the lock sentence's reach leaves a carve or a moved river across a drawn wall undecided (PRB7B-07), and its
(G3) ladders are split or rungless for a river-moved run (PRB7B-08) and out of order at F-WALL (PRB7B-09). The bent-group
sentence's gate rule clears two different strips, and points where footprints stand (PRB7B-02); *Line and band* never
reached the pull's homes (PRB7B-12). The fixed-place sentence gives the castle's weld vertices three mechanisms and omits
its other fixed places (PRB7B-03), and its non-generation paths are untested (PRB7B-13). Thirteen majors remain, none a
blocker. The family ruling is therefore not yet complete enough for repair. A third probe pass should follow the amend
seat's decisions on these. Every major here closes locally — a union sentence, a footprint sentence, one castle sentence,
one epoch clause, one held-building sentence, one repair exception, one reach clause, one F-REDERIVE rung, one F-WALL
order, one retaining-wall clause, and the readers' citations and test clauses listed — and none reopens J144's rule or a
Part P decision.
