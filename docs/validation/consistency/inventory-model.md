## Internal conflicts (the part states one fact two ways)

### MODEL-C01 — split-flag clearing by a move (minor)

* **A:** §2.2 Plat.vertices.flags comment: split is cleared "by a move that takes it off that edge's route or — for a vertex of that route's own level — moves the vertex beside it on that route"
* **B:** §2.2 rule 3 (b)/(b′): cleared by any move (warp or smoothing) "that displaces the vertex, except a Steiner vertex re-seated onto its coarser edge's route and a vertex sliding along a fixed route"; (b′) the neighbour-move clause
* **Difference:** The comment conditions clearing on leaving the route; the rule clears on any displacement except two named cases. A warp that moves the vertex together with its route (neither a re-seat nor a slide) clears the flag under the rule but arguably not under the comment.
* **Proposed canonical statement:** kernel §3.6 (split-flag mechanism); §2.2 should state the flag and point to §3.6
* **Inventory rows:** MODEL-074, MODEL-021
