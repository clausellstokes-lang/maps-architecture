## Internal conflicts

### C-1 (minor) — §6.1 l. 5250 and l. 5264 vs §6.3 l. 5483

- **Statement A.** §6.1: the entity key mixes the seed key, the lineage hash, the layer and the ISLAND; the lineage component is (layer, island, derivation unit, stage, creation epoch, creation index).
- **Statement B.** §6.3: the lineage key is (arena key = layer and PART, the derivation unit's LINEAGE HASH, stage, creation epoch, creation index, the derivation unit's REROLL [, dup]).
- **Proposed canonical home.** §6.3 (identity owns the lineage key); §6.1 should reference it. The differences: island vs part, derivation unit vs its lineage hash, and §6.1 omits the reroll and dup components.
- **Items.** DETERMINISM-138, DETERMINISM-145, DETERMINISM-210

### C-2 (minor) — §5.1 (6) l. 5084 vs §7.1 named actions l. 5892

- **Statement A.** §5.1 (6): an unchanged scope entity advances its reroll up to reroll.maxAdvance times (a table value, default 3).

- **Items.** DETERMINISM-020, DETERMINISM-021, DETERMINISM-344

### C-3 (minor) — §5.2 F-REDERIVE l. 5140 vs §7.3 (b) 2 l. 6185-6191
