## 1 VHM1-03 — a tower's spiral core and its round room overlapped as fixed rings (ruling, widened)

**Adopted.** The verifier's ruling, which is PLAUSIBLE and vetoable, follows the realism tiebreaker. The spiral core's bay
stands inside the tower's round room, against its wall, and the plan rung passes the round room less that bay. Where the
bay fills the room (a stair turret), it passes the bay alone.

**Widened.** The defect is one shape: a fixed ring that step 3 places inside a composer's room, which is itself a fixed
ring (§3.4 refuses the overlap). The tower's spiral is one instance. A hall that a composer emits is another:

* its hearth's chimney stack must stand at the hall's own wall, and on an exterior wall that places it inside the hall;
* a stair bay in a wing whose storey is one composer's hall has no candidate outside the hall.

Both reach F-PLAN tier 2, then tier 6: the chimney is omitted, or the upper storeys are left unreached. So the rule is
stated once, for any stair core's bay or chimney stack inside a composer's room. The ring stands against that room's wall,
meeting its sides along one run and nowhere else, or fills the room. Step 3 filters its candidates to that. At each such
storey the plan rung passes the room less the ring, or no room where the ring fills it. "Along one run and nowhere else"
is the exact condition for the room less the ring to be one ring without a hole.

**Where the text goes.**

* The stage-3 contract holds the rule, as a sentence after the tower paragraph and before "Every composer's room…". It
  sits on its own source line to keep a chance C4 hit off l. 1266 (§4).
* Short pointers keep the glosses exact: step 3 (the placement), step 4 and the re-cut bullet (the fixed-ring lists: "the
  composer's rooms, each less a bay or stack it holds") and step 6 (the tower gloss).
* J-A28.26's index row carries the verifier's addition, reworded for the widening. The Part A entry carries the
  verifier's reasons and adds the widening with its reason.

**Consistency checked.**

  and the ring share sides only, and both lie inside the room's seed piece.

  includes the filter. Its "a refused composer's room … is a bug" stays true.
