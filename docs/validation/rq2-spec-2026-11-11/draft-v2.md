## The design text, final wording (the session writes it into docs/ARCHITECTURE.md BEFORE the launch)

**(c) 1 — §2.2 rule 3, *The `split` flag* paragraph (today :748-754), after the sentence "A chain's own shape vertices
never carry it."**
> A vertex that is both — a chain's own point landed on another chain's run it shares on both sides (§3.11's collinear
> overlap) — keeps the flag and carries a vertex row `(shape, chain)` that the chain's removal strips: the row holds the
> vertex while the chain lives (the dissolution rule's "no vertex role"), and its removal lets the flag restore the
> other chain's edge exactly. `insertChain` writes the row at the chain's commit, on each own landed point that carries
> the flag and through which the chain passes once — the chain's rows on exactly two of the vertex's incident edges,
> another chain's rows on both of them — whatever its degree. Every batch keeps the row only while that still holds and
> the vertex keeps its flag, and strips it otherwise; when no other chain's row remains on either side and nothing else
> meets the vertex, the row is stripped and the flag cleared: the vertex is the chain's own shape vertex. The guard is
> stated for a chain inserted onto another chain's run and removed whole or by a stretch through the point.

**(c) 2 — §2.2 rule 3 (b) *flags* (today :768-769), the sentence "A vertex from which the batch strips its last vertex
role row other than knot rows loses its kernel `pinned` flag, since a pin guards the feature its role names …"** —
replace "other than knot rows" by:
> other than knot rows and `shape` rows

**(c) 3 — §2.2 rule 7.** (i) In the table's vertex row (today :914), after "*`deck_order_knot`* (`RaisedWayRecord.courseAt`;
both §2.7's laws' *Order*)", before the closing "|":
> ; and the kernel-internal `shape` (`('shape', entity, 0)`: a chain's own point on another chain's run it passes
> through once, rule 3; written by `insertChain` alone and kept or stripped by every batch — no stage, record or caller
> writes or names it; not indexed; registry version 2)

(ii) After the sentence "… never by an interned handle, whose value depends on the order in which a session interned
it." (today :909):
> The registry is append-only: a later version appends rows at the next id and the next rank, so a later row reads
> after every earlier one.

**(c) 4 — §3.4 *The doors* (today :4858-4864), after "`removeEdges` on a `VertexRef` the door for stripping one (an S8
portal whose lane is removed, §4.6; a tower `wallToWay` removes, §4.11; a profile writer's dropped knot)":**
> ; the kernel-internal `shape` row (§2.2 rule 3) has no caller's door — `insertChain` writes it at a chain's commit,
> and it is stripped only by the re-levelling of `removeEdges` (by `ChainRef` or `StretchRef`), `clearFiner` and
> `retypeEdges` (`remove`, `rekey`) and by the commit of a batch that clears a split flag where it stands

**(c) 5 — §3.4 *Stretches (J-K31)* (today :4906), after "… before the stretch's rows and its interior knot rows leave":**
> (a `shape` row of the chain at the stretch's vertices is then kept or stripped by §2.2 rule 3)

**(c) 6 — §3.4 *Arenas, keys and services* (today :4917-4919), after "valid until that arena's next commit (§2.2 rule 5)":**
> ; a copy of an arena — compacted on save or loaded (§3.9) — is another arena state, on which no slot or stamp read on
> the original is current (the copy takes a fresh, process-local restore generation compared by equality only; nothing
> is stored in the block)

**(c) 7 — §3.3 *The commit record* (today :4648-4649), after "of the display list's dirty set (`faces`, `owners`; §8.1)":**
> — `faces` lists every record a batch created, changed or removed at each level ≥ its own, a record re-parented by
> the re-walk (§3.6 step 5) or by the re-levelling of `removeEdges`, `retypeEdges` and `clearFiner` among the changed

**(c) 8 — §3.3 *The commit record*, after its last sentence "Commit granularity is part of every stage's declaration
(§4.1), so the ≈ 48 s cost of per-insertion commits, and its per-batch twin, cannot arise." (today :4656):**
> Between two ops of a batch each level's live face records own disjoint rings: `insertChain`, `removeEdges`,
> `retypeEdges`' `rekey`, `clearFiner` and `setOwner` each return — committed op or refused — with no ring claimed by
> two live records, so `faceNeighbours` called between them reads one record per region.

**(c) 9 — §3.9, after "An arena that holds only its outer ring writes its level-≤ 1 chunk alone and loads to that one
face." (today :5545):**
> Each chunk names the role registry version it was written under; a load reads a chunk of that version or an earlier one
> (rows are append-only, §2.2 rule 7) — the byte format's reading, not a promise that an older cache's content is
> current — and refuses a later one as a corrupt cache.
