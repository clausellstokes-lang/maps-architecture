## 1 The design (coherence-first)

### 1.1 One sentence

A world site's kind row carries its **coherence expectation** — its road access and the terms its kind implies — and
V13's P1, in a **site form**, measures it as two site terms of the one `CoherenceCost`; an unlawful drop or move is
**autocorrected by the kind's own rule** (the site moved to the nearest lawful point, the road brought to it, or the
site held and its gap reported) within the kind's radius, named in the dry run, and offered beyond it; the road that
lawfulness requires is a `WorldRoute` — a leg, a host route the site stands on or is re-laid through, or a site trail
— that every road reader already reads, continued to the site's entrance inside its frame and drawn by the crop, so
the correction holds at the world, the site frame and the encounter scale (item 135).

### 1.2 The words (one word, one meaning)

**Reused unchanged:** `WorldSite`, `attraction`, `routed` (a seat of W7's graph, V13:1713–1716), `SuitabilityTerm` and
V14's seventeen plug-ins (V14:984–992), `CoherenceCost`, `lawfulMax`, `nearestLawful` (overloaded on the subject, never
renamed), `PlacementAnomaly`, `Placement.hostLocked` (V13:746), `keep` / `takeOffer` / `cancel` (`W_PLACE_NO_LAWFUL`),
`keep` / `worldWins` (`W_PLACE_ANOMALY`), `accept` (as `W_PLACEMENT_OVERLAP`'s), `worldWins` on a site = its

### 1.4 The coherence expectation as cost terms (V13 §3.11.1a — P1's site form)

`siteCoherence(world, site, origin)` returns the `CoherenceCost` whose terms are `siteAccess` (the card's `roadAccess`
against the routes of the network in force other than site trails, a hidden road no public road: `on` 0 when a
polyline lies within `onWithinM`, `onRiverCrossing` 0 when a route–reach crossing does, `served` 0 standalone and
host-bound within `autocorrectM` of a host route, `reached` 0 when a routed cell lies within `trailMaxM`, `none` 0,
`shunned` 1 when a route lies within `shunWithinM`; else 1) and one `siteExpect` row per `expect` entry
(max(0, `min` − value) ÷ `min`, the value the greatest of its terms by V14's plug-ins at the origin's fine cell);
`total = Σ w · raw`, `lawful = total ≤ lawfulMax`. A site has no bearing search (G3 (a)'s bearing, re-read after a
move). The frame's seven terms read nothing for a site (it has no frame to measure). Text: fragment C-18.

### 1.6 Road integration — what lawfulness requires, and what is laid, at every scale (item 135)

**World (V13 §3.8).** `on`, `onRiverCrossing` and `served` kinds are W7 seats as today (`routed`); on a standalone
world W7 lays their legs by (4)–(5) — and now **with the drop** (V13 §4.3's site row, fragment C-23; V14's drop row,

host adopting by its re-canonize). A **site placement is W7's terminal at its origin** (W7 (11), J-V13.114): the
cell-centre path's last vertex is replaced by the origin, so the route crosses the site frame's ring at a **station**
(the charter road row's `atCm`, V13:726, P4 (6) V13:2156–2160). **Site trails** (W7 (12)): every `reached` site within
`trailMaxM` of a routed cell gets one `trail`-class `WorldRoute` of origin `siteTrail` — on either world, map-tier (no
host key, no edge between settlements), on the route layer for the charters, the view, the crop and the travel law, and
no road to `existingRoute`, `connectivity`, W8b's patrols, the simulator feed or V14's siting terms (C-16, C-36).
