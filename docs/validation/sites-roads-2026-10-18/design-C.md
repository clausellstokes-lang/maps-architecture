## 1 The design (coherence-first)

### 1.1 One sentence

A world site's kind row carries its **coherence expectation** — its road access class and the terms its kind implies — and V13's placement operator measures that expectation as site terms of the one `CoherenceCost`; an unlawful drop is corrected **by the kind's own rule** (the site moved to the nearest lawful placement, the road brought to the site, or the site held and the gap reported), within the kind's tolerance silently-but-named and beyond it offered; the road so joined is a `WorldRoute` every reader of the network already reads, at the world, the site frame and the encounter crop.

### 1.2 The words (one word, one meaning)

Reused unchanged: `WorldSite`, `attraction`, `routed` (a seat of W7's graph, V13:1700–1703), `SuitabilityTerm` and the seventeen plug-ins (V14:978–986), `CoherenceCost`, `lawfulMax`, `nearestLawful`, `PlacementAnomaly`, `Placement.hostLocked` (an origin edit refused, V13:733), `keep` / `takeOffer` / `cancel` (`W_PLACE_NO_LAWFUL`, V13:2873), `keep` / `worldWins` (`W_PLACE_ANOMALY`, V13:2874–2875), `worldWins` on a site = the re-materialization (V14:1414–1415), `WorldRoute` with its classes `highway`/`road`/`track`/`trail` and `layVersion` (V13:737–744), `W_HOST_CANON_BEHIND`, `W_ROUTE_NO_PATH`, the charter's `roads` row and its `WayClass` mapping (`trail → path`, V13:713), `drawWay` (the base's road-making event, base:11092–11094), the hinterland decoration row's `trail` link (base:8677–8679), `bend.site.*` (V14:2423–2424), the `world.placed` outcome, `timeDelta`, `patrolAt`.

New words, each with one meaning: **`roadAccess`** (the kind row's and the card's road-access class — the owner's phrase compounded, because the bare word `access` already names the `AccessPoint` (V14:736–745), the dossier's access word `site.access` (base:12940) and the water route class `WaterClassReport.access` (V14:808)); its six values **`on`** (the site stands on a route: a route vertex within `onReachM` of its origin), **`onCrossing`** (the site stands on a route at its crossing of a reach — `WorldRoute.crossings`, V13:742 — within `onReachM`), **`served`** (a route ends at or passes the site: W7 seats it standalone; the detour host-bound), **`reached`** (a spur of class `trail` to the nearest routed cell), **`none`** (nothing expected, nothing laid), **`shunned`** (a route within `shunReachM` is incoherent); **`expect`** (the kind's expectation rows: terms and a minimum); **`correction`** (`move` / `road` / `hold`); **`toleranceM`**; the cost terms **`siteAccess`**, **`siteExpect`**; the anomaly kinds **`siteOffRoute`**, **`siteOnRoute`**, **`siteExpectation`**; a route **`spur`** (origin `'spur'`: a `WorldRoute` of class `trail` from a site's road station to the nearest routed cell); the **detour** (W7 (12): a host route re-laid through a `served` site's road station as a dated lay version); the site's **road station** (the point of the site frame's outer ring where its approach meets the world: the charter road's station, V13:713 `atCm`; every reach of §1.3 is measured from the placement's **origin**, the road station derived from the charter afterwards); the **approach way** (G3 (e): the way inside the site frame from its road station to its entrance); the catalogue key **`bend.site.placement`**; the report code **`W_ROUTE_DETOUR`**; the budget rows **`WORLD-16`**, **`WORLD-17`**. The site form of P1 is **"P1 (site form)"**, V13 §3.11.1a, the function `siteCoherence(world, site, origin)`; `nearestLawful` is overloaded on the subject, never renamed.

### 1.4 The coherence expectation as cost terms (V13 §3.11.1a — P1's site form)

### 1.6 Road integration — what lawfulness requires, and what is laid, at every scale (item 135)

**World (V13 §3.8).** (i) `on`, `onCrossing` and `served` kinds are W7 seats as today (`routed`; V13:1700–1703): on a standalone world a leg is laid to them by W7 (4)–(5) (a site–town edge `road`, site–site `track`, by the rank law unchanged); on a host-bound world no leg (W7 (8), (11)): an `on` or `onCrossing` site stands on a route (its origin within `onReachM` of a vertex, or of a reach crossing), a `served` site is a position along a route by the detour (§1.7). (ii) **Spurs** (W7 (12), new): after the passes, every site placement whose card's `roadAccess` is `reached` and that lies within `spurMaxM` of a routed cell gets a **spur** — a `WorldRoute` of class `trail`, origin `'spur'`, `a` the site's placement, `b` the route it joins with `join: { route; station }` at the joined vertex — by one Dijkstra to the route set from the site's road station (W7 (5)'s trail mechanism, V13:1736–1737; ties by (cost × 1 000, cell index)), on a standalone **and** on a host-bound world: a spur is map-tier geometry with no host key, outside the canon, `routeFacts`, `patrolOffer` and every host message (the host's graph has settlement nodes only, read-research HS20; J-V13.79 keeps topology the host's — a leaf trail to a hermitage is no pair the host could own), never a leg (W7 (11)) and never a W7 seat. A `none` or `shunned` kind gets no spur. (iii) The route layer, the charter's `roads`, `roadsAt`, the view's `routes` layer and `worldFactsAt.route` read spurs as any route (a spur's class `trail` maps to the charter's `path`, V13:713).

**The site frame (V14 §3.4).** The charter of a site frame already carries `roads` for every route crossing its ring (P4 (6), V13:2143–2147) — a leg, a detoured host route or a spur among them; the site's **road station** is that row's `atCm`. New **G3 (e), the approach way**: for every charter road row the site document's creation prefix gains one `drawWay` (§7.1's from-points form, base:11092–11094: "a road joins its points, pair by pair, by S4's router with its class") from the road station on the frame's outer ring to the nearest entrance unit's point (G5 (e)'s `GateSite`), class the charter road's `WayClass` for `on`, `onCrossing` and `served` (`through`/`primary`/`track`), `path` for `reached` — written beside the terrain pre-edit (G3 (b)) in the prefix, so it replays byte-identically with the document (V14:1427's rule) and is laid by G5 (f) after S12a (the hinterland unit's tracks then meet it). On adoption it enters the settlement as a drawn constraint with "its ways" (V14:1903–1910 already names them; T-V14-7 asserts them). An `inFrame` site (no frame of its own) is joined to the nearest way corridor by the hinterland unit's L3 link — §4.9's decoration row's `trail` form (base:8677–8679), class `track` for `on`/`onCrossing`/`served`, `path` for `reached`, none otherwise (BCR-V14-26) — never a new S4 destination at the drop (a destination added re-derives S3's gates, base:11703; bar 6). After adoption of a free site the gate is S4's `worldSite` destination as today (BCR-V14-13, unchanged).

**The encounter crop (V14 §3.10).** E1 (4) (iii) lays "the charter's `roads` as way bands" — a spur or a leg crossing the 100 m crop frame is a band of its class by construction; a crop over the site document reads the approach way as any way of its ground part; E4 (b)'s `patrol` item appears only on a segment with a cadence (a spur has none, §1.8). No change: the chain holds because the spur and the approach are ordinary routes and ways.

### 1.7 The detour on a host-bound world (W7 (12))

W7 (11) and T-V13-43 already say a site dropped on a host route "changes a route's geometry and effort, never the number of legs" and "moves its effort by the detour only" (V13:1769–1771, 3589); the mechanism is stated here once. For a `served` site on a host-bound world within `toleranceM` of a host route: the route's polyline is re-laid **inside a window** — between the two polyline vertices nearest the site on either side, the window's half-length the kind's `toleranceM` — under the host's cost law with the site's road station as a required via, one A\* per window (W7 (4)'s run, confined to the window's ellipse), simplified at `routeToleranceM`; every vertex outside the window byte-identical; the leg count unchanged; the result **a dated lay version under the same host key** (W7 (10)'s *this happened* form, V13:1763–1768: a live overlay the host may adopt, `W_HOST_CANON_BEHIND` until its re-canonize), reported `W_ROUTE_DETOUR` with the route, the site and the effort delta, and carried in `timeDelta` (T-V13-43). An `on` or `onCrossing` site needs no detour (it stands on the route). A site's removal or `unplaceFrame` restores the former lay as a new lay version (never silently: W7 (10)). A hidden host road is no public road and is never detoured.

### 1.8 Patrols, the travel law, the lenses

Patrols are per (route segment, power) (V13:1963–1979): a `served`/`on` site on a host route lies on a patrolled segment, a fort or watchpost is a patrol source along it (V13:1965–1966) and `patrolOffer` names "the nearest garrisoned seat or site" (V13:2348–2349) — unchanged. A **spur carries no cadence** (`everyWeeks: null`; its class `trail` has no `gradeShift` row, V13:1110): nothing patrols a path to a hermitage. The travel law reads a spur's `stations` as any route's (effort-metres under the time terms, V13:741), so `derivedTime` can label the walk to the site "on foot" (V13:3000); no time is sent to the host. The lenses draw nothing new: the patrols lens reads cadences (none on a spur), the trade lens's "likely road" is `pathGeometry` over host edges (V13:3309), the sway lens's held points are unchanged; a spur is drawn by the `routes` layer in the trail style (V13:3000).

### 1.9 Anomalies and the DM's choices (V13 §3.11.5 (4), §3.14.2)

`PlacementAnomaly.kind` gains three kinds, each a reported reading, never an annotation (the `roadToNowhere` precedent, V13:788): **`siteOffRoute`** — an `on`, `onCrossing`, `served` or `reached` site with no route within its reach (a spur W7 could not lay, `W_ROUTE_NO_PATH`; a host route demoted to hidden or retracted; a detour beyond tolerance kept by the DM); **`siteOnRoute`** — a `shunned` site with a route within `shunReachM` (a host `chartered` route later laid through a hideout); **`siteExpectation`** — an `expect` row below its minimum (`detail` the terms and the value: a mine whose ore the cascade's re-thinning removed, a bridge fort whose reach a `rerouteReach` moved). They are listed by P5 at the placement edit and **re-read by the cascade's step 6** (V13:2651: "one level up") for every site placement whose terms the dirty set reaches, and by §3.14.3's row 13 (a route edit) — a later change never moves a site (politics and later edits never re-open a placement: J-V13.76's spirit; item 137 (c)). The DM's choices per anomaly are V13's two (V13:2279–2284): `keep` (default) or `worldWins { placement, ids }` — on a site the re-materialization under the new charter (V14:1414–1415); a `takeOffer` is reachable by the DM's own `setPlacement` whose dry run offers again. On a host-placed site `keep` is the one action.
