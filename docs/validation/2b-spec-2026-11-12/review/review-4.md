## The questions asked

**1. The ladder.** By construction, only for an op whose arena equals the base's and within the work limit (U-3). The
undo between rungs: every refusal inside the loop goes through `rollback`, which restores the arena (`undoTo`: arena half
and index half, `markArena` with counters, `handleCount` and `version`), the journals, the chain journal, the log and the
warnings. It does not restore work or `failed`. CLOSINGS and CENSUS are rewritten by rung 2. Nothing a rung-1 insertion
creates survives the undo: vertices, hot pixels and handles are all arena state. The arena after a rolled-back rung 1 is
bit-identical by M0-a-06's generic undo oracle. S21 line 4 (i) pins it only on the prototype's list, and pins rung 2
against the wrong comparand (U-2). The measurement showed "commits", not byte-equality.

**2. The price.** U-15: harmless at M0 to what S8 hands on. The measure is right with its tolerance. The pins need U-7.

**3. Determinism and order.** U-16.

branch and everything only it reaches are absent from production. "Byte-equal served reads and committed arenas, switch
off, shadow off and on" is checkable by line 7 (1) – (3). Add U-5's single-rung counter, and note that kerbCensus(p) may
differ shadow on and off if the observer ever runs rung 2; it need not.
