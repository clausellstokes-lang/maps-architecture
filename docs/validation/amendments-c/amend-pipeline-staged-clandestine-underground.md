  allocation's (b) reads the `underground` mode and names the fallback), PART III (a village's concealed institution takes
  a front).
* **Mechanism, in plain words.** A clandestine institution gets a concealment mode — from the dossier, an assignment or its
  class's weights — and the placement solver hides it accordingly: behind a working front business (placed where the front
  is plausible, near what the hidden function needs, preferring alleys and courts), on a hidden back-court plot, or
  underground. If the chosen mode has no ground to stand on, a fixed fallback chain is walked and reported; it is never
  placed openly and never dropped. The building is the front's in every public respect; the hidden function adds only
  flagged back rooms, a flagged service or yard door on an alley or passage, and a higher chance of a cellar and of a way
  down. Changing the front keeps the hidden function; moving the hidden function leaves an ordinary business. Everything is
  an `extension`, so the parity presets conceal nothing.
* **Rulings:** J-A63.41 (resolution and the fallback chain), J-A63.42 (one item, two uses; S11 builds the front),
  J-A63.43 (mutation), J-A63.44 (emulation; the village front). Decisions met: J-A17.21 (a second `Use` — no new

  by the rendering part for concealed parts — a cross-part note), J-A14.43 (navigability for the underground mode).
