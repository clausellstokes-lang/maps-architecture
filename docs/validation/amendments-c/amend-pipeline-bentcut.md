## 2. The mechanism, item by item, in plain language

* **33 — citations.** The bent cut is now described as what it is: the rule set of Neighbourhood 1.2.2's plain bisector,
  read in its code and re-expressed in metres. The profile's measured numbers stay the targets we test against (§13.5).
  Where MFCG 12710731 and VG 1.6.9 behave differently (they try a straight cut first) the text says so at step 5. The
  hillside rule that turns cuts toward the contours is ours alone — MFCG's model has no terrain — so it is marked as our
  extension and switched off in the parity presets.
* **34 — when to stop cutting.** A face becomes a block when it is thinner than a threshold drawn fresh for that face,
  between two back-to-back rows of the district's shallowest plots and two rows of its deepest (never below the stage
  regime's floor). The draw leans heavily toward the shallow end, so most blocks are compact and a few are deep — the
  size spread the references show, measured on depth rather than area because a plot row needs depth.
* **35 — where to cut.** The cut sits halfway between the face's middle-ish bell draw and the point under its centre of
  mass, so a lopsided face is split toward its bulk and both halves get sensible plot depths; nothing is clamped or
  redrawn. Holed faces use a plain bell kept away from the ends.
* **36 — which side to start from.** No coin: the cut starts from the side that runs along the face's long direction.
* **37 — the bend.** The cut goes in from the start side, the corner is placed first (a bell draw along that first leg,
  kept away from both ends), and only then the far side is chosen as the side the corner can reach square and
  unobstructed. By default a shallow bend is straightened (Neighbourhood's look); the MFCG preset tries a straight cut
  first with MFCG's own flatten height, so each preset reproduces its own reference's street signature.
* **38 — rounding the corner.** Sharp turns and short legs keep a crisp corner; gentle turns between long legs are
  rounded by an arc that spans the whole shorter leg. The measured 53 % rounding share falls out of this rule.
* **39 — retries.** When a cut fails its checks, the first retry now tries the same face in a frame turned by 36° (up
  to three frames), a genuinely different cut; the rest of our bounded ladder is unchanged and nothing ever restarts the
  city.
* **40 — lane width.** Longer lanes are wider, saturating at the top of the class's metric band, with one shape
  exponent per district; the old per-free-end term (a drawing artefact) is gone.
