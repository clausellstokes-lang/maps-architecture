**50 — the main door is the owner's rule.** The parity presets still give every building one street door and no side
or back door, but as the owner's item-29 rule, not as a copy of MFCG or FTG: neither models a door (the FTG client's
point on a building's road-facing edge is where it draws the night light; MFCG 12710731's model carries no door record —
an inference from reading it in full). The "3 ± 1 m" offset went with its FTG source: the main door sits on the side of
the footprint facing the street wherever the site plan put the front line, its place along that side drawn.

**59 — stage 3's path shapes.** When the Fantasy-Manor-class building generator arrives, its path of wings comes from
five layout families (rings, stars, arcs, rectangles, grid snakes) held as a data table, `PathShapeRow`. Ours adds three
rules that model lacks: wings are in the collision set, every building's main door faces its street, and the scale is
in metres.

**60 — the plan rung's baselines.** Dwellings' basement law (n ÷ (n + 1)) and its random loops are recorded as
descriptions of the reference, never as our rules; our basements are decided by class, band, size and terrain, and our
loops by rule.

**79 — powers as standings.** The base never builds a faction object for each dossier power; it reads each power's
standing from the circumstance slice (§7.7). An institution may list the powers it answers to (`Institution.powers`,
weights summing to one): absent, its class's rows apply; present, the dossier's override, which moves only at an epoch
past the dials' hysteresis and is shared by relative standing. `Faction.kind` gains `military` and `arcane`; an arcane
power exists standalone only under the magic circumstance; a hidden power keeps its kind and sets `secret`.

**88 — the dossier's staged records.** Condition overlays, deposit sites, relations between powers, a pre-creation
history (on the recipe) and dossier-named people are declared now, empty, so the SettlementForge stage only fills them.
A dossier-named person is pinned and never re-rolled away.

**20 (h) — who owns a value.** Every attribute is held by one of three tiers — `dossier`, `declared` (an author's
declaration, item 101) or `map` (derived by the map's own rules; the value names the rendering seat's reader and exports
already use) — and `factTier(entity, path)` works it out at read from what the document already
stores; nothing new is stored, so it cannot disagree with its sources. The registry lists, per kind, the only paths a
dossier may own.

**63–66 — concealment and the underground's records.** A clandestine institution's hidden function is a second `Use` in
the front's building (listed in `Building.concealed`), with its concealed rooms and door as flags; the underground
layer's other systems (catacombs, caverns, quarries, vaults, a buried town, dug passages) are records in arena parts of
their own, joined by typed links; a basement's way down is an access record and a fixed anchor of the plan; an access
edit that needs a basement asks for one and takes the richest lawful form — full, half or walk-out, then a crawl space
under a raised floor — or is refused with the reason.

**67–68 — time and light.** A physical change is a `Works` record with a start week and a duration; the stage and the
heights at any week are derived, never stored. A point light is a `LightSource` record on its host (with its flame's or orb's size, which sets the
shadow's penumbra); windows and room lights stay derived; the sun, night and lantern controls are per-viewer view keys.

**70, 72 — informal ground and a neighbourhood's character.** Plots carry a derived `tenure`; a cluster plot's structures
each open on a footpath. A district's character is a derived five-part profile (`CharacterVector`, one type); its
identity, pressure and former names are records; the recipe's per-district input is renamed `districtIntent`.
