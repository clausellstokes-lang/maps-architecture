## Words used below (plain glosses)

- **The host** / **the simulation**: SettlementForge, the program that simulates the campaign week by week. **The map**: this design (Maps), which draws the world.
- **Seat**: a settlement the host treats as a political seat (a town or city with its own standing).
- **Seat territory (W8a)**: the host's own meaning of a settlement's land: every reachable land cell goes to the settlement it is cheapest to reach. Total, no wild land, no politics. Frozen into **the canon**, the fixed record of the world that the host's travel distances are built from.
- **The sway (W8b)**: the map's picture of who politically holds which land: how far each settlement's reach runs, where two hostile powers meet in contested land, which land no one holds. Drawn and revised, never the host's truth.
- **Allegiance**: a top power together with its vassals. **Vassal chain**: the line of vassals under a top power.
- **March / debatable land**: land where two hostile powers' reaches meet and neither holds it.
- **Held by force**: debatable land given to one side by military strength (the tie-breaker).
- **Fighting**: an overlay mark on a stretch of march where a fight is going on or just happened; it never moves a border. (The design deliberately never writes "embattled" for it.)
- **Patrol cadence**: how often a power's patrol passes along a stretch of road: weekly, every four weeks, every thirteen weeks, or never.
- **Dwell**: the time a changed fact must stand before the border reacts (the guard against flicker; "hysteresis").
- **Revision**: one dated re-derivation of the sway, at most one per week.
- **Polity facts window**: the bundle of political facts the host sends after each advance (`PolityFacts`).
- **Lens**: a map layer that draws one of the host's simulated systems, with a legend, an audience filter and a time scrub. **Regime**: which mode a lens runs in, decided by what the host actually sends.
- **Band**: a value cut into a few steps (for example strength "in fifths", 0–4).
- **H-row**: a numbered item of the host-change list, V13 §8.5.
- **J-row**: a numbered row of the owner's and session's rulings in docs/PROGRESS.md.

## Notes (things that came to mind while reading)

1. **What the default preset actually gives today.** On the most common birth preset (RRb) the sway can honestly show "reach by tier, size and wealth, split by hostility, resisted by culture": tier, size, a frozen prosperity rung, drift relations with hostile pairs and raids, accepted vassal links and culture (no faith term); no military, no occupation, no verdicts. The legend should say military enforcement is not yet a host fact. lens-implications.md §3.3 (line 373).
2. **Patrols have no host consumer today.** The only nearby host slot is embattlement's capped security relief (weight 0.4, capped 0.25), and route danger is zero in a realm that never held a live war front; plague gives patrols no job. So patrols are, for now, a map presentation read by the encounter tier; H25's "host meaning" ask is what would ever let them matter to the simulation. lens-implications.md §3.2 patrol inputs row (line 371), §2.2 (line 193).
3. **The patrol garrison input is a stand-in until force bands arrive.** The "derived" patrol regime states it; T-V13-42 pins the shift rule (band 3 or more one rung denser, band 1 or less one rung sparser, no shift with no band sent). V13-world.md §5.9 (line 3267); T-V13-42 (line 3582).
4. **Item 714 is older than the current V13 text.** Item 714 (AMENDMENTS-2026-10-17m.md lines 968–1171) has the adversarial push as "the stronger holds" and patrol shifts by hostility and grade only; current V13 §3.9 adds J384's deference to the host's strength band (line 1847), the force-band patrol shift `forceShift` (line 1962), the satellite details (line 1924) and the civil-regime hold bands (J386, line 1909). Where they differ, the V13 volume text is the one in force; a host developer should read V13, not the amendment.
5. **What the encounter maps read from this family.** Inside the map, V14's encounters read the patrol presence by address and week (`patrolAt`, V14's E4), the sway's borders (V14's `territoryBorder` term) and the polity facts at a week through `worldFactsAt` (holder, status, sides, occupation, patrols present, fighting). V13-world.md §1b.1 (line 440), §1b.2 (line 503), §2.2 WorldFacts polity (line 1166). These are map-internal and send the host nothing.
6. **Things the patrols lens deliberately does not draw yet.** The character of a route's intermediary settlements (the host judges a caravan route at its worst intermediary and sends no per-settlement verdict) and a road's escort quality (a property of a named traveller, waiting on H23's journey rows). V13-world.md §5.9 patrols (line 3279).
