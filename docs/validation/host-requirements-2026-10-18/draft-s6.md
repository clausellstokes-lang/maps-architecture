## Encounter maps and world sites: the FP-related items

FP is the host's family of settlement-simulation programs: the parts of SettlementForge that move trade, people, armies, news and danger across the realm week by week (`research/sf/fp-physical-integration.md` §1, line 13). Encounter maps (a framed, gridded crop of the map where a fight or a scene plays out) and world sites (a ruin, fort, camp or crypt standing outside a settlement) take four things from FP: where things are, how far they are, how dangerous the road is, and what the players have been told (`docs/volumes/V14-sites.md` §1b.5, lines 450–461; §2.3, line 765).

The research ledger (the row-by-row list of every physical fact the simulation reads) counts 80 rows: 30 supplied by the map, 17 partly, 7 missing, 26 where the host owes the map nothing (`research/sf/fp-physical-integration.md` §1, assembler's note, line 25). A dated note of 2026-10-17 moves row A1 (seat territory) from supplied to partly, so the working count is 29, 18, 7 and 26 (`fp-physical-integration.md` §8, lines 458–468). The ledger's own summary paragraph still says 62 rows; the assembler's note explains the drafts did not match and keeps both for review (line 25).

As of 2026-10-18 the owner has answered yes to V13 §11 Q4, so the world canon (the map's one fixed record of the world) is outside door 1, and H5 (the host's own build of its territory record over the map's) is lit. Several rows below lean on H5 and are marked where they do. The headings sort each fact by what the host has today, not by the ledger's verdict, so a "missing" ledger row the host already holds appears under "supplies"; the sorting rule is stated at the end of this section.

### FP laws the map obeys

These are the host's standing rules that bind every encounter map and world site. "Always" means the rule holds in every build stage.

1. **The host's geography is frozen; the campaign only adds side records.** FP reads the world's geography as a record taken once and never changes it. Everything the campaign adds (roads lived, foundings, deaths, danger, occupation, columns, caravans) is kept in side records keyed by the host's settlement ids. A site's status change or an adoption must reach the host as a dated event or a receipted re-capture, never as a silent rewrite. Always.
