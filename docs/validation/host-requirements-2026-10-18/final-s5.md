## Patrols within a territory: what the map needs from the simulation

A territory on the map is a projection of the simulation's political facts over the terrain, never a fact of its own. Patrols derive from military enforcement, neighbour hostility and route class, offered back as coverage per road stretch the host uses only by its choice (docs/PROGRESS.md J377 (line 436), J378 (line 437); docs/volumes/V13-world.md §3.9 (line 1781)).

**Words used here.** *The host* or *the simulation* is SettlementForge, the program that runs the campaign week by week; *the map* is this module. A *seat* is a settlement the host counts as a political seat. The *seat territory* is the host's own kind of territory: every land cell goes to the seat it is cheapest to reach, with no politics (V13 §3.9 (line 1781)).

The *sway* is the map's picture of who politically holds which land, derived and redrawn, never the host's truth (V13 §3.9 (line 1781)). *Debatable land* or a *march* is land where two hostile reaches meet and neither holds it (V13 §3.9 (8) (line 1846)). A *cadence* is how often a power's patrol passes a road stretch: weekly, every four weeks, every thirteen weeks, or never (V13 §3.9 (14) (line 1963)).

A *window* is the bundle of facts the host sends after each advance; the *polity facts window* is its political bundle, `PolityFacts` (V13 §2.2 (line 809)). An *H-row* is a numbered host change in V13 §8.5 (line 3702; its table from line 3726). *Lit* means a host feature is switched on and sending; *dark* means it is not. *RRb* is the host's most common birth preset (research/sf/lens-implications.md §3.3 (line 373)).

### The binding rules, in plain words

- **Two partitions, never one.** The host's seat territory and the map's sway stay separate; the sway never becomes the host's territory, never enters the canon, and the host's crossings and distances never come from it (V13 §3.9 (line 1781); §5.9 sway refusals (line 3270); J-V13.65 (line 3829)).
- **Borders move only on dated facts, after a wait.** Each changed fact waits its dwell (prosperity 13 weeks; strength, force and culture bands their own); a fact that rose and fell inside one window never reaches the border (V13 §3.9 (13) (line 1938); T-V13-41 (c) (line 3587); J377 (line 436)).
- **The map never decides a war.** Between hostile neighbours the border's push defers to the host's own strength order; only a host verdict moves a march's hold band; a raid with no outcome word only marks "fighting" (J384 (3), (5) (line 443); V13 §3.9 (8) (line 1846), (11) (line 1904)).
- **Military strength is the tie-breaker only.** It enters only land left debatable, never extends reach, never moves a decided line, never touches a seat's own cell; the per-tier ceiling holds (J378 (line 437); V13 §3.9 (10) (line 1891)).
- **Patrols by address and week, never a roll.** Cadence per road stretch and power is revised only with the sway; presence in a week is fixed by a hash of the stretch and the power, so every run agrees (V13 §3.9 (14) (line 1963); §1b.2 `patrolAt` (line 509); T-V13-42 (line 3588)).
- **Cadence never reaches the host as a time.** The offer sends only the stretch covered and its basis; the host prices any round trip with its own distance reader, and reads the offer only behind its own switch (V13 §3.9 (14) (line 1963); §3.11.6 (line 2347); §5.9 patrols refusals (line 3284)).
- **No invented effects.** A patrol is never drawn lowering danger or holding a quarantine line; a march is never a road hazard; no border or patrol slows a plague (V13 §5.8 (4) (line 3154); §5.9 (line 3284); lens-implications.md §2.2 (line 193)).
- **Reach is bounded but exponential; unclaimed land stays wild.** Reach grows with tier, size and relative prosperity, fades with travel time, and is capped per tier (J377 T2 (line 436); V13 §3.9 (7) (line 1831)).
- **Prosperity counted once, relative to the realm's median.** The rung enters as its distance from the realm's held median, clamped to plus or minus 3, and is left out of the force band (J384 (1), (4) (line 443); V13 §3.9 (7) (line 1831)).
- **Culture and water as the host has them.** Culture resists reach only by the host's plain-form culture distance; water carries reach only along the host's lit lanes, to at most a coastal foothold (J382 (line 441); J384 (2) (line 443); V13 §3.9 (6) (line 1814)).
- **Vassal chains in two passes; satellites never counted twice.** A vassal joined to its overlord's land is continuous and gets a capped bonus, else it is an exclave; a satellite is a claim point, never a chain member, never in its parent's size (V13 §3.9 (9) (line 1866), (12) (line 1921); J384 (11) (line 443)).
- **Occupation is an overlay, never ownership.** It hatches land at the host's rung and changes no holder until the host's vassalized rung; a razing transfers nothing (V13 §3.9 (12) (line 1921); docs/AMENDMENTS-2026-10-17m.md item 714 evidence (line 1171)).
- **The host reads the sway only between advances, and its live reads win.** Any host read is fetched at the start of an advance, fixed for it, at most one advance stale; the DM's sway locks never feed what is offered (V13 §3.9 (13) (line 1938); §3.11.6 (line 2369); §1b.3 `setSwayLock` (line 533)).
- **Honesty, banner and audience.** Each lens states its regime from what the host sends; an off gate shows as "still", never hidden or extrapolated; every lens opens DM-only, and a covert power's patrols fail closed (V13 §5.8 (2), (3), (7) (lines 3133, 3140, 3179); §5.9 (lines 3247, 3273); J384 (13) (line 443)).
- **No budget, cost vector or power key is asked of the host.** The sway's settings are the map's own; the host sends only its facts (V13 §3.9 (5) (line 1808); AMENDMENTS-2026-10-17m.md item 714 (line 968)).
- **One word, one meaning.** "Fighting", never "embattled"; a third realm in a cell is "outranked", never "claimant" (the host's word for an heir); "gate" is never written bare (V13 §3.9 (8) (line 1846); naming rule (line 2445); §5.8 (6) (line 3170)).
- **Patrols are per road stretch; water has none.** The cadence is set per road stretch and power, its inputs are garrisons, hostility and the road grade, and the sway crosses water only along the host's lit lanes to a coastal lodgement (V13 §3.9 (14) (line 1963), (6) (line 1814)); no sea patrol exists in the design, and the only water "watch" is the host's own naval blockade (W11, host-owned; research/sf/fp-physical-integration.md §2.4 (line 90)). The owner's directive of 2026-08-01 that water routes form and fade organically exactly as land routes do, one calibration for all movers (fp-physical-integration.md §6 row I20 (line 324)), means H12's route changes and grades per window must include the lived state of water lanes as well as roads; a host developer should not expect the patrol layer to cover them.
