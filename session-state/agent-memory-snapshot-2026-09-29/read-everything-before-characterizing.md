On 2026-09-21 I told the owner the Patreon posts "describe features, not algorithms" after reading ~3 of 88; the owner pushed back ("don't the posts also describe the techniques and mathematical principles per iteration?") and was right — several posts and their linked devlogs explain method.

**Why:** the owner asked for an exhaustive read precisely because the value is in per-iteration technique detail; a sampled verdict undermines trust in the confidence figures that depend on it.
**How to apply:** when asked for interim thoughts before a full read, report only what was read and label the sample size; hold corpus-level verdicts until the readers and the completeness critic have finished. See [[maps-project-state]].
